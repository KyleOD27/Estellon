## Essential Materials (Required)

### Core Rules
- **Custom Character Tableau System** - See **[Custom Character Sheet](custom-character-sheet.md)**
  - Character tableau boards (11x17 paper)
  - Role, Race, Weapon, Armor, Item, and Ability cards
  - Simplified 2d6 resolution system
- **Dice needed:** 2d6 per player (simplified from full D&D)
- **Alternative:** Standard D&D 5e character sheets if preferred

### Campaign-Specific Trackers

#### 1. Faction Progress Tracker
Create or print a sheet with three tracks (0-10 scale):
- **Revolutionary Zeal** (Kael's faction)
- **City Control** (Captain Brennis's faction)
- **Coalition Strength** (Ser Elara's faction)

*Suggestion: Use tokens, markers, or a sliding scale to track*

#### 2. Individual Faction Reputation Trackers
Each player needs a sheet tracking their reputation (1-20) with:
- Revolutionary Zeal
- City Control
- Coalition Strength
- Independent Influence

*Template:*
```
Player Name: _________
Revolutionary Zeal:    [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
City Control:         [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
Coalition Strength:   [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
Independent Influence:[1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
```

## Card Components

### Character Tableau System Cards

Full details and templates available in **[Custom Character Sheet](custom-character-sheet.md)**

#### Role Cards (7 total)
Print one of each on cardstock (3.5" x 2.5"):
- **Merchant** - Market Network ability, +50 gold, +2 Persuasion
- **Scholar** - Research Network ability, +2 Investigation
- **Sellsword** - Mercenary's Reputation ability, +1 weapon/armor
- **Pilgrim** - Divine Guidance ability, +1 Wis saves
- **Noble** - Political Influence ability, +100 gold
- **Artisan** - Master Craftsman ability, +50 gold, tools
- **Refugee** - Street Knowledge ability, +2 Survival/Stealth

#### Race Cards (5-6 recommended)
- **Human** - Versatile, +1 all skills, extra ability slot
- **Orc** - Strong, +2 combat, intimidating
- **Elf** - Perceptive, +2 knowledge, diplomatic
- **Dwarf** - Hardy, +2 defense, craft expertise
- **Halfling** - Lucky, +2 stealth, charming
- *Add others as needed*

#### Weapon Cards (10-12 recommended)
Basic weapons:
- **Sword** - Damage 2, Close range, versatile
- **Crossbow** - Damage 2, Far range, ignores light armor
- **Dagger** - Damage 1, Close range, concealable
- **Staff** - Damage 1, Close range, defensive
- **Bow** - Damage 2, Far range, quick
- *Create more as needed*

#### Armor Cards (8-10 recommended)
- **No Armor** - Defense +0, no restrictions
- **Leather** - Defense +1, Light, no stealth penalty
- **Chain Mail** - Defense +2, Medium, -1 stealth
- **Plate Mail** - Defense +3, Heavy, -2 stealth, -1 speed
- **Shield** - Defense +1 (stacks), requires free hand
- *Add quality variants (Fine, Masterwork)*

#### Item Cards (20-30 recommended)
Consumables:
- **Healing Potion** (3-5) - Restore 2 health
- **Smoke Bomb** (2-3) - Create concealment
- **Alchemist's Fire** (1-2) - Damage over time

Tools:
- **Merchant's Ledger** - +2 to trade
- **Thieves' Tools** - Enable lockpicking
- **Scholar's Kit** - +2 to research
- **Rope & Grapple** - Climbing aid
- **Disguise Kit** - Change appearance

#### Ability Cards (15-20 for campaign)
Starting abilities from roles (7)
Earnable abilities:
- **Street Fighter** - +2 unarmed damage
- **Faction Champion** - Bonus from high reputation
- **Veteran's Insight** - Reroll failed checks 1/session
- *Create more as rewards*

### Player Handouts

#### 1. Character Tableau Board
Print on 11x17 or A3 paper, one per player:
- Card placement zones for Role, Race, Weapon, Armor
- 3 Item slots
- 3 Ability slots
- Integrated faction reputation tracker (1-20 scale)
- Character name and notes section

#### 2. Dialogue Bingo Cards
Create cards with a 4x4 or 5x5 grid containing phrases from different alignments:

**Lawful Good Examples:**
- "Justice must be served, but with mercy"
- "The law exists to protect the innocent"
- "We must do what is right, not what is easy"

**Chaotic Good Examples:**
- "Sometimes you have to break unjust laws"
- "The people matter more than the system"
- "Freedom is worth fighting for"

**Lawful Neutral Examples:**
- "Order must be maintained at all costs"
- "The procedure exists for a reason"
- "Stability benefits everyone"

**Neutral/Pragmatic Examples:**
- "What's the practical solution here?"
- "Every choice has consequences"
- "Sometimes compromise is necessary"

#### 2. Character Role Reference Cards
Quick reference cards for each player showing:
- Role name and special ability
- Starting bonuses
- Social access areas
- Initial faction modifiers

#### 3. Map of Estellon

**Full City Map:** See **[City Map: Estellon](city-map.md)** for complete district details and locations

Basic district map showing:
- **Noble Quarter** (wealthy district) - Ducal Palace, noble estates
- **Cobbleward** (poor district) - Kael's territory, working class
- **Trade District** (markets and guilds) - Market Square, The Grumbling Ox
- **Temple District** (religious centers) - Cathedral, Ser Elara's Chapterhouse
- **Guard Barracks & Ironhold Prison** (military areas) - Captain Brennis's headquarters, city jail

*Note: Detailed tactical maps not required - this is a narrative-focused campaign*

## Minimal Setup

If pressed for time or resources, the **absolute minimum** needed:
1. **Three d10s or paper tracker** for main faction scores
2. **Index cards** to create improvised character cards
3. **2d6 per player** (or share dice)
4. **Paper tableau** - hand-drawn character zones
5. **Campaign notes** (this repository)

### Quick Card Alternative
Use index cards (3x5) to create cards on the fly:
- Write role name and bonus on one side
- Add special ability on the other
- Use different colored cards for different types

## Creating Your Own Assets

### DIY Faction Tracker
Draw three thermometer-style tracks on paper:
```
Revolutionary Zeal:  0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
City Control:       0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
Coalition Strength: 0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
```

### Simple Bingo Cards
Create a 3x3 grid with key phrases that match your party's style. Even a simplified version maintains the roleplay incentive.

### Digital Alternative
Use a shared Google Sheet or similar for all tracking - no printing required!

## Storage & Organization

### Physical Storage
- **Card storage box** or multiple deck boxes:
  - Separate by type (Roles, Races, Weapons, etc.)
  - Use dividers or rubber bands
- **Portfolio folder** for tableau boards
- **Dice bag** for 2d6 sets
- **Card sleeves** (optional but recommended for durability)
- **Dry erase markers** for reputation tracking

### Digital Organization
- **Campaign folder** with subfolders for:
  - Character sheets
  - Session notes
  - Audio files
  - Player resources
  - DM references

## Notes for Game Masters

- **Most materials are optional** - the campaign runs fine with just basic tracking
- **Focus on faction trackers** - these are the most important mechanical element
- **Dialogue Bingo is highly recommended** - it encourages roleplay
- **Maps are narrative, not tactical** - you don't need grid maps or miniatures
- **Audio enhances but isn't essential** - the campaign works without voice files
