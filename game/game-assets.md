# Game Assets & Materials List

> *"Everything you need to run The Weight of the Crown campaign"*

## Essential Materials (Required)

### Core Rules
- **D&D 5e Basic Rules** or Player's Handbook
- **Standard dice set** (d20, d12, d10, d8, d6, d4)
- **Character sheets** (standard D&D 5e)

### Campaign-Specific Trackers

#### 1. Faction Progress Tracker
Create or print a sheet with three tracks (0-10 scale):
- **Revolutionary Zeal** (Kael's faction)
- **City Control** (Captain Brennis's faction)
- **Coalition Strength** (Ser Elara's faction)

*Suggestion: Use tokens, markers, or a sliding scale to track*

#### 2. Individual Faction Reputation Trackers
Each player needs a sheet tracking their reputation (1-20) with:
- Revolutionary Zeal
- City Control
- Coalition Strength
- Independent Influence

*Template:*
```
Player Name: _________
Revolutionary Zeal:    [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
City Control:         [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
Coalition Strength:   [1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
Independent Influence:[1][2][3][4][5]...[20]
```

### Player Handouts

#### 1. Dialogue Bingo Cards
Create cards with a 4x4 or 5x5 grid containing phrases from different alignments:

**Lawful Good Examples:**
- "Justice must be served, but with mercy"
- "The law exists to protect the innocent"
- "We must do what is right, not what is easy"

**Chaotic Good Examples:**
- "Sometimes you have to break unjust laws"
- "The people matter more than the system"
- "Freedom is worth fighting for"

**Lawful Neutral Examples:**
- "Order must be maintained at all costs"
- "The procedure exists for a reason"
- "Stability benefits everyone"

**Neutral/Pragmatic Examples:**
- "What's the practical solution here?"
- "Every choice has consequences"
- "Sometimes compromise is necessary"

#### 2. Character Role Reference Cards
Quick reference cards for each player showing:
- Role name and special ability
- Starting bonuses
- Social access areas
- Initial faction modifiers

#### 3. Map of Estellon
Basic district map showing:
- **Noble Quarter** (wealthy district)
- **Cobbleward** (poor district)
- **Trade District** (markets and guilds)
- **Temple District** (religious centers)
- **Guard Barracks** (military areas)

*Note: Detailed tactical maps not required - this is a narrative-focused campaign*

## Creating Your Own Assets

### DIY Faction Tracker
Draw three thermometer-style tracks on paper:
```
Revolutionary Zeal:  0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
City Control:       0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
Coalition Strength: 0 [_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_|_] 10
```

### Simple Bingo Cards
Create a 3x3 grid with key phrases that match your party's style. Even a simplified version maintains the roleplay incentive.

### Digital Alternative
Use a shared Google Sheet or similar for all tracking - no printing required!

## Storage & Organization

### Physical Storage
- **Folder or binder** for player handouts
- **Box or bag** for tokens/markers
- **Protective sleeves** for frequently used trackers

### Digital Organization
- **Campaign folder** with subfolders for:
  - Character sheets
  - Session notes
  - Audio files
  - Player resources
  - DM references

## Notes for Game Masters

- **Most materials are optional** - the campaign runs fine with just basic tracking
- **Focus on faction trackers** - these are the most important mechanical element
- **Dialogue Bingo is highly recommended** - it encourages roleplay
- **Maps are narrative, not tactical** - you don't need grid maps or miniatures
- **Audio enhances but isn't essential** - the campaign works without voice files
