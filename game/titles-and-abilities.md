# Titles and Abilities System

> *"Your deeds shape your legend. Your legend shapes the city."*

---

## Overview

As PCs complete missions and make choices throughout the campaign, they earn **Titles** that reflect their reputation and abilities. These titles provide:
- **Mechanical bonuses** in specific mission types
- **Unlocked mission options** (new approaches or objectives)
- **Social advantages** (NPC reactions, dialogue options)
- **Unique abilities** (special actions in combat/social encounters)

---

## How Titles Work

### Earning Titles

Titles are earned through:
1. **Mission Achievement** - Complete missions with specific success criteria
2. **Faction Reputation** - Reach reputation thresholds with factions
3. **Story Choices** - Make significant narrative decisions
4. **Combat Excellence** - Achieve bonus objectives in encounters

### Title Tiers

Titles progress through three tiers:

**🥉 Bronze (Basic)** - Earned from first success, provides minor bonus
**🥈 Silver (Advanced)** - Earned from multiple successes, provides significant bonus
**🥇 Gold (Master)** - Earned from legendary achievements, provides major bonus + unique ability

### Using Titles

- **Active Title:** PCs can have ONE active title at a time (choose before each mission)
- **Known Titles:** PCs retain all earned titles and can switch between them
- **Title Stacking:** Some titles have prerequisites (must have Bronze to earn Silver)
- **NPC Recognition:** NPCs react based on your active title

## Combat Titles

### 🗡️ Breacher
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Complete any infiltration mission (K4 Ironhold, K5 Armory, E2 Rykard Evidence)
- Silver: Complete 3 infiltration missions with zero alarm triggers
- Gold: Complete K4 Ironhold Prison Break with Legendary Success (all prisoners, no alarm, no casualties)

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Stealth checks during infiltration missions, advantage on Thieves' Tools checks to disable locks
- **Silver:** Gain "Silent Takedown" - Once per mission, automatically succeed on surprise attack against single guard (they're unconscious, no alarm)
- **Gold:** Gain "Breach Master" ability - Team gains +5 to initiative during infiltration, can take 10 on Stealth checks

**Unlocks:**
- Alternative approach in B7 (Raid Safehouse): "Breacher's Entry" - Infiltrate through sewers silently, gain surprise round
- E4 (Legal Archive): Can bypass locked Restricted Section, gain 1 hour head start

---

### ⚔️ Duelist
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Win any 1v1 combat (Challenge Knight-Captain Aldric, duel Gorok)
- Silver: Win 3 honorable duels without using magic/items
- Gold: Defeat Gorok in honorable 1v1 duel (he dismisses bodyguards)

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to hit in 1v1 combat (no allies within 10 ft of you or enemy)
- **Silver:** Gain "Riposte" - When enemy misses you in melee, reaction to make attack of opportunity
- **Gold:** Gain "Champion's Challenge" ability - Action to challenge enemy to duel; DC 15 Charisma save or they must accept (dismiss minions, fight 1v1)

**Unlocks:**
- Knight-Captain Aldric automatically accepts duel (dismisses bodyguards)
- Gorok respects you, offers honorable 1v1
- B5 (Hunt Agitator): Can challenge Red Fox to rooftop duel instead of chase

---

### 🔥 Pyroclast
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Deal 50+ fire damage in single mission
- Silver: Use fire strategically to solve mission objective (burn evidence, create distraction, forge weapon)
- Gold: Master fire tactics - Use fire 5+ times across campaign without civilian casualties

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** Fire damage +2 per die (Fireball 8d6+16 instead of 8d6)
- **Silver:** Gain "Controlled Burn" - Fire spells you cast don't harm allies or unintended targets
- **Gold:** Gain "Inferno Master" ability - 1/day, maximize one fire damage spell (all dice = max value)

**Unlocks:**
- K2 (Printing Press): Can use fire to create defensive perimeter (no risk of burning press)
- K6 (Expose Duke): Can burn evidence publicly as dramatic statement
- Front 2 (Streets): Can use Ashka's Arsenal Master + your fire to devastating effect

---

### 🛡️ Guardian
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Protect an NPC through entire mission without them taking damage
- Silver: Protect 3 NPCs across multiple missions
- Gold: E5 (Protect Reformer) - Magistrate Helena takes zero damage, assassin captured

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 AC when standing adjacent to protected ally
- **Silver:** Gain "Bodyguard" reaction - When adjacent ally is hit, you can take the damage instead
- **Gold:** Gain "Aegis" ability - 1/day, grant protected ally resistance to all damage for 1 minute

**Unlocks:**
- E5 (Protect Reformer): Can assign PC as dedicated bodyguard (advantage on Perception to spot assassin)
- Front 1 (Walls): Can protect Brennis or Elara, they gain +5 HP and advantage on saves
- Civilians trust you immediately (no Persuasion checks needed for evacuation)

## Social Titles

### 🎭 Infiltrator
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Successfully deceive guards/officials in any mission
- Silver: Complete 3 social infiltration missions without being detected
- Gold: K6 (Expose Duke) - Infiltrate gala, gather all evidence, remain completely undetected

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Deception and Persuasion checks when impersonating officials
- **Silver:** Gain "False Identity" - Can create believable cover story; NPCs believe you unless they have specific reason to doubt (Insight DC 18)
- **Gold:** Gain "Master Spy" ability - 1/day, automatically succeed on one Deception check

**Unlocks:**
- K6 (Expose Duke): Can pose as noble, gain access to restricted areas
- E2 (Rykard Evidence): Can impersonate inspector, Rykard's guards cooperate
- E4 (Legal Archive): Can convince Archivist you're authorized researcher (no Persuasion check)

---

### 🗣️ Orator
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Successfully persuade 10+ NPCs in single mission (K3 Rally Dockworkers, K7 Recruitment Drive)
- Silver: Win 3 social encounters without combat
- Gold: K3 (Rally Dockworkers) - All 3 guild leaders sign pact, 200+ workers commit

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Persuasion checks when addressing groups (3+ NPCs)
- **Silver:** Gain "Inspiring Speech" - Bonus action, grant all allies within 30 ft +1d4 to next attack or save
- **Gold:** Gain "Master Orator" ability - 1/session, deliver speech that rallies entire district (mass Persuasion, affects 50+ NPCs)

**Unlocks:**
- K7 (Recruitment Drive): Automatically recruit 100+ fighters (skip rolls)
- E3 (Noble Assembly): Can address entire assembly at once (one check instead of individual)
- E6 (Peace Summit): Can mediate successfully (advantage on all Persuasion checks)

---

### 🕵️ Investigator
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Gather all evidence in any investigation mission
- Silver: Complete 3 investigation missions finding all clues
- Gold: E2 (Rykard Evidence) - Find ledger, 4+ witnesses, 3+ documented crimes, Rykard arrested

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Investigation and Insight checks when examining evidence
- **Silver:** Gain "Deduction" - Spend 1 minute examining scene, DM reveals one hidden clue
- **Gold:** Gain "Master Detective" ability - 1/mission, ask DM one yes/no question about mystery (DM must answer truthfully)

**Unlocks:**
- E2 (Rykard Evidence): Can find hidden ledger location automatically
- K8 (Counter-Intelligence): Can identify informant with single Investigation check (DC 13)
- Front 2 (Streets): Automatically discover Pellor's treachery from bandit lieutenant

---

### 💰 Negotiator
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Successfully negotiate non-violent resolution to any mission
- Silver: Achieve 3 bloodless mission completions through negotiation
- Gold: B4 (Suppress Strike) - End strike through negotiation with zero deaths

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Persuasion and Intimidation during negotiations
- **Silver:** Gain "Compromise" - Once per mission, both sides accept partial victory (no total failure)
- **Gold:** Gain "Peacemaker" ability - 1/session, end combat encounter peacefully (all sides agree to ceasefire, DC 15 Charisma check)

**Unlocks:**
- B4 (Suppress Strike): Can negotiate with Torrin (DC reduced to 11)
- E6 (Peace Summit): Automatically achieve temporary ceasefire
- Front 1 (Walls): Can parley with Knight-Captain Aldric mid-battle

## Faction Titles

### ⚡ Revolutionary Hero (Ashka's Faction)
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Complete 3 Ashka missions successfully
- Silver: Complete Ashka's entire quest chain (K1, K3, K5 - Ashka EMPOWERED)
- Gold: Front 2 (Streets) - Heroic Victory (all lieutenants dead, <10 civilian casualties, no fires)

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Persuasion with common folk, can call on 1d4 revolutionary sympathizers for aid
- **Silver:** Gain "People's Champion" - Cobbleward civilians provide shelter, resources, and intelligence freely
- **Gold:** Share Ashka's "Arsenal Master" ability - Can summon 1d6 armed citizens once per session

**Unlocks:**
- K2 (Printing Press): Revolutionary Veterans defend press automatically (8 NPCs aid you)
- K4 (Ironhold): 1d6 revolutionary sympathizers within prison aid escape
- Act III: Revolutionary Veterans follow you into palace assault

**NPC Reactions:**
- Cobbleward residents: Hero worship, offer homes/food
- Guards: Hostile, see you as dangerous radical
- Nobles: Fear and respect, see you as threat

---

### ⚖️ Champion of Reform (Elara's Faction)
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Complete 3 Elara missions successfully
- Silver: Complete Elara's entire quest chain (E1, E3, E4 - Elara EMPOWERED)
- Gold: E4 (Legal Archive) - Find Charter of Common Rights, authenticate it, preserve it for trial

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Persuasion with nobles and clergy, legal arguments carry weight
- **Silver:** Gain "Legal Authority" - Can invoke laws to compel cooperation (DC 14 Charisma check)
- **Gold:** Share Elara's "Righteous Authority" ability - Can command NPCs to stand down once per session

**Unlocks:**
- E3 (Noble Assembly): Noble houses trust you, DC reduced by 3 for all Persuasion
- E7 (Reform Guard): Guards recognize your legitimacy, 30 guards join reform instead of 20
- Epilogue: You can formally present charges against Duke Valerius

**NPC Reactions:**
- Nobles: Respect, invite you to assemblies
- Guards: Respectful, see you as legitimate authority
- Revolutionaries: Skeptical, see you as establishment

---

### 🛡️ Defender of Order (Brennis's Faction)
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Complete 3 Brennis missions successfully
- Silver: Complete Brennis's entire quest chain (B2, B3, B6 - Brennis EMPOWERED)
- Gold: Front 1 (Walls) - Decisive Victory (wall held, <10 deaths, 2+ siege towers destroyed, Aldric captured)

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Intimidation and commanding troops, guards obey orders
- **Silver:** Gain "Military Command" - Can command NPC guards in combat (they act on your initiative)
- **Gold:** Share Brennis's "Tactical Commander" ability - Coordinate elite units for combined tactics once per session

**Unlocks:**
- B6 (Elite Guard): You personally train elite units (they have +2 to hit, +1 AC)
- B7 (Raid Safehouse): Elite Guards follow your tactical commands precisely
- Front 1 (Walls): Can deploy reserves tactically (move 10 guards as bonus action)

**NPC Reactions:**
- Guards: Absolute loyalty, salute you
- Nobles: See you as reliable, trustworthy
- Common folk: Fear tinged with respect, see you as enforcer

## Specialist Titles

### 🏹 Marksman
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Kill 3 enemies in one mission using only ranged attacks
- Silver: Score critical hit from 100+ feet
- Gold: Front 1 (Walls) - Destroy all 3 trebuchets using ballista (you personally)

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +1 to ranged attack rolls, ignore half-cover
- **Silver:** Increase range increment by 50% (shortbow 120/600 instead of 80/320)
- **Gold:** Gain "Deadeye" ability - 1/day, automatically hit with ranged attack (roll damage with advantage)

**Unlocks:**
- Front 1 (Walls): Can man ballista with expert precision (+5 to hit)
- B5 (Hunt Agitator): Can shoot Red Fox mid-leap (DC 16 instead of 18)

---

### 🧪 Alchemist
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Use alchemical items (acid, alchemist's fire, antitoxin) successfully in mission
- Silver: Craft or use alchemical items in 3 different missions
- Gold: Craft masterwork alchemical solution that solves mission objective creatively

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** Alchemical items deal +2 damage or provide +2 to associated check
- **Silver:** Can craft basic alchemical items during short rest (3 per rest)
- **Gold:** Gain "Master Alchemist" ability - Craft legendary alchemical item once per arc (custom effect, DM approval)

**Unlocks:**
- K5 (Sabotage Armory): Can use alchemical acid to melt locks silently (no Thieves' Tools check)
- E1 (Corrupt Cleric): Can detect poison in communion wine (automatic success)

---

### 🔓 Lockbreaker
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Pick 5 locks successfully in single mission
- Silver: Pick 15 locks across campaign without failure
- Gold: K4 (Ironhold) - Open all 8 prison cells without using Warden's keys

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +2 to Thieves' Tools checks, can pick locks faster (half time)
- **Silver:** Can pick locks one difficulty tier higher than normal (DC 18 becomes DC 15)
- **Gold:** Gain "Master Lockpick" ability - Automatically succeed on any lock DC 20 or below once per mission

**Unlocks:**
- E4 (Legal Archive): Can bypass all locks to Restricted Section
- K5 (Sabotage Armory): Can open armory locks silently and quickly

---

### 🏃 Runner
**How to Earn:**
- Bronze: Complete chase sequence without failing any Athletics/Acrobatics check
- Silver: Win 3 chase sequences
- Gold: B5 (Hunt Agitator) - Catch Red Fox in 3 rounds or less

**Bonuses:**
- **Bronze:** +10 ft movement speed, advantage on chase checks
- **Silver:** Can Dash as bonus action (3/day)
- **Gold:** Gain "Parkour Master" ability - Ignore difficult terrain, can run on walls for 1 round (1/day)

**Unlocks:**
- B5 (Hunt Agitator): Automatically close gap each round (no checks needed until final rooftop)
- Front 2 (Streets): Can sprint between districts in half time

## Using Titles in Missions

### Title Benefits in Achievement Criteria

Titles directly affect mission achievement criteria:

**Example: K4 Ironhold Prison Break**

*Without Titles:*
- Full Success: Free all 8 prisoners, escape within 2 hours, no PC casualties

*With Breacher (Silver):*
- Can use "Silent Takedown" on Warden Thurl (auto-success on surprise)
- Full Success becomes easier: Warden neutralized, guards unaware, extra time

*With Lockbreaker (Gold):*
- Automatically succeed on all 8 cell locks
- Full Success achieved faster: Saves 8 rounds of lock-picking

### Mission Unlocks

Some missions have alternative objectives only available with specific titles:

**K5: Sabotage the Armory**

*Standard Objectives:*
- Destroy or steal 100 weapons
- Escape undetected

*With Breacher (Gold) + Alchemist (Silver):*
- **UNLOCKED: "Ghost Protocol"** - Use alchemical acid to melt armory locks, infiltrate as shadow, steal 150 weapons in complete silence
- Achievement: Legendary Success - Armory raided, guards never knew you were there

### Social Advantages

NPCs react to your active title:

**Title: Revolutionary Hero (Gold)**
- Cobbleward NPCs: Auto-friendly, provide aid without checks
- Guards: Hostile on sight, DC +5 to Persuasion
- Nobles: Intimidated, DC -2 to Intimidation

**Title: Champion of Reform (Gold)**
- Nobles: Respectful, DC -3 to Persuasion
- Clergy: Trusting, share information freely
- Revolutionaries: Skeptical, DC +2 to gain trust

**Title: Defender of Order (Gold)**
- Guards: Obey commands, DC -5 to command
- Common folk: Fearful but compliant
- Rebels: Will flee rather than fight you

## DM Guidance

### Awarding Titles

**When to Award:**
- **During Session:** Award Bronze titles immediately upon qualifying achievement
- **Between Sessions:** Award Silver/Gold titles after reviewing player performance
- **Milestone Moments:** Award special titles at end of acts

**Announcement:**
```
DM: "As you successfully extract all prisoners from Ironhold without a single alarm, the guards find only empty cells in the morning. Your reputation grows - you've earned the title 'Breacher (Bronze)'."
```

### Balancing Titles

**Power Level:**
- Bronze = +2 bonus or minor ability (available early Act II)
- Silver = +2 bonus + special ability (mid Act II)
- Gold = +3 bonus + powerful ability (late Act II / early Act III)

**Limiting Abuse:**
- Only ONE active title at a time
- Switching titles requires long rest in safe location
- Some titles have cooldowns (1/day, 1/session abilities)

### Custom Titles

Encourage players to propose custom titles:

**Player:** "My character has focused on protecting civilians. Can I earn a 'Protector of the People' title?"

**DM:** "Absolutely! Here's what you need:
- Bronze: Save 10+ civilians in single mission
- Silver: Save 50+ civilians across 3 missions
- Gold: Complete Front 2 (Streets) with zero civilian casualties

**Bonuses:**
- Bronze: +2 to Persuasion with civilians, they trust you
- Silver: Civilians will hide you from guards (advantage on Stealth in populated areas)
- Gold: Can call on civilian militia (2d6 commoners aid you once per session)"
