# K3: Guild Leader Negotiations - Dialogue Template

**Scene:** The Broken Mast Inn - Back Room
**Purpose:** Unite three dockworker guilds under revolutionary banner
**PCs Must Convince:** Torrin (Longshoremen), Salla (Fishmongers), Vex (Warehouse Workers)

---

## The Setting

**The Back Room:**
- Circular table with three chairs, one for PCs
- Smell of fish, sweat, and cheap ale
- Dim lighting from single lantern
- Tension palpable - these guild leaders have old rivalries

**Pre-Scene Preparation:**
If PCs spent time building trust (FREE-ROAM phase), they know:
- Torrin needs PROOF the revolution will succeed
- Salla is already angry and ready to strike
- Vex is calculating risk vs. reward
- Old guild rivalry: Torrin and Vex competed for same dock contracts 5 years ago

---

## Opening Scene - All Three Present

**As PCs Enter:**

> SALLA *(stands, gesturing wildly)*: "Finally! Someone with spine!" *(to Torrin and Vex)* "I told ye Ashka's people would come!"
>
> TORRIN *(arms crossed, skeptical)*: "Sit down, Salla. We'll hear 'em out first." *(eyes PCs)* "You say you're with Ashka. We've heard promises before. Why should we risk our necks for this revolution?"
>
> VEX *(quiet, calculating)*: "More importantly - what's in it for us? And what happens when we lose?" *(leans forward)* "Because if this goes wrong, the Duke crushes us."

**The Challenge is Clear:** Three personalities, three needs, one goal.

---

## Guild Leader Personalities

### Torrin (Longshoremen Guild)
**Personality:** Cautious, responsible, protective of his people
**Voice:** Deep, measured, stern
**Mannerisms:**
- Crosses arms when skeptical
- Leans back when considering options
- Scratches weathered beard when conflicted

**Key Motivations:**
- Protect his guild members and their families
- Won't commit to suicide mission
- Needs concrete proof revolution can win
- Respects strength and evidence

**Speech Pattern:**
- Formal, thoughtful
- "Let's be practical here..."
- "I've got 500 families depending on me..."
- Questions everything

---

### Salla (Fishmongers Guild)
**Personality:** Passionate, angry, ready to fight
**Voice:** Loud, emotional, rapid-fire
**Mannerisms:**
- Stands and paces when excited
- Pounds table to emphasize points
- Gets in people's faces

**Key Motivations:**
- Her husband was beaten by guards last year
- Tired of Duke's exploitation
- Already emotionally committed to revolution
- Wants others to match her courage

**Speech Pattern:**
- Interrupts others
- "Damn the consequences!"
- "Are ye cowards or fighters?"
- Curses when frustrated

---

### Vex (Warehouse Workers Guild)
**Personality:** Pragmatic, cold, calculating
**Voice:** Quiet, steady, unsettling calmness
**Mannerisms:**
- Counts on fingers when calculating
- Never raises voice
- Steeples fingers
- Makes eye contact to intimidate

**Key Motivations:**
- Pure self-interest - wants best deal
- Needs guarantees of protection
- Will abandon strike if it's failing
- Respects practical solutions

**Speech Pattern:**
- Short sentences
- Numbers and specifics: "How many fighters? What are the odds?"
- "Let's talk reality."
- Emotionless delivery

---

## Negotiation Sequence

### Round 1: Opening Arguments

**Torrin Opens:**
> TORRIN: "Here's my concern. The Duke has guards, weapons, the palace. What do we have? Clubs and righteous anger?" *(looks at Salla pointedly)* "That's not enough. I need to know we can win before I put my people in danger."

**PC Must Respond to Torrin:**

**Option 1 - Show Evidence (Tax Ledger from K1):**
> **PC:** *"Look at this. The Duke's hoarding 80,000 gold while you starve. This ledger proves it - the people will rise when they see this."*
>
> TORRIN *(takes ledger, studies it, jaw clenches)*: "Eighty thousand... and he told us there's no money for dock repairs?" *(looks up)* "If this is real, the city will riot." *(Persuasion DC reduced to 12)* "Alright, I'm listening. What's the plan?"

**Option 2 - Appeal to Revolutionary Momentum:**
> **PC:** *"The Cobbleward is with us. We've already hit the Duke's tax carriage [if K1 succeeded]. The momentum is building. Join us now, or be left behind."*
>
> TORRIN *(raises eyebrow)*: "You hit the carriage? That's bold." *(pauses)* "But bold doesn't mean smart. How many fighters does Ashka have? Real numbers."

**Option 3 - Promise Strength in Numbers:**
> **PC:** *"Alone, we're weak. United - all three guilds plus Ashka's revolutionaries - we're thousands strong. The Duke can't arrest us all."*
>
> TORRIN *(considers)*: "Maybe. But it only takes one informant to bring guards down on us before we're ready." *(looks at Vex and Salla)* "Can we trust everyone here?"

---

**Salla Interrupts (Always Supports PCs):**
> SALLA *(slams table)*: "Trust? I'll tell ye what I trust - that the Duke's a tyrant! My husband lost three teeth to guard batons for 'speakin' out of turn.' We're already suffering! A strike is the LEAST we can do!" *(to Torrin)* "When did ye become such a coward, Torrin?"

**Torrin's Response:**
> TORRIN *(voice hardens)*: "Coward? I'm being responsible! You want to charge in like a fool? Be my guest. I'm protecting my people."

**Tension is Building - PCs Must Mediate:**

**PC Option - Mediate the Argument:**
> **PC:** *"Stop. We're not enemies here. The Duke is. Torrin, your caution keeps people alive. Salla, your passion inspires them. We need both."*
>
> TORRIN *(grudgingly)*: "Fine. Continue."
>
> SALLA *(sits, still glaring at Torrin)*: "Just get on with it."

---

**Vex Speaks (Calculating):**
> VEX *(quiet, cutting through tension)*: "Let's talk numbers. How many guards does Brennis have? How many do we have? What happens if we lose?" *(steeples fingers)* "I need guarantees. Protection for strikers. Safe houses for families. Escape routes if it goes bad."

**PC Must Address Vex's Concerns:**

**Option 1 - Provide Tactical Details:**
> **PC:** *"Brennis has 200 guards, but they're spread thin across five districts. We have 500+ revolutionaries in Cobbleward, and if all three guilds strike, that's 300 more. We outnumber them where it counts."*
>
> VEX *(calculates)*: "800 to 200. Good odds." *(pause)* "But guards have training. Armor. We have clubs." *(looks up)* "How do we equip our people?"

**Option 2 - Promise Ashka's Protection:**
> **PC:** *"Ashka has safe houses throughout the city. Any striker who's targeted gets protection. She's already moved 50 families to safety. Your people will be protected."*
>
> VEX: "Ashka has resources, I'll grant that." *(pause)* "But what about after? If we win, who's in charge? I don't want to trade one tyrant for another."

**Option 3 - Offer Material Incentives:**
> **PC:** *"When this is over, the docks will be yours. No more Duke's taxes, no more corrupt officials skimming your profits. You'll control your own trade."*
>
> VEX *(eyes narrow, interested)*: "Complete control?" *(looks at Torrin and Salla)* "Split three ways?"
>
> TORRIN: "If we're doing this, aye. Equal shares."
>
> SALLA: "I don't care about profits - I care about justice!"
>
> VEX: "You may not care, but I do. And if this deal's real..." *(looks at PC)* "I'm interested."

---

## Round 2: Addressing Individual Concerns

### Convincing Torrin (Persuasion DC 14, or DC 12 if tax ledger shown)

**Torrin's Core Question:**
> TORRIN: "Give me one good reason to believe this revolution won't get my people killed."

**Successful Responses:**

**Response A - Demonstrate Past Success:**
> **PC:** *"We've already proven we can win. [Mention K1 success / K2 defense]. Each time, we've protected our people and struck the Duke's authority. This isn't a fantasy - it's happening."*
>
> TORRIN *(slow nod)*: "Aye, I heard about the carriage. That was risky, but ye pulled it off." *(looks at guild leaders)* "Maybe... maybe this has a chance."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 14**

**Response B - Appeal to His Responsibility:**
> **PC:** *"You're right to be cautious - that's what makes you a good leader. But ask yourself: is it more responsible to let your people suffer under the Duke's boot, or to fight for their future?"*
>
> TORRIN *(long pause, wrestling with it)*: "I... I've been asking myself that for months." *(voice heavy)* "Ye're right. Sitting by while they suffer... that's not protection. That's cowardice." *(stands)* "Alright. I'm in."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 14**

**Response C - Show the Tax Ledger:**
> **PC:** *"[Shows ledger] The Duke's been lying to you. There's money - he's hoarding it. Your people are dying of poverty while he feasts. This proves it."*
>
> TORRIN *(reads, face darkens)*: "This son of a... we've been begging for food subsidies while he's..." *(slams ledger down)* "You want a strike? You've got one." *(extends hand)* "Count the Longshoremen in."
> **Automatic success if tax ledger shown**

---

### Convincing Salla (Persuasion DC 10 - She's Already Ready)

**Salla's Core Question:**
> SALLA *(to PC, ignoring others)*: "When do we start? I'm ready now!"

**PC Just Needs to Organize Her Passion:**

**Response A - Channel Her Energy:**
> **PC:** *"Your fire is exactly what we need, Salla. But a successful strike needs coordination. We strike together, at the right moment, for maximum impact."*
>
> SALLA: "Coordination. Right." *(takes deep breath)* "Alright. I can wait. But not long! When Ashka gives the signal, we move."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 10 (Easy success)**

**Response B - Honor Her Sacrifice:**
> **PC:** *"Your husband suffered for speaking truth. We're going to make sure his sacrifice means something. This strike will honor him."*
>
> SALLA *(eyes well up)*: "His name was Garren. He'd be proud of this." *(voice firms)* "For Garren, and for everyone else they've hurt - I'm with ye."
> **Automatic success**

---

### Convincing Vex (Persuasion DC 15 - He Needs Guarantees)

**Vex's Core Question:**
> VEX *(flat tone)*: "What guarantees can you give me? Specifics."

**Successful Responses:**

**Response A - Provide Tactical Guarantees:**
> **PC:** *"Safe houses in Cobbleward and Temple District for any targeted strikers. Communication network via runners. Emergency escape routes through sewers. Armed revolutionaries will defend picket lines. Your people won't be abandoned."*
>
> VEX *(nods slowly)*: "Detailed. I like details." *(pause)* "And if guards raid our homes?"
>
> **PC:** *"We evacuate families first, before the strike goes public. They're safe before you're exposed."*
>
> VEX: "That... is acceptable." *(extends hand)* "You have my guild."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 15**

**Response B - Economic Incentive:**
> **PC:** *"When we win, you control the docks. No Duke's cut, no bribes to officials. Every gold piece goes to the guilds. You'll be three times richer within a year."*
>
> VEX *(calculates silently)*: "Three times richer... minus strike costs... plus risk..." *(looks up)* "The math works. But only if we win."
>
> **PC:** *"We will win. The momentum is already building."*
>
> VEX: "Alright. I'm in. But if this starts going bad, I'm pulling my people out. Non-negotiable."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 15**

**Response C - Promise Personal Protection:**
> **PC:** *"Ashka herself will guarantee your safety. You have her word - and mine. Your family will be under guard 24/7 once the strike begins."*
>
> VEX *(raises eyebrow)*: "Ashka's word carries weight. And yours?" *(studies PC)* "You seem competent enough." *(pause)* "Fine. I'll trust you. This time."
> **Persuasion Check: DC 15**

---

## If One Guild Leader Refuses

### If Torrin Refuses:
> TORRIN: "I can't do it. I won't risk my people on maybes." *(stands)* "I'm sorry, but the Longshoremen are out."
>
> SALLA *(furious)*: "Ye spineless—!"
>
> VEX *(quiet)*: "If Torrin won't commit, the numbers don't work. I'm out too."
>
> **Result:** Only Salla's guild strikes (100 workers, not 300). Partial success only.

**PC Can Make One Last Appeal:**
- Persuasion DC 16 (harder)
- Must address his core fear: *"Your people are already at risk under the Duke's rule. This is their chance to change that."*

### If Salla Refuses (Unlikely):
> SALLA *(bitter)*: "If ye don't have the stomach for this, neither do I. I won't die alone for yer cowardice."
>
> **Result:** Lose passionate champion. Strike lacks energy. DC +2 for others.

### If Vex Refuses:
> VEX: "The numbers don't add up. I'm out." *(stands, leaves without ceremony)*
>
> TORRIN: "If Vex won't commit, he's probably right - the risk's too high. I'm out too."
>
> **Result:** Only Salla strikes. Partial success.

---

## Round 3: Sealing the Alliance (All Three Agree)

**When All Three Are Convinced:**

> TORRIN *(stands, extends hand to PC)*: "Alright. You've convinced me. The Longshoremen stand with the revolution."
>
> SALLA *(jumps up, grinning)*: "Finally! About damn time!" *(to Torrin)* "I knew ye had it in ye!"
>
> VEX *(stands slowly)*: "The Warehouse Workers are in. But remember - we have terms. Break them, and we're out."
>
> TORRIN *(looks at the other two)*: "If we're doing this, we do it right. United front. No backing out halfway."

**Bonus Objective Opportunity - The Signed Pact (2/3 Ashka Quest Chain):**

**PC Must Ask:**
> **PC:** *"Will you sign a formal alliance? A written commitment to the revolution?"*

**Their Responses:**

> SALLA: "Sign it in blood for all I care! Where's the parchment?"
>
> VEX *(cautious)*: "A written document is dangerous. If guards find it..."
>
> TORRIN: "It's also binding. Shows we're serious." *(to Vex)* "We're already risking everything. What's one more risk?"
>
> VEX *(long pause)*: "...Fine. I'll sign."

**The Signing:**
> TORRIN *(signs first, firm signature)*: "For the Longshoremen Guild, and for Estellon."
>
> SALLA *(signs eagerly)*: "For justice! For Garren! For the revolution!"
>
> VEX *(signs last, neat careful signature)*: "For the Warehouse Workers. And for profit, when this is done."

**Torrin raises a mug:**
> TORRIN: "To the revolution. And to Ashka - may she lead us to victory."
>
> ALL THREE: "To Ashka! To the revolution!"

**✅ BONUS OBJECTIVE COMPLETE: All three guilds formally allied (2/3 Ashka Quest Chain)**

## Post-Negotiation Dialogue

**Discussing Strike Logistics:**

> TORRIN: "When do we strike? Ashka needs to coordinate this with her plans."
>
> SALLA: "The sooner the better! We've waited too long already!"
>
> VEX: "We need three days to prepare. Move families, organize communications, brief workers. Anything faster is sloppy."

**PC Sets Timeline:**
> **PC:** *"Three days. I'll tell Ashka. On the morning of the fourth day, no one goes to work."*
>
> TORRIN: "The docks will be silent. The Duke will feel it immediately."
>
> SALLA: "And when the guards come to break it up?"
>
> **PC:** *"We'll be ready."*

**Final Words:**

> VEX *(to PC, quiet)*: "You've got nerve. I respect that. Don't let us down."
>
> SALLA *(claps PC on back)*: "Ye're alright! When this is done, drinks are on me!"
>
> TORRIN *(serious, grips PC's forearm)*: "Five hundred families trust me. I'm trusting you. Don't make me regret it."

---

## If Negotiation Fails Completely

**All Three Refuse:**

> TORRIN: "I'm sorry. But I can't commit to this."
>
> SALLA *(deflated)*: "If Torrin and Vex won't stand with us... I won't die alone." *(voice breaks)* "I wanted to fight. I really did."
>
> VEX: "This meeting is over." *(leaves)*

**PC Returns to Ashka:**
- Ashka is disappointed but understanding: "The docks were always going to be hard. We'll find another way."
- Strike mission fails; must build revolutionary strength elsewhere
- Guilds remain neutral through Act II

## Complications & Interruptions

### Complication 1: Old Guild Rivalry Erupts

**During heated argument:**

> VEX *(to Torrin, cold)*: "You cost me the West Dock contract five years ago with your backroom dealing. How do I know you won't betray this too?"
>
> TORRIN *(stands, angry)*: "That contract? You were overcharging! I saved the merchants money!"
>
> VEX: "You saved yourself money, you mean."
>
> SALLA *(exasperated)*: "Oh for the love of—! This is why nothing ever changes! Ye're too busy fighting each other!"

**PC Must Mediate (Persuasion DC 13):**

**Success:**
> **PC:** *"The Duke loves that you fight each other. It keeps you weak. Don't give him what he wants."*
>
> TORRIN *(sits, grudging)*: "...You're right. Old grudges can wait."
>
> VEX: "Agreed. For now."

**Failure:**
> One or both walk out. Must chase them down individually and renegotiate.

---

### Complication 2: Guard Patrol Nearby

**Mid-negotiation:**

> BARTENDER *(bursts in)*: "Guards outside! Routine patrol, but they're asking questions!"
>
> TORRIN *(tense)*: "If they find us together like this..."
>
> VEX: "We're done for."

**PC Options:**
1. **Hide (Stealth DC 12):** Everyone hides until patrol passes
2. **Bluff (Deception DC 14):** "We're just having drinks, officer!"
3. **Bribe (50 gold):** Pay the guards to look the other way
4. **Fight (Bad Idea):** Attacking guards reveals plot immediately

---

### Complication 3: Informant Present

**If PC rolls low Insight (below 10):**

> VEX *(whispers to PC)*: "That man in the corner. He's been listening. I've seen him talking to guards before."

**Informant is a Brennis spy. PC must deal with him:**
1. **Confront:** Intimidation DC 15 to scare him off
2. **Bribe:** 100 gold to keep quiet
3. **Capture:** Grapple check, then decide what to do with him
4. **Ignore:** He reports to Brennis, strike is compromised (guards raid before it starts)

## Roleplaying Tips for DM

**Torrin:**
- Speak slowly, thoughtfully
- Cross arms frequently
- When convinced, show visible relief - he wanted to join but needed justification
- Protective father figure energy

**Salla:**
- Loud, frequent interruptions
- Stand up when excited
- Emotional vulnerability when talking about husband
- Infectious enthusiasm when committed

**Vex:**
- Never raise voice
- Maintain unsettling eye contact
- Pause before responding (he's always calculating)
- Cold, but not cruel - just pragmatic

**Dynamic Between Them:**
- Salla and Torrin bicker like old friends
- Vex is the outsider - newer guild leader
- Torrin and Vex have old rivalry (5 years ago)
- Salla gets frustrated with both when they hesitate

## Success Indicators

✅ **Full Success + Bonus:** All three sign formal alliance pact
✅ **Full Success:** All three verbally commit to strike
⚠️ **Partial Success:** 1-2 guilds commit (weaker strike)
❌ **Failure:** No guilds commit to strike

**Quest Chain Progress:**
- ✅ Bonus Objective Achieved: 2/3 Ashka Quest Chain complete
- Unlocks K5 bonus path (Arm the People - final step)
