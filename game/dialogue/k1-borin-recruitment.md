# K1: Borin Ironheart Recruitment - Dialogue Template

**Scene:** During the Tax Carriage Ambush
**Purpose:** Convince Borin to defect from the guards and join the revolution
**Base DC:** 13 Persuasion (11 if PCs mention protecting workers)

---

## Borin's Personality & Speech Pattern

**Voice:** Gruff dwarf, weary but kind-hearted
**Accent:** Scottish-inspired, drops some pronouns
**Mannerisms:**
- Grips his warhammer nervously when conflicted
- Looks away when ashamed of his work
- Voice softens when talking about his family or craft

**Key Motivations:**
- Wants to forge weapons for a just cause, not oppression
- Fears for his wife (Helga) and children (Torin, age 8; Mara, age 5)
- Deeply ashamed of working for the Duke's regime
- Values honor and protecting the weak

---

## Opening Context

**If PCs surprise attack without talking:**
> BORIN *(defensive stance, not attacking)*: "Wait! I've no quarrel with ye! I'm just here 'cause they forced me!" *(Persuasion DC 11 to convince him to stand down)*

**If PCs approach before violence:**
> BORIN *(lowers warhammer slightly)*: "Who are ye? Revolutionaries?" *(looks around nervously at guards)* "I... I can't help ye. Me family's in the city. If the Duke learns I turned..." *(trails off)*

---

## Persuasion Approaches & Dialogue Trees

### Approach 1: Appeal to Justice (Persuasion DC 13)

**PC Option:** *"You're making weapons that oppress your own people. Is that the legacy you want?"*

> BORIN *(grimaces)*: "Ye think I don't know that? Every blade I forge ends up in the hands of guards beatin' down workers. Good folk. Me folk." *(grips hammer tighter)* "But what choice do I have? The Duke's got me by the throat."

**Follow-up - Emphasize Revolution's Cause:**
> **PC:** *"We're fighting for those workers. For families like yours. We need someone who believes in the cause to arm us."*
>
> BORIN *(looks conflicted)*: "Arm the revolution? Forge weapons for... for freedom?" *(pause, searching PC's face)* "Ye really mean it? This isn't just more violence?"

**Success Path:**
> **PC:** *"This is about protecting people like your family. Join us."*
>
> BORIN *(slowly lowers weapon)*: "Aye. Aye, ye're right. I'm done forging chains." *(looks at Captain Marlis)* "Sorry, Captain. I'm out." *(turns to PCs)* "Tell me where, and I'll set up a smithy. But swear to me - ye'll protect me wife and children."

**DC Met - He Defects:**
- Drops warhammer
- Provides intelligence: "The carriage has a false bottom. Tax ledger's hidden there - key's 'round Marlis's neck."
- "Accidentally" opens carriage door during fight (grants advantage to one PC)

---

### Approach 2: Show Evidence of Corruption (Persuasion DC 11 if tax ledger shown)

**PC Option:** *"Look at this tax ledger. The Duke's hoarding wealth while your people starve. You're helping him do it."*

> BORIN *(takes ledger, scans it, face darkens)*: "Eighty thousand gold... and we're told there's no coin for healers? For food?" *(slams fist on carriage)* "That whoreson liar!"

**Follow-up:**
> BORIN *(voice shaking with anger)*: "Me brother died last winter. Couldn't afford medicine. And the Duke's got... this?" *(throws ledger down)* "What do ye need from me?"

**Success Path - Automatic:**
> **PC:** *"We need a smith. Someone who'll arm the people, not their oppressors."*
>
> BORIN: "Done. I'll forge weapons for ye. For the revolution. For me brother." *(to guards)* "Stand down, lads. This fight's over for me."

---

### Approach 3: Promise Protection (Persuasion DC 13)

**PC Option:** *"We can protect your family. We're not leaving anyone behind. Join us, and they'll be safe in Cobbleward."*

> BORIN *(eyes narrow)*: "Protect them how? Ye're rebels, not an army. The moment I defect, Brennis sends guards to me home."

**Follow-up - Provide Specifics:**
> **PC:** *"Ashka has safe houses. We'll move your family tonight. Guards, revolutionaries - they'll be watched over."*
>
> BORIN: "Ashka... the Flameheart?" *(scratches beard)* "Heard she's fierce. Protects her people." *(long pause)* "Swear it. Swear on yer honor ye'll protect Helga and the wee ones."

**Success Path:**
> **PC:** *"I swear it. Your family will be safe."*
>
> BORIN *(extends hand)*: "Then I'm with ye. Let's get this done, and I'll go gather me family."

---

### Approach 4: Appeal to His Craft (Insight DC 14 to notice his shame)

**PC Option:** *"You're a master smith. Is this really what you want your hammers to create? Weapons of oppression?"*

> BORIN *(looks down at warhammer, voice quiet)*: "I was trained by me father. He taught me: 'A smith's work is sacred. Forge tools that help folk, weapons that protect the innocent.' Every night, I hear his voice askin' me what I've done." *(pause)* "I've shamed his memory."

**Follow-up:**
> **PC:** *"Then honor it. Forge weapons for those who fight for the innocent. Redeem your craft."*
>
> BORIN *(tears in eyes)*: "Redeem me craft..." *(voice breaks)* "Aye. Aye, that's what I'll do." *(to guards)* "I'm sorry, but I can't do this anymore." *(to PCs)* "Where do ye need me?"

---

## If Persuasion Fails

### Failed Attempt (Below DC)

> BORIN *(shakes head)*: "I... I can't. I'm sorry, but me family... I can't risk it." *(backs toward guards)* "Ye should go. Now. Before more guards arrive."

**PC Options:**
1. **Try again with different approach** (one more attempt, DC +2)
2. **Knock him out non-lethally** (can be rescued/recruited later in Act II)
3. **Let him go** (missed opportunity for bonus objective)

### If Combat Continues Without Persuasion:

> BORIN *(fights defensively)*: "I don't want to hurt ye! Please, just... just leave!"
> *(Will surrender if reduced below 10 HP)*: "Enough! I yield! Don't... don't kill me. Me children need their father."

**After Surrender:**
- Second chance to recruit (DC 11, he's shaken)
- If recruited after surrender: "Ye spared me. I owe ye a debt."

---

## Mid-Combat Persuasion

**If PCs try to talk during active combat:**

> BORIN *(parrying attacks)*: "Are ye mad? We're in the middle of a fight!"

**PC can use Action to make Persuasion check (DC 13):**

**Success:**
> BORIN *(lowers weapon mid-swing)*: "Damn it all. Ye're right." *(to guards)* "I'm out, lads!" *(disengages, won't attack PCs, but guards may attack him)*

**Failure:**
> BORIN: "I can't listen to this now! Fight or flee, but stop talkin'!"

---

## Borin's Combat Dialogue (If Fighting)

**Round 1:** "I don't want this! Why can't ye just leave?"
**Round 2:** "Me family's countin' on me!"
**Round 3 (if bloodied):** "Please... I just want to go home..."
**If ally drops:** "No! This has gone too far!"

---

## Captain Marlis's Interruptions

**If Borin hesitates:**
> MARLIS *(shouting)*: "Borin! Remember your oath! Defend the carriage or I'll have you arrested for treason!"

**If Borin defects:**
> MARLIS *(enraged)*: "Traitor! You've signed your family's death warrant!" *(turns to guards)* "Kill him with the others!"

**PC Response Option:** *"Threaten his family and you'll answer to all of Cobbleward!"* (Intimidation DC 15 to make Marlis reconsider)

---

## Post-Combat Success Dialogue

**If Borin Recruited:**

> BORIN *(catches breath, looks at fallen guards)*: "I never thought it'd come to this." *(to PCs)* "Ye saved me, in a way. Gave me the courage to do what's right."

**PC:** *"What happens now?"*

> BORIN: "I need to get Helga and the wee ones. Move them somewhere safe before word spreads. Then... then I'll set up a smithy. A proper one. I'll forge weapons for yer cause." *(pauses)* "What do ye need first?"

**PC Options:**
1. *"Weapons for our fighters."* - "Aye. Swords, axes, spears. I'll start tonight."
2. *"Can you repair equipment?"* - "Of course. Bring me anything broken."
3. *"How long until you're operational?"* - "Give me three days to set up. After that, I'm at yer service."

> BORIN *(extends hand for handshake)*: "Thank ye. For givin' me a chance to be a smith me father would be proud of." *(grips firmly)* "I won't let ye down."

---

## Roleplaying Notes for DM

**Borin's Inner Conflict:**
- He WANTS to join the revolution but is terrified for his family
- Every mention of family makes him waver
- He's looking for an excuse to do the right thing - give him one
- His shame is palpable - he knows he's been complicit

**Speech Patterns:**
- "Aye" instead of "yes"
- "Ye" instead of "you"
- "Me" instead of "my" (me family, me hammer)
- "Wee ones" for children
- "That whoreson" or "Damn it all" when angry (keep PG-13)

**Body Language:**
- Shoulders slumped when talking about his work
- Eyes light up when discussing proper smithcraft
- Voice cracks when talking about his father or children
- Stands taller when deciding to join revolution

**If Players Are Struggling:**
- Have Borin drop hints: "If only I knew me family would be safe..."
- Show his internal conflict: *Borin looks at his warhammer with disgust*
- Let him ask questions: "This revolution of yers... it's really about helpin' folk, aye?"

---

## Connection to Mission K5 (Arm the People)

**Foreshadowing:**
> BORIN: "If ye're serious about this revolution, ye'll need more than just me smithy. The Duke's armory has enough weapons to outfit an army. If ye could... liberate those weapons..." *(trails off meaningfully)*

**This plants the seed for Mission K5's bonus objective (Arm the People).**

---

## Alternative: If Borin Dies or Escapes

**If Borin is killed:**
- Guards flee in horror: "You murdered a civilian!"
- Ashka is disappointed: "He was a victim, not our enemy."
- Mission K5 bonus objective is unavailable
- In Act III, his widow confronts PCs: "Ye killed me husband!"

**If Borin escapes:**
- Can be re-recruited in Mission K2 or K3
- DC reduced to 11 (he's had time to think)
- "Ye spared me before. I should have joined ye then. I'm here now."

---

## Success Indicators

✅ **Full Success:** Borin defects during combat, actively helps PCs, pledges to join revolution
✅ **Partial Success:** Borin stands down, doesn't fight but doesn't actively help
❌ **Failure:** Borin fights to defend himself, escapes, or is killed

**Bonus Achievement Trigger (1/3 Ashka Quest Chain):**
- Borin explicitly commits: "I'll set up a secret smithy and start forgin' weapons tonight."
- Provides specific location: "There's an old mill in Cobbleward. I'll work there."
- Ready to begin immediately: "What do ye need first? Blades? Polearms? I'll forge night and day."
