## How to Use These Templates

### During Session Prep:
1. **Read the full template** before the session
2. **Mark dialogue paths** most likely based on your party's tendencies
3. **Prepare key NPC voices** - practice accents/speech patterns
4. **Note the DCs** for various approaches
5. **Highlight alternative branches** in case players surprise you

### During the Session:
1. **Don't read verbatim** - use templates as inspiration, adapt to your table
2. **Let players drive** - respond to their approaches, don't railroad to specific dialogue
3. **Use personality notes** to improvise responses beyond template
4. **Track success conditions** - know when bonus objectives are achieved
5. **Adapt tone** to your table's preferences (more/less dramatic as needed)

### Customization:
- **Adjust DCs** based on party composition and power level
- **Change names** if you've modified NPCs in your campaign
- **Add/remove dialogue** based on session pacing
- **Merge approaches** if players combine multiple tactics

## Creating Your Own Dialogue Templates

If you want to create similar templates for other encounters, follow this structure:

### Template Structure:
1. **Scene Context** - Where, when, why
2. **NPC Personality Profile** - Voice, mannerisms, motivations, speech patterns
3. **Opening Dialogue** - How the scene begins
4. **Dialogue Tree** - Multiple approaches and branches
5. **Success/Failure Paths** - What happens for each outcome
6. **Mid-Scene Complications** - Interruptions, twists
7. **Resolution Dialogue** - How the scene concludes
8. **Roleplaying Tips** - Notes for DM to bring NPC to life

### Key Elements:
- ✅ Multiple player approach options
- ✅ Clear success conditions and DCs
- ✅ NPC personality consistency
- ✅ Memorable quotes for dramatic moments
- ✅ Connection to broader campaign arc
- ✅ Alternative paths for different party choices
