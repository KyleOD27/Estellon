## Simplified Game Mechanics

### Basic Resolution System
- **Simple Check:** Roll 2d6 + relevant bonus
  - 6 or less: Failure
  - 7-9: Partial success
  - 10+: Full success

### Combat (Simplified)
- **Attack:** Roll 2d6 + weapon damage vs enemy defense
- **Defend:** Roll 2d6 + armor bonus
- **Health:** Start with 6 health points

### Skill Bonuses (From Cards)
- Stack all bonuses from Role, Race, Items, and Abilities
- Maximum bonus: +5

## Printing Instructions

### Materials Needed
- **Cardstock** for cards (110lb or heavier recommended)
- **11x17 paper** for tableau boards
- **Card sleeves** (optional, for protection)
- **Laminator** (optional, for tableau)

### Card Quantities to Print
- **7 Role cards** (one of each role)
- **5-6 Race cards** (common races)
- **10-12 Weapon cards** (variety of options)
- **8-10 Armor cards** (light to heavy options)
- **20-30 Item cards** (consumables and tools)
- **15-20 Ability cards** (earned through play)

### Assembly Tips
1. Print cards on cardstock
2. Cut with paper cutter for clean edges
3. Optional: Round corners with corner punch
4. Sleeve cards for durability
5. Laminate tableau boards
6. Use dry-erase markers on laminated reputation tracker

## Storage Solution
- **Card box** or **deck boxes** for each card type
- **Portfolio folder** for tableau boards
- **Dice bag** for 2d6 per player
- **Dry erase markers** for tracking reputation

## Digital Alternative
Create this system in:
- **Tabletop Simulator**
- **Roll20** with card decks
- **Google Slides** for online play
