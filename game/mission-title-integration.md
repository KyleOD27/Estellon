## Quick Reference: Missions with Title Rewards

**Earnable Titles:**
- 🥉 **Breacher (Bronze)** - Complete ambush with zero alarm raised

## DM Quick Reference

### When to Award Titles

**Immediate (Bronze):**
- First time achieving specific feat
- Announce at table: "You've earned [Title]!"
- Player can activate immediately

**Between Sessions (Silver):**
- After 3 similar successes
- Award during recap at start of next session
- Requires consistency, not just luck

**Major Milestone (Gold):**
- Legendary achievement or quest completion
- Award with fanfare during dramatic moment
- Often comes with narrative recognition (NPCs know your reputation)

---

### Title Activation

**Switching Titles:**
- Requires long rest in safe location
- Player announces: "I'm switching from Breacher to Orator"
- DM notes active title on tracking sheet

**NPC Recognition:**
- NPCs react to active title
- Revolutionary Hero: Common folk hero-worship
- Champion of Reform: Nobles respect
- Defender of Order: Guards salute
