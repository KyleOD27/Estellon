## How Missions Work

### Mission Structure

Each mission card contains:
- **Mission Name** - Memorable title
- **Mission Type** - Category (Sabotage, Investigation, Protection, Recruitment, etc.)
- **Granted By** - Which faction leader offers this mission
- **Prerequisites** - What's needed to unlock (faction reputation, previous missions)
- **Main Objective** - Primary goal that affects Progress Tracks
- **Bonus Objective** - Optional goal advancing Leader Quest Chain
- **Success/Failure** - Mechanical outcomes and narrative consequences
- **Suggested Approaches** - Different ways to tackle the mission
- **Key NPCs** - Important characters involved
- **Location(s)** - Where the mission takes place

### Acquiring Missions

**From Faction Leaders:**
- Players aligned with a faction receive missions from their leader
- Missions become available based on:
  - Individual faction reputation (minimum required)
  - Current Progress Track levels (city mood affects available missions)
  - Completion of previous missions in quest chains

**Timing:**
- Early Act II (Zeal/Control/Coalition 0-3): Foundational missions
- Mid Act II (4-6): Escalation missions
- Late Act II (approaching 7): Critical missions before Mandate reveal

**Mission Availability:**
- DM can offer 2-3 missions simultaneously, letting players choose
- Some missions have time limits or expire if ignored
- Opposing factions react to completed missions (Faction Turn)

### ⚔️ ENCOUNTER: The Tax Carriage Ambush
**Duration:** 45-60 minutes
**Encounter Type:** Combat with Social Elements
**Purpose:** Execute the heist, recruit Borin, seize the wealth

#### Main Objective
Intercept and rob the quarterly tax collection carriage traveling from the Trade District to the Palace. Seize the tax ledger showing where money actually goes (evidence of corruption).

#### Bonus Objective (Leader Quest 1/3)
**Secure a Smith:** The carriage is being guarded by Borin, the city's master weaponsmith, who is sympathetic to the cause but forced to work for the regime. Convince him to defect and join the revolution.

#### Achievement Criteria
**Full Success:**
- Acquire the tax ledger (physical evidence of corruption)
- Seize at least 500 gold worth of tax money
- Borin agrees to join the revolution (Persuasion DC 13 success)
- No PC deaths

**Partial Success:**
- Acquire tax ledger OR seize money (at least one accomplished)
- Borin escapes but remains neutral (Persuasion attempted but failed)

**Failure:**
- Tax carriage escapes intact
- Borin is killed or arrested
- Multiple PC deaths/captures

**Bonus Achievement (1/3 Ashka Quest Chain):**
- Borin explicitly commits to forging weapons for the revolution
- Borin provides location for secret smithy

#### Success Outcomes
- **Main Success:** +2 Revolutionary Zeal (money redistributed to Cobbleward)
- **Bonus Success:** Borin joins revolution (1/3 Ashka Quest Chain complete)
- **Full Success Narrative:** The people celebrate the wealth redistribution; Borin begins secretly forging weapons

#### Failure Outcomes
- **Main Failure:** No change to Zeal; +1 City Control (crackdown on Cobbleward)
- **Bonus Failure:** Borin arrested, must be rescued later
- **Partial Success Option:** Get ledger but Borin escapes back to guards

#### Suggested Approaches
1. **Ambush** (Combat) - Overpower the guards on the road
2. **Deception** (Social) - Pose as replacement guards, trick the drivers
3. **Stealth** (Infiltration) - Sneak aboard at a rest stop, steal silently
4. **Persuasion** (Borin Focus) - Talk to Borin before the ambush, convince him to help from inside

#### Key NPCs
- **Borin Ironheart** (Dwarf Weaponsmith, age 45)
  - Alignment: Neutral Good
  - Personality: Gruff but kind; hates what his work is used for
  - Motivation: Wants to forge weapons for a just cause, not oppression
  - Persuasion DC: 13 (lower if PCs mention protecting workers)
  - **📖 [Detailed Dialogue Template](dialogue/k1-borin-recruitment.md)** - Full conversation options, speech patterns, multiple persuasion approaches
- **Guard Captain Marlis** (Human Fighter)
  - Veteran, loyal to Brennis, will fight to the death
  - Combat: Medium difficulty (CR 3-4 encounter for party of 4)

#### Locations
- **Ambush Site:** Forest road between Trade District and Noble Quarter
- **Alternative:** Warehouse District if PCs choose to intercept at night

#### Combat Encounter: The Tax Carriage Ambush

**Setup:**
The tax carriage travels from Trade District to the Palace along a forest road. It's guarded by veteran guards and Borin the weaponsmith (unwillingly). PCs must intercept, seize the tax ledger and gold, and ideally convince Borin to defect.

**Enemy Forces:**

*Guard Captain Marlis* (Human Fighter 4 - Veteran)
- AC 18 (chain mail, shield)
- HP 37
- Melee: Longsword +6 (1d8+3 slashing), Extra Attack
- Ranged: Heavy crossbow +5 (1d10+2 piercing) 100/400
- Abilities: Second Wind, Action Surge, Fighting Spirit (bonus action, 3 temp HP + advantage on attacks for 1 round, 3/day)
- Tactics: Loyal to Brennis, fights to the death to protect the carriage
- Will call for reinforcements if battle lasts 5+ rounds (arrive round 8)

*Borin Ironheart* (Dwarf Fighter 3 - Reluctant Guard)
- AC 16 (chain mail)
- HP 32
- Melee: Warhammer +5 (1d8+3 bludgeoning)
- Abilities: Second Wind, Dwarven Resilience
- **Key Trait:** Does NOT want to fight; will accept surrender/defection if approached correctly
- Persuasion DC 13 during combat to convince him to stand down (DC 11 if PCs mention protecting workers)
- If convinced, provides intelligence about carriage defenses and distracts guards

*Carriage Guards* (4x Veterans - CR 3 each)
- AC 17 (splint armor)
- HP 58 each
- Melee: Longsword +5 (1d8+3 slashing), Extra Attack
- Abilities: Two guards have heavy crossbows (+5, 1d10+3, 100/400)
- Tactics: Two guards stay with carriage, two engage PCs directly
- Will surrender if Marlis falls and outnumbered 2:1

*Carriage Driver* (Commoner)
- AC 10
- HP 4
- Non-combatant; will flee if able or surrender immediately

**The Carriage:**
- AC 15, HP 50
- Contains: 500 gold, tax ledger (locked box DC 16 Thieves' Tools), valuable goods
- Horses can be spooked (DC 13 Animal Handling to control, or DC 12 Intimidation to frighten them)
- If destroyed: Gold scattered, ledger at risk of damage

**Battlefield Map:**
```
[Forest Cover - PC ambush position]
         |
[Road - Carriage traveling west to east]
         |
[Rocks/Trees - Cover options]    [Steep Ravine - 20 ft drop, dangerous terrain]
```

**Combat Phases:**

**Phase 1: Ambush (Surprise Round if successful)**
- PCs can attempt Stealth DC 14 to gain surprise round
- Surprise allows PCs to act first; guards flat-footed (AC -2 for round 1)
- If no surprise, guards form defensive perimeter around carriage

**Phase 2: Initial Engagement (Rounds 1-3)**
- Guards defend carriage; crossbowmen provide covering fire
- Marlis shouts orders, coordinates defense
- **Borin Moment:** PCs can attempt to persuade Borin during this phase
- If Borin persuaded: He "accidentally" opens carriage door, distracts a guard (ally gains advantage)

**Phase 3: Resolution (Rounds 4+)**
- If Marlis still stands: She makes last stand at carriage
- If Borin defected: Remaining guards demoralized (disadvantage on attacks)
- If carriage undefended: PCs can loot (requires 2 rounds to secure ledger and gold)
- Reinforcements arrive round 8 if alarm raised (6 additional guards, 1 sergeant)

**Tactical Ambush Options:**
1. **Tree Blockade:** Fell tree across road (requires prep time, DC 14 Str check); carriage must stop
2. **Spooked Horses:** Target horses first; DC 12 Animal Handling by guards to maintain control
3. **Split the Party:** Half engage guards, half secure carriage and valuables
4. **Borin First:** Approach Borin directly before violence, talk him down (Persuasion DC 13)

**Environmental Hazards:**
- **Forest Cover:** Trees provide half-cover (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves)
- **Ravine:** If carriage forced off road, crashes (50% destroy ledger); DC 15 Acrobatics to avoid 2d6 falling damage
- **Muddy Road:** Difficult terrain; mounted guards at disadvantage

**Moral Considerations:**
- Killing Marlis: Honorable soldier doing her duty (may haunt PCs)
- Sparing guards: Can bind and leave them (no casualties, +1 Coalition Strength from mercy)
- Borin's family: He mentions wife and children; PCs can offer protection to sweeten deal

**Scaling:**
- **Easier (party level 2-3):** Marlis is Fighter 3 (HP 27), only 2 carriage guards
- **Harder (party level 5-6):** Marlis is Fighter 5 (HP 45) with Indomitable, 6 guards, Borin initially hostile (DC 15 to persuade)

**Success Outcomes by Approach:**
- **Bloodless Victory:** All guards subdued/surrendered, no deaths → Legendary (+3 Revolutionary Zeal, Borin definitely joins)
- **Standard Victory:** Ledger + gold secured, Marlis defeated, Borin recruited → Full Success
- **Costly Victory:** Objectives met but Borin killed or fled, heavy PC casualties → Partial Success
- **Carriage Escapes:** Failed ambush → +1 City Control (guards report rebel activity)

**Loot:**
- 500 gold pieces (for redistribution)
- Tax Ledger (proof of corruption, can be published)
- Marlis's Masterwork Longsword (+1 weapon)
- Borin's future services (weapon crafting, repairs, eventually weapon caches)

**Encounter Type:** Combat Defense or Stealth Evacuation
**Purpose:** Protect the press, ensure Elena survives

#### Bonus Objective
**No Leader Quest** - Pure defensive mission

#### Achievement Criteria

- All press workers (including Elena) survive

**Partial Success:**
- Press survives but damaged (requires 1 week repair)
- Elena survives but some workers captured
- Press relocated successfully before raid

**Failure:**
- Press destroyed beyond repair
- Elena killed or captured
- Multiple civilian casualties

**Critical Success:**
- Press intact AND no guards escape (perfect defense)

#### Success Outcomes
- **Success:** +1 Revolutionary Zeal; printing press survives, more pamphlets spread
- **Critical Success (no guards escape):** +2 Revolutionary Zeal; guards fear Cobbleward

#### Failure Outcomes
- **Failure:** +2 City Control; press destroyed, -1 Revolutionary Zeal
- **Partial (press destroyed but rebels escape):** No track changes, but must find new location

#### Suggested Approaches
1. **Direct Defense** (Combat) - Fortify location, fight off guards
2. **Misdirection** (Deception) - Plant false information, send guards to wrong location
3. **Evacuation** (Stealth) - Move press before guards arrive
4. **Turn the Tables** (Ambush) - Let guards enter, trap them inside

#### Key NPCs

- AC 15 (studded leather)
- HP 38
- Speed 30 ft.
- Melee: Poisoned dagger +7 (1d4+4 piercing + 2d6 poison, DC 14 Con save or poisoned for 1 minute)
- Ranged: Hand crossbow +7 (1d6+4 piercing, 30/120), loaded with poisoned bolt (same poison)
- Abilities:
  - **Assassinate:** Advantage on attacks vs. creatures that haven't acted yet; auto-crit on surprised creatures
  - **Sneak Attack:** +3d6 damage once per turn when having advantage
  - **Cunning Action:** Bonus action Dash/Disengage/Hide
  - **Uncanny Dodge:** Reaction to halve damage from one attack
- Equipment: Smoke bomb (bonus action, creates 10-ft heavily obscured area for 1 minute), disguise kit, grappling hook
- Tactics: Strikes from hiding with surprise round if possible; uses hit-and-run; attempts to flee if reduced below 15 HP to be interrogated later

**Magistrate Helena** (Civilian Target)
- AC 12 (no armor)
- HP 22
- Cannot defend herself effectively
- Will panic if attacked (Wisdom save DC 12 or cower, losing action)
- Must survive for mission success

**Crowd** (30 civilians attending speech)
- Provide cover for assassin (heavily obscured due to crowd movement)
- May panic if combat erupts (50% chance each round, causing difficult terrain)
- Assassin can blend into crowd (Deception +6 vs. Perception to spot)

**Battlefield Map:**
```
[Courthouse Steps - Helena speaking]

[Open Plaza - 30 civilians gathered]
         |
[Rooftops - Assassin's likely position]    [Market Stalls - Cover/escape routes]
```

**Phase 2: The Strike (Rounds 3-5)**
- Assassin attempts kill (poisoned crossbow bolt or dagger)
- If first attempt fails, uses smoke bomb and tries again
- PCs must position between Helena and assassin

**Phase 3: Pursuit or Interrogation (Rounds 6+)**

- Chase sequence: Athletics/Acrobatics checks DC 13 to keep pace

- Interrogation reveals employer (Lord Pellor, radical cell, or corrupt noble - DM choice)

**Scaling:**
- **Easier (party level 3-4):** Assassin is Rogue 3 (HP 24, +2d6 sneak attack), no smoke bomb

```

         |

    |         |
```

**Phase 1: Negotiation (Pre-Combat)**

#### Combat Encounter: The Rooftop Chase

**Setup:**
Intelligence reports the Red Fox will appear at a protest in Market Square tonight. The PCs must identify, pursue, and capture this elusive agitator. This is a chase encounter with combat elements.

**The Red Fox** (Sera - Human Rogue 4)
- AC 15 (studded leather)
- HP 27
- Speed 30 ft., Climb 30 ft. (Athlete feat)
- Melee: Shortsword +6 (1d6+3 piercing)
- Abilities: Sneak Attack +2d6, Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge
- Equipment: 3 smoke bombs, grappling hook, caltrops
- Skills: Acrobatics +8, Stealth +8, Deception +5
- Tactics: Evade, don't kill; uses smoke bombs and terrain to escape; will surrender if cornered with no escape

**The Chase Sequence:**

**Phase 1: Identification (Market Square)**
- 50 protesters gathered, Red Fox addressing crowd
- PCs must identify Red Fox among crowd (Perception DC 15, or Insight DC 13 if watching for agitator behavior)
- If spotted, Red Fox notices being watched (passive Perception 14) and flees
- If not spotted before speech ends, Red Fox vanishes into crowd (DC 18 Investigation to track)

**Phase 2: The Pursuit (5 Chase Segments)**
Each segment is 60 feet and requires an ability check. Red Fox has a 60-foot head start.

**Segment 1: Market Square to Alley**
- Obstacle: Crowded market stalls
- Check: Acrobatics DC 12 to vault stalls, or Athletics DC 14 to push through crowd
- Failure: Knock over stall (difficult terrain, vendor angry, lose 30 ft)
- Red Fox: Automatically succeeds (knows the route)

**Segment 2: Narrow Alley**
- Obstacle: Stacked crates and barrels
- Check: Athletics DC 13 to climb/jump, or Acrobatics DC 11 to squeeze through gaps
- Failure: Crates collapse, DC 13 Dex save or take 1d6 bludgeoning damage, lose 30 ft
- Red Fox Action: Throws smoke bomb (heavily obscured area, disadvantage on Perception)

**Segment 3: Rooftop Ascent**
- Obstacle: Climbing building wall (20 ft)
- Check: Athletics DC 14 to climb quickly, or use grappling hook (automatic if PC has one)
- Failure: Slow climb (lose 30 ft) or fall DC 15 Acrobatics save (2d6 falling damage)
- Red Fox: Uses grappling hook (automatic success)

**Segment 4: Rooftop Run**
- Obstacle: Gaps between buildings (10 ft jumps)
- Check: Athletics DC 13 to jump, or Acrobatics DC 15 to use ropes/swinging
- Failure: Fall to alley (2d6 damage, chase ends for that PC)
- Red Fox Action: Scatters caltrops behind her (1d4 damage, DC 12 Perception to spot, speed halved if stepped on)

**Segment 5: Final Rooftop**
- Obstacle: Dead end - cornered on rooftop above tavern
- Check: Now combat or negotiation begins
- Red Fox: Fights if no escape, or surrenders if offered mercy (Persuasion DC 13)

**Combat (If Red Fox Fights):**
- Red Fox prefers hit-and-run tactics
- Uses Cunning Action to Disengage and reposition
- Attacks with Sneak Attack if she has advantage
- Will jump to lower rooftop (10 ft drop, DC 10 Acrobatics) to continue fleeing
- Surrenders if reduced below 10 HP or all escape routes blocked

**Alternative: Street Level Pursuit**
If PCs lose rooftop chase, Red Fox descends to streets:
- Hides in crowd (DC 16 Perception to spot)
- May disguise quickly (Disguise kit, DC 14 Investigation to see through)
- Can be tracked to safe house (Investigation DC 15, or Survival DC 13 if tracking through streets)

**Complications:**
- **Guard Patrol:** Random guards may interfere (think PCs are criminals), DC 13 Persuasion to explain
- **Innocent Bystanders:** On rooftops (clotheslines, residents); harming them costs Coalition Strength
- **Red Fox's Allies:** 1d4 sympathizers may try to misdirect PCs (Deception vs. Insight)
- **Weather:** If raining, slippery rooftops (all checks +2 DC)

**Capturing Red Fox:**
- Combat: Reduce to 0 HP (non-lethal preferred)
- Grapple: DC 14 Athletics/Acrobatics contested check
- Net/Magic: Restrained condition, Persuasion DC 13 to convince surrender
- Corner: All escape routes blocked, Intimidation DC 12 or Persuasion DC 13

**Interrogation (If Captured):**
- **Intimidation DC 15:** Reveals safe house location and 2-3 rebel sympathizers
- **Persuasion DC 13:** Reveals information willingly + may be turned as double agent
- **Failed Interrogation:** Sera stays silent or gives false information

**Scaling:**
- **Easier (party level 3-4):** Sera is Rogue 2 (HP 16), chase DCs reduced by 2
- **Harder (party level 6-7):** Sera is Rogue 6 (HP 40), chase DCs increased by 2, has Magic Initiate (Misty Step 1/day)

**Success Outcomes:**
- **Captured Alive:** +2 City Control, interrogation provides intelligence
- **Captured + Turned:** +3 City Control, ongoing double agent benefits
- **Killed:** +1 City Control (rebels lose agitator but gain martyr)
- **Escaped:** +1 Revolutionary Zeal (Red Fox becomes legend)

**Chase Tracking:**
Track distance between PCs and Red Fox:
- Start: 60 ft apart
- Each successful check: Close gap by 30 ft (or maintain if Red Fox succeeds)
- Each failed check: Gap increases by 30 ft
- If gap reaches 180 ft: Red Fox escapes (lost in city)

Raid Ashka's safehouse and capture or kill revolutionary leadership. This could end the rebellion in one stroke.

**Partial Success:**

**Failure:**

- Guards ambushed; multiple casualties
- Raid becomes propaganda victory for rebels

- Entire guard force ambushed and defeated
- Revolution becomes legendary
- +3 Revolutionary Zeal instead of City Control

**Bonus Achievement (Capture Ashka Alive - EXTREMELY DIFFICULT):**
- Ashka captured alive (requires DC 20+ combination of combat, magic suppression, and tactics)
- Ashka brought to trial publicly
- Revolutionary movement broken without martyr
- Campaign shifts dramatically toward Brennis victory

#### Success Outcomes
- **Success:** +3 City Control (revolution crippled)
- **Bonus Success:** +4 City Control, -3 Revolutionary Zeal (Ashka captured, movement broken)
- **Revolutionary path essentially ended** (campaign shifts dramatically)

#### Failure Outcomes
- **Failure:** +3 Revolutionary Zeal (failed raid makes Ashka a legend)
- **Guards killed in ambush**
- **PCs may be wounded or captured**

#### Suggested Approaches
1. **Overwhelming Force** (20+ guards + PCs) - Brennis's plan
2. **Surgical Strike** (stealth team) - Fast extraction before rebels rally
3. **Siege** (surround, wait them out) - Slow but safer
4. **Warning** (if PCs conflicted) - Tip off Ashka, fake raid

#### Key NPCs
- **Ashka** (Sorcerer 7, Chaotic Good)
  - Will fight with wild magic and desperation
  - Has escape routes prepared
  - Will not surrender (capture DC 20+ combination of combat/magic suppression)
- **Rebel Defenders** - 15-20 revolutionaries
  - Willing to die for the cause

#### Locations
- **Ashka's Safehouse** - See [City Map](city-map.md)

#### Combat Encounter: Assault on the Safehouse

**Setup:**
Intelligence has revealed Ashka's safehouse location. Captain Brennis has authorized a raid to capture or kill the revolutionary leader and break the rebellion. This is a high-stakes, high-difficulty encounter with serious campaign consequences.

**Enemy Forces:**

*Ashka the Flameheart* (Human Wild Magic Sorcerer 7 - Revolutionary Leader)
- AC 13 (mage armor)
- HP 45
- Speed 30 ft.
- Spells Prepared: Mage Armor, Shield, Burning Hands, Scorching Ray, Fireball, Counterspell, Fly, Greater Invisibility
- Spell Slots: 4/3/3/1
- Abilities:
  - **Wild Magic Surge:** When she casts 1st level or higher spell, roll d20; on 1, roll Wild Magic Surge table
  - **Tides of Chaos:** Advantage on one attack/ability check/save; recharge when Wild Magic Surge triggers
  - **Bend Luck:** Reaction to add 1d4 to ally's roll or subtract from enemy's (3 sorcery points)
  - **Sorcery Points:** 7 points; can quicken spells or convert to spell slots
- Tactics:
  - Opens with Fireball from hiding
  - Uses Fly to reach rooftops/escape routes
  - Counterspells enemy magic
  - Will flee via sewer if reduced below 15 HP
  - **Will NOT surrender** - dies before capture (DC 20+ to take alive)

*Elena "Ink-Fingers"* (Human Rogue 4 - Trusted Lieutenant)
- AC 14 (leather armor)
- HP 28
- Melee: Shortsword +6 (1d6+3 piercing)
- Ranged: Light crossbow +6 (1d8+3 piercing) 80/320
- Abilities: Sneak Attack +2d6, Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge
- Equipment: Smoke bomb, caltrops, rope
- Tactics: Hit-and-run, uses smoke bomb to cover Ashka's escape
- Will surrender if Ashka dies or escapes

*Revolutionary Veterans* (6x Thugs - CR 1/2 each)
- AC 12 (leather armor)
- HP 32 each
- Melee: Clubs and improvised weapons +4 (1d6+2 bludgeoning)
- Pack Tactics
- Morale: Fanatically loyal; fight to the death to protect Ashka
- 2 carry nets (DC 13 Str check to escape)

*Revolutionary Defenders* (10x Commoners)
- AC 11 (improvised armor)
- HP 4 each
- Melee: +2 (1d4 bludgeoning)
- Will flee if 5+ commoners drop or Ashka flees
- Some may surrender if offered quarter

**Friendly Forces:**

*Captain Brennis* (Optional - may personally lead raid)
- AC 17 (half plate)
- HP 75
- Fighter 6 with Extra Attack, Action Surge, Indomitable
- Melee: Longsword +8 (1d8+4 slashing)
- Commands guard forces efficiently

*Elite Guard Squad* (8-12 Guards - if B6 completed)
- AC 16, HP 11 each
- Better coordinated than normal guards (+2 to hit when adjacent to ally)

*PC Squad* (8x Regular Guards)
- AC 16, HP 11 each
- Follow PC orders

**Battlefield - The Safehouse:**
```
[Rooftop Access - Ashka's escape route]
         |
[3rd Floor - Ashka's quarters, trapped]
         |
[2nd Floor - Barricaded, rebel defenders, narrow stairs]
         |
[1st Floor - Entry point, 4 veterans guard door]
         |
[Basement - Sewer access tunnel, Elena guards]
```

**Traps & Defenses:**
- **Entry Alarm:** DC 16 Perception to spot; ringing alerts entire safehouse
- **Stairwell Barricades:** Provide half-cover to defenders; DC 15 Str check to break through
- **Oil Slick (2nd Floor):** DC 14 Dex save or fall prone; can be ignited (2d6 fire damage)
- **Caltrops (3rd Floor):** Scattered; 1d4 piercing damage, speed halved
- **Sewer Grate (Basement):** Locked; DC 18 Str check to force or DC 16 Thieves' Tools

**Combat Phases:**

**Phase 1: Breach (Rounds 1-2)**
- Guards breach door (or PCs infiltrate stealthily)
- 4 veterans defend entry fiercely
- Alarm sounds if not disabled (alerts everyone)

**Phase 2: Floor-by-Floor Assault (Rounds 3-6)**
- Each floor must be cleared
- Rebels fight defensively, buying time for Ashka
- Elena sets traps and delays pursuers

**Phase 3: Ashka's Stand (Rounds 7-10)**
- Ashka makes final stand on 3rd floor or rooftop
- Uses Fireball and Scorching Ray aggressively
- Wild Magic may cause unexpected effects (DM rolls)
- If reduced below 15 HP: Attempts Fly + Greater Invisibility escape

**Phase 4: Pursuit (Rounds 11+)**
- If Ashka flees: Chase through sewers or across rooftops
- Elena may sacrifice herself to buy Ashka time
- DC 14 Athletics/Acrobatics checks to keep up
- If Ashka escapes, raid fails

**Special Mechanics:**

**Wild Magic Chaos:**
When Ashka casts spells under stress, Wild Magic may trigger:
- Could buff allies (Turn Blue, gain 1d10 temp HP)
- Could harm self (Cast Fireball centered on self)
- Could create opportunity (Teleport 60 ft randomly - may escape)
- DM uses Wild Magic Surge table

**Capture Mechanics:**
- To take Ashka alive requires: Reduce to 0 HP non-lethally + DC 18 Str check to restrain + DC 16 Arcana check to suppress magic
- Even captured, she may be rescued in Act III or become martyr if executed

**Moral Dilemmas:**
- Commoners surrender: Accept surrender or "no witnesses" (Brennis suggests latter)
- Elena captured: Interrogate, spare, or execute
- Ashka's journal found: Contains names of sympathizers (arrest them?)
- Families hiding in safehouse: Children present (collateral damage risk)

**Scaling:**
- **Easier (party level 4-5):** Ashka is Sorcerer 5 (HP 33, no 4th level spells), 8 total rebels
- **Harder (party level 7-8):** Ashka is Sorcerer 9 (HP 60, 5th level spells like Cone of Cold), 20 total rebels, additional escape routes

**Success Outcomes:**
- **Ashka Killed:** +3 City Control, -3 Revolutionary Zeal (revolution crippled but martyr created)
- **Ashka Captured:** +4 City Control, -3 Revolutionary Zeal (can be tried/executed publicly or rescued)
- **Ashka Escapes:** +3 Revolutionary Zeal (becomes legend, "Unkillable Flame")
- **Failed Raid:** +3 Revolutionary Zeal, multiple guard casualties (Brennis furious)

**Campaign Impact:**
- **Ashka Removed:** Revolutionary missions unavailable in future; other faction must fill power vacuum
- **Ashka Survives:** She becomes more cautious, harder to find; may appear in Act III finale
- **Commoner Casualties:** Families of killed rebels join revolution (Act III enemies)
- **Clean Raid:** Minimal casualties earn respect even from opponents

**Loot:**
- Ashka's Arcane Focus (if defeated)
- Revolutionary Manifesto (political document)
- Safe with 300 gold (revolutionary funds)
- Intelligence documents (maps of weapon caches, sympathizer lists)
- Elena's printing press plates (if she's captured/killed)

**Granted By:** Captain Brennis

Enforce a strict citywide curfew (9 PM - 6 AM). Arrest anyone on the streets after dark. Project overwhelming presence to suppress final resistance before total control.

#### Achievement Criteria

- Curfew enforced for 7 consecutive days
- At least 50 arrests for violations
- Visible guard presence in all districts
- Compliance rate above 80%

**Partial Success:**
- Curfew enforced 3-6 days
- 20-49 arrests
- Some districts comply, others resist
- Compliance rate 50-79%

**Failure:**
- Curfew openly defied

- Rebels organize resistance
- Compliance rate below 50%

**Bonus Achievement (Zero Violations - Draconian):**
- Perfect curfew compliance for full week
- Zero successful violations
- Harsh penalties deter resistance
- Atmosphere of total fear established

## DM Guidance: Running Missions

### Pacing Act II

**Early Act II (Sessions 1-3):**
- Offer foundational missions (K1, E1, B1, B2)
- Establish faction dynamics
- Begin Leader Quest Chains

**Mid Act II (Sessions 4-6):**
- Escalation missions (K3, E3, B3, B4)
- Continue quest chains
- Introduce opposing faction missions (raids, counter-ops)

**Late Act II (Sessions 7-9):**
- Critical missions (K5, E4, B6)
- Complete quest chains (empower leaders)
- Push toward threshold 7

**Climax (Session 10+):**
- Mandate Reveal when any track hits 7
- Vault Showdown
- Transition to Act III

### Dynamic Mission Selection

- **Offer Choice:** Present 2-3 missions at once
- **Time Pressure:** Some missions expire or change if delayed
- **Faction Reactions:** Opposing factions react via Faction Turn
- **Consequences:** Failed missions have real impacts

### Opposing Faction Missions

When PCs complete a mission, opposing factions may respond:
- **K2 (Protect Press)** triggers **B1 (Shut Down Press)**
- **E2 (Expose Rykard)** removes him from B-missions
- **B3 (Informants)** enables counter to **K8 (Counter-Intel)**

### Moral Complexity

Encourage moral questioning:
- **Ashka's missions** may harm innocents (collateral damage)
- **Elara's missions** may be too slow (people suffer waiting for reform)
- **Brennis's missions** may be oppressive (arresting protesters)

### Custom Missions

DMs can create additional missions using this template:
- Clear main objective (affects Progress Tracks)
- Optional bonus objective (quest chain or special reward)
- Multiple approach options
- Meaningful consequences for success/failure
- Ties to established NPCs and locations
