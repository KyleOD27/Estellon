# City Map: Estellon

> *"A city of faded grandeur, where every street tells a story of what was and what might yet be."*

---

## Overview

Estellon is a mid-sized city built along the Silverrun River, once prosperous under wise leadership but now decaying under Duke Valerius II's negligent rule. The city is divided into distinct districts, each reflecting the social and political tensions that define the campaign.

**Population:** Approximately 15,000 citizens
**Government:** Dukedom (currently Duke Valerius II)
**Military:** City Guard commanded by Captain Brennis
**Geography:** Built on both banks of the Silverrun River, connected by three stone bridges

## Districts & Key Locations

### 1. The Noble Quarter

**Location:** Northwest district, elevated ground
**Population:** ~1,500 (nobles, wealthy merchants, servants)
**Atmosphere:** Wide cobblestone streets, ornate townhouses, manicured gardens

#### Key Locations:

**The Ducal Palace**
- **Description:** A once-magnificent seat of power, now showing signs of neglect
- **Occupants:** Duke Valerius II, Lord Pellor (Chamberlain), palace staff
- **Access:** Heavily restricted; nobles and those with official business only
- **Notable Features:** Great Hall (where the Council of Estellon convenes), Duke's chambers, throne room

**The Goldleaf Estate**
- **Description:** Largest noble manor, owned by House Goldleaf
- **Significance:** Potential ally for Elara's Coalition; hosts political gatherings
- **Access:** By invitation only

**The Promenade**
- **Description:** Elegant tree-lined avenue where nobles take afternoon walks
- **Encounters:** Political discussions, social maneuvering, information gathering

---

### 2. The Temple District

**Location:** Northeast district, adjacent to Noble Quarter
**Population:** ~800 (clerics, pilgrims, monastery residents)
**Atmosphere:** Peaceful, spiritual, incense-scented air

#### Key Locations:

**The Cathedral of the Eternal Scales**
- **Description:** Grand cathedral dedicated to the god of justice and law
- **Occupants:** High Priestess Mara, clerics, paladins
- **Significance:** Source of Ser Elara's divine authority; potential Coalition ally
- **Access:** Open to public for worship; inner sanctum restricted

**The Pilgrim's Rest**
- **Description:** Inn and hospice for travelers and the faithful
- **Services:** Healing, sanctuary, information from travelers
- **Access:** Open to all; safe haven during unrest

**The Chapterhouse of the Silver Oath**
- **Description:** Headquarters of the paladin order; Ser Elara's base of operations
- **Occupants:** Ser Elara Vance, 12 paladins, squires
- **Access:** Open courtyard; inner chambers by permission
- **Notable Features:** Training grounds, armory, meeting hall

---

### 3. The Trade District

**Location:** Central-west district, spanning both sides of Middle Bridge
**Population:** ~4,000 (merchants, artisans, guild members)
**Atmosphere:** Bustling, colorful, commercial energy

#### Key Locations:

**The Market Square**
- **Description:** Large open plaza where the Sunstone Festival Riot occurs (Act I)
- **Features:** Vendor stalls, auction platform, town crier's stand
- **Encounters:** Merchants, pickpockets, recruiters, protests
- **Significance:** Neutral ground where all factions meet

**The Guild Hall**
- **Description:** Multi-story building housing various craft guilds
- **Occupants:** Guildmasters, artisans, apprentices
- **Significance:** Key to Kael's revolutionary support; artisan organizing
- **Access:** Guild members and those with business

**The Grumbling Ox Inn**
- **Description:** Large, respectable inn where the PCs first stay
- **Services:** Lodging (5 silver/night), meals, common room gathering space
- **Proprietor:** Marta Thickbrew (neutral, gossip source)
- **Atmosphere:** Middle-class travelers, merchant deals, rumor mill

**The Goldweigh Exchange**
- **Description:** Banking house and money-lender
- **Services:** Currency exchange, loans, secure storage
- **Significance:** Merchant role players have access; financial intelligence

---

### 4. The Guard Barracks & Ironhold Prison

**Location:** East-central district, fortified section
**Population:** ~600 guards + ~50-150 prisoners
**Atmosphere:** Martial, tense, watchful

#### Key Locations:

**The Barracks Compound**
- **Description:** Walled military complex; headquarters of the City Guard
- **Occupants:** Captain Brennis, Sergeant Rykard, 500+ city guards
- **Features:**
  - Training yard and armory
  - Officers' quarters
  - Command center
  - Guard mess hall
  - Stables
- **Access:** Military personnel and official visitors only
- **Significance:** Captain Brennis's seat of power

**■ Ironhold Prison** *(New Location)*
- **Description:** Imposing stone fortress attached to the eastern wall of the Barracks; three stories of cells built into thick stone walls reinforced with iron bands
- **Capacity:** 150 prisoners (currently 80-120 depending on recent arrests)
- **Structure:**
  - **Ground Floor:** Intake and processing, guard station, interrogation rooms
  - **Second Floor:** Standard cells for common criminals (theft, brawling, unpaid debts)
  - **Third Floor (The Irons):** High-security cells for political prisoners, dangerous criminals, and "enemies of the state"
  - **Basement (The Pit):** Solitary confinement; damp, dark, rarely used except for punishment
- **Security:**
  - 20 dedicated prison guards (separate from patrol guards)
  - Iron-banded oak doors with multiple locks
  - Narrow windows with bars (too small to escape through)
  - Single entrance through Barracks compound (heavily guarded)
- **Conditions:**
  - Ground/Second Floor: Harsh but survivable; communal cells, basic rations
  - The Irons: Isolated individual cells, minimal light, interrogation access
  - The Pit: Brutal; used for "breaking" prisoners
- **Current Prisoners:**
  - Petty criminals, debtors, brawlers (Second Floor)
  - Revolutionary sympathizers arrested during crackdowns (The Irons)
  - Conscription resisters (mix of Second Floor and The Irons)
- **Significance:**
  - **Act I:** Players might be arrested here if they attack Sergeant Rykard or resist authority
  - **Act II:** Potential rescue missions for rebel prisoners; interrogation scenes
  - **Faction Missions:**
    - **Kael's Rebels:** "Break out the arrested protesters from Ironhold"
    - **Elara's Reformers:** "Investigate prisoner mistreatment; gather evidence of illegal detention"
    - **Brennis's Guard:** "Interrogate a captured rebel agitator in The Irons"
- **Access:**
  - **Normal:** Impossible without official business or guard authority
  - **Visitor Access:** Rare; requires Captain Brennis's written permission
  - **Escape Difficulty:** Extremely high (DC 18+ for lockpicking, Stealth, or deception)
- **NPC:** **Warden Thurl** - A grim, by-the-book veteran loyal to Brennis
  - Alignment: Lawful Neutral
  - Personality: Humorless, efficient, believes in "order through discipline"
  - Interaction: Will not bend rules; respects chain of command absolutely

**The Eastern Gatehouse**
- **Description:** Fortified gate controlling eastern approach
- **Features:** Murder holes, reinforced gate, archer positions
- **Significance:** Act III defense point during siege

### 5. The Cobbleward (Poor District)

**Location:** Southwest district, low-lying near river docks
**Population:** ~6,000 (laborers, refugees, poor families)
**Atmosphere:** Cramped, desperate, defiant

#### Key Locations:

**The Tannery Row**
- **Description:** District of smelly, hard labor; tanning, dyeing, butchering
- **Significance:** Where Aldric the Tanner (Act I, Part 2) works
- **Atmosphere:** Brutal work, low pay, resentment

**Kael's Safehouse (Secret)**
- **Description:** Hidden refuge in abandoned tenement or sewer access
- **Occupants:** Kael, inner circle of rebels, rotating safe refugees
- **Access:** Only by Kael's invitation; known to trusted revolutionaries
- **Features:** Weapons cache, printing press for pamphlets, escape routes

**The Rusty Nail Tavern**
- **Description:** Rough tavern where Sergeant Rykard frequents (Elara's Act I quest target)
- **Atmosphere:** Seedier than Grumbling Ox; gambling, brawls, corrupt dealings
- **Proprietor:** "One-Eye" Garrett (knows everyone's secrets, for a price)
- **Significance:** Information gathering, underworld contacts

**The Soup Kitchen (Temple-Run)**
- **Description:** Charity food distribution run by temple volunteers
- **Significance:** Where common folk gather; recruiting ground for all factions
- **Atmosphere:** Desperation mixed with community solidarity

---

### 6. The Dockside

**Location:** Southeast district along the Silverrun River
**Population:** ~2,000 (dockworkers, sailors, smugglers)
**Atmosphere:** Rough, transient, semi-lawless

#### Key Locations:

**The Wharves**
- **Description:** Wooden docks for river trade and fishing boats
- **Significance:** Supply lines; smuggling routes; Guild Representative hook
- **Activity:** Loading/unloading cargo, fishmongering, black market

**The Warehouse District**
- **Description:** Storage facilities for goods awaiting distribution
- **Significance:** Potential sabotage targets (Act II); theft missions
- **Security:** Varies from none (abandoned) to heavy (valuable goods)

**The Broken Mast Inn**
- **Description:** Sailors' tavern and flophouse
- **Atmosphere:** Transient population, foreign travelers, smugglers
- **Services:** Cheap lodging, rumors from upriver/downriver

## Campaign Use: District Control by City Mood

Districts shift control based on faction dominance (see **[City Mood System](game-overview.md)**):

### Revolutionary Fervor (Kael 5+)
- **Cobbleward:** Rebel stronghold; guard patrols rare
- **Dockside:** Smuggling weapons; workers strike
- **Trade District:** Graffiti, pamphlets, protests

### Martial Law (Brennis 5+)
- **All Districts:** Checkpoints, curfews enforced
- **Cobbleward:** Heavy patrols, mass arrests to Ironhold
- **Noble Quarter:** Protected, isolated from unrest

### Hopeful Reform (Elara 5+)
- **Temple District:** Public debates, reform meetings
- **Trade District:** Guild support for legal change
- **Noble Quarter:** Some nobles openly support Coalition

### Powder Keg (Multiple Factions 6+)
- **Market Square:** Rival faction rallies clash
- **Street borders:** De facto territories emerge
- **Ironhold Prison:** Overflowing with political prisoners

## Secret Locations (DM Only)

### The Vault of Founding
- **Location:** Hidden beneath the Ducal Palace
- **Contents:** The Crown-Regent's Mandate (revealed Act II climax)
- **Access:** Secret; Lord Pellor knows the entrance

### Forgotten Tunnels
- **Location:** Old smuggler tunnels beneath Cobbleward and Dockside
- **Use:** Escape routes, infiltration paths, rebel movements
- **Knowledge:** Kael knows them; refugees know rumors

### The High Priestess's Sanctum
- **Location:** Cathedral inner chamber
- **Significance:** Where Elara seeks the Church's blessing (Act II quest)
- **Access:** Restricted to highest clergy and those summoned

## Travel Times (On Foot)

- **Noble Quarter ↔ Cobbleward:** 25 minutes
- **Temple District ↔ Trade District:** 15 minutes
- **Barracks ↔ Market Square:** 10 minutes
- **Any Gate ↔ Palace:** 20-30 minutes
- **Crossing city (North to South):** 45 minutes

**Note:** Times increase during:
- Curfews (guards stop and question)
- Riots (blocked streets, danger)
- Checkpoints (Martial Law city mood)
