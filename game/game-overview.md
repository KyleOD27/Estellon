# Game Overview: Core Mechanics

> *"This framework is designed to make political intrigue tangible and engaging, turning philosophical debates into measurable game mechanics with clear consequences."*

---

## 1. Progress Tracks (The Faction War)

### What it is
Three scores (0-10) that measure the city's balance of power.

#### Revolutionary Zeal 
- **Who:** Ashka's rebellion 
- **Measures:** Public support and revolutionary fervor
- **High Score Effects:** Riots, civil disobedience, underground networks grow

#### City Control
- **Who:** Captain Brennis's guard
- **Measures:** Order, fear, and authoritarian grip
- **High Score Effects:** Curfews, checkpoints, oppressive patrols

#### Coalition Strength
- **Who:** Elara's reformers
- **Measures:** Political and institutional support
- **High Score Effects:** Legal challenges, noble backing, church support

### How it works
- **Mission Success/Failure** modifies these tracks (+1 to +3 typically)
- **Critical Threshold:** When any track hits **7**, Lord Pellor panics and reveals the **Crown-Regent's Mandate**, triggering the final race of Act II
- **Dynamic Balance:** As one faction rises, others may react and counter

### DM Use
The current scores dictate:
- **City Mood** - Tense, hopeful, fearful, or chaotic (see City Mood System below)
- **Random Encounters** - Protests, guard patrols, or political rallies
- **NPC Attitudes** - How citizens and officials react to the PCs

## City Mood System

### Overview
The **City Mood** represents Estellon's overall atmosphere based on the relative strength of faction scores. Calculate it at the start of each session to inform narration, encounters, and NPC behavior.

### How to Calculate City Mood

#### Step 1: Identify the Dominant Faction(s)
- **Clear Dominance:** One faction is 3+ points higher than all others
- **Contested Leadership:** Two factions within 2 points of each other (both high)
- **Balanced/Stalemate:** All three factions within 2 points of each other
- **Power Vacuum:** All factions below 4

#### Step 2: Check Overall Tension Level
Add all three faction scores together:
- **0-9:** Low Tension (Early conflict)
- **10-17:** Rising Tension (Escalating crisis)
- **18-24:** High Tension (Boiling point)
- **25+:** Critical Mass (City on edge of explosion)

#### Step 3: Determine City Mood

### City Mood States

#### 1. **FRAGILE PEACE**
**Conditions:** All factions 0-3, Total < 10
- **Atmosphere:** Calm before the storm; people sense something brewing
- **Street Life:** Normal activities, but whispered concerns in taverns
- **Guard Presence:** Standard patrols, relaxed posture
- **Encounters:** Merchants worried about future, anxious nobles, quiet tension
- **Mechanical Effects:** +1 to social checks; information flows freely

---

#### 2. **REVOLUTIONARY FERVOR**
**Conditions:** Revolutionary Zeal 5+, Zeal leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Defiant energy, underground meetings, graffiti everywhere
- **Street Life:** Protests, rallies, workers organizing openly
- **Guard Presence:** Nervous patrols, avoid Cobbleward at night
- **Encounters:** Revolutionaries recruiting, guard crackdowns, secret supply lines
- **Mechanical Effects:** +2 to recruiting common folk; -2 to noble audiences; random protests

**Sub-Mood - Revolutionary Uprising (Zeal 7+):**
- Open riots, barricades in poor districts
- Guards only patrol in force
- +1 Revolutionary Zeal per session automatically

---

#### 3. **MARTIAL LAW**
**Conditions:** City Control 5+, Control leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Oppressive fear, checkpoints everywhere, informants watching
- **Street Life:** Curfews enforced, people hurry home, streets empty at night
- **Guard Presence:** Heavy patrols, suspicious of everyone, quick to arrest
- **Encounters:** Random stop-and-searches, informant accusations, public punishments
- **Mechanical Effects:** -2 to Stealth in open; +2 to Intimidation by authority; curfew restrictions

**Sub-Mood - Iron Fist (Control 7+):**
- Brutal crackdowns, mass arrests
- Fear dominates all social interaction
- +1 City Control per session automatically

---

#### 4. **HOPEFUL REFORM**
**Conditions:** Coalition Strength 5+, Coalition leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Cautious optimism, public debates, intellectual energy
- **Street Life:** Political discussions in squares, pamphlets everywhere, hope in air
- **Guard Presence:** Moderate, trying to appear reasonable
- **Encounters:** Town hall meetings, petition drives, noble reformers speaking publicly
- **Mechanical Effects:** +2 to Persuasion in debates; +1 to gathering noble support; peaceful rallies

**Sub-Mood - Peaceful Transition (Coalition 7+):**
- Major reforms being discussed openly
- System changing from within
- +1 Coalition Strength per session automatically

---

#### 5. **POWDER KEG**
**Conditions:** Two factions at 6+, within 2 points of each other
- **Atmosphere:** Extreme tension, everyone choosing sides, violence imminent
- **Street Life:** Divided city, rival rallies, street fights between factions
- **Guard Presence:** Overwhelmed, picking sides themselves
- **Encounters:** Faction confrontations, forced loyalty checks, targeted violence
- **Mechanical Effects:** All social DCs increase by 2; violence erupts randomly; factions demand commitment

**Variations:**
- **Zeal vs Control:** Street battles between rebels and guards
- **Zeal vs Coalition:** Radicals vs moderates, ideology war
- **Control vs Coalition:** Authority vs reform, institutional crisis

---

#### 6. **CHAOS REIGNS**
**Conditions:** All three factions 5+, within 3 points of each other
- **Atmosphere:** Complete breakdown of order, three-way conflict, city paralyzed
- **Street Life:** Different districts controlled by different factions, internal borders
- **Guard Presence:** Fighting two fronts, desperate and dangerous
- **Encounters:** Crossfire situations, trapped civilians, district borders, desperate deals
- **Mechanical Effects:** All checks difficulty increased; random faction violence; no safe zones

---

#### 7. **COLD WAR**
**Conditions:** Total 10-17, all factions within 2 points (balanced tension)
- **Atmosphere:** Tense standoff, shadow conflicts, information warfare
- **Street Life:** People cautious, rumors everywhere, fear of provoking conflict
- **Guard Presence:** Watching and waiting, tense but restrained
- **Encounters:** Spies, propaganda battles, proxy conflicts, covert operations
- **Mechanical Effects:** Deception and Insight checks favored; violence avoided but tensions high

---

#### 8. **BOILING POINT**
**Conditions:** Total 18+, any faction approaching 7
- **Atmosphere:** City on knife's edge, one incident from explosion
- **Street Life:** Everyone braced for war, hoarding supplies, avoiding conflict
- **Guard Presence:** Battle-ready, treating everything as potential riot
- **Encounters:** Desperate negotiations, final chances for peace, inevitable violence looming
- **Mechanical Effects:** Failed social checks can trigger major incidents; factions hypersensitive

---

#### 9. **MANDATE CRISIS**
**Conditions:** Any faction hits 7 (triggers Crown-Regent's Mandate reveal)
- **Atmosphere:** Everything changes overnight; the race for power begins
- **Street Life:** Open three-way conflict for control of the Mandate
- **Guard Presence:** All factions mobilizing for final confrontation
- **Encounters:** Desperate alliances, betrayals, all-or-nothing moves
- **Mechanical Effects:** Acts as transition into Act III endgame; all faction actions intensify

---

### Using City Mood in Play

#### Narrative Application
- **Opening Description:** Start each session with City Mood atmosphere
- **NPC Reactions:** Filter all social interactions through current mood
- **Random Encounters:** Use mood to determine what players encounter on streets
- **Escalation:** Show how player actions shift the mood session to session

#### Example Narration
**Fragile Peace → Revolutionary Fervor:**
> *"When you arrived in Estellon two weeks ago, the Sunstone Festival felt like a celebration. Now, as you walk through the Cobbleward, revolutionary graffiti covers every wall, workers gather in angry crowds, and the air crackles with defiant energy. The peace was an illusion—the revolution has begun."*

**Hopeful Reform → Powder Keg:**
> *"Last session, nobles debated reform in comfortable parlors. Today, a revolutionary march collided with a guard checkpoint, and blood stains the cobblestones. The moderates' moment has passed—now, only force remains."*

#### Mechanical Integration
- Adjust social DCs based on mood
- Generate random encounters fitting the atmosphere
- Track how player choices shift between moods
- Use mood transitions as session recap/opening hooks

## 2. Dialogue Bingo Card (Roleplaying Incentive)

### What it is
A player handout with phrases aligned to different philosophical viewpoints:

#### Lawful Good Examples
- *"Justice must be served, but with mercy."*
- *"The law exists to protect the innocent."*
- *"We must do what is right, not what is easy."*

#### Chaotic Good Examples  
- *"Sometimes you have to break unjust laws."*
- *"The people matter more than the system."*
- *"Freedom is worth fighting for."*

#### Lawful Neutral Examples
- *"Order must be maintained at all costs."*
- *"The procedure exists for a reason."*
- *"Stability benefits everyone."*

#### Neutral/Pragmatic Examples
- *"What's the practical solution here?"*
- *"Every choice has consequences."*
- *"Sometimes compromise is necessary."*

### How it works
- **Single Use:** Player uses a phrase well → **Small bonus** (Inspiration, +1 to a Faction Track)
- **Bingo! (Complete a row/column)** → **Potent Favor** (free healing, key information, temporary immunity from that faction)


### Goal
Encourages players to engage with the campaign's philosophical themes through roleplay while providing mechanical benefits.

## 3. Faction Leader Quest Chains

### What it is
Each faction leader has a unique **3-step personal quest** that runs parallel to the main story.

#### Ashka's Quest Chain Example
1. **Secure a Smith** - Convince Borin to join the revolution
2. **Rally the Workers** - Unite the guilds under revolutionary banner  
3. **Arm the People** - Establish weapon caches throughout the Cobbleward

#### Elara's Quest Chain Example
1. **The Church's Ear** - Gain audience with the High Priestess
2. **Noble Backing** - Convince key lords to support reform
3. **Legal Precedent** - Discover ancient laws supporting change

#### Brennis's Quest Chain Example
1. **Veteran Loyalty** - Recruit retired guard captains
2. **Intelligence Network** - Establish informants in all districts
3. **Military Precision** - Organize the guard into elite units

### How it works
- **Dual Objectives:** Missions have a **Main Goal** (affects Progress Tracks) + **Bonus Objective** (advances Leader Quest)
- **Empowerment:** Completing all 3 steps before the Mandate is revealed **"empowers"** the leader
- **Final Siege Benefits:** Empowered leaders gain powerful abilities:
  - **Ashka** can heal allies with revolutionary fervor
  - **Elara** grants divine protection to the righteous
  - **Brennis** boosts ally coordination and tactics

### Goal
Provides long-term personal investment in faction leaders alongside the main political story.

## 4. The Faction Turn

### What it is
A **narrative tool** to make the city feel alive and reactive to player actions.

### How it works
At the **start of each session**, the DM briefly narrates what the **two other factions** have been doing off-screen, adjusting their Progress Tracks accordingly.

### Example Faction Turns

**Revolutionary Activity:**
> *"While you were investigating the tax records, Ashka's rebels successfully raided a guard post, freeing several arrested protesters. (+1 Revolutionary Zeal)"*

**Guard Response:**
> *"In response to the raid, Captain Brennis tightened curfews and doubled patrols in the Cobbleward. (+1 City Control)"*

**Political Maneuvering:**
> *"Ser Elara used the chaos to meet privately with several guild leaders, gaining their cautious support for reform. (+1 Coalition Strength)"*

### Benefits
- **Living World:** City reacts to PC actions
- **Pacing Control:** Keeps all factions relevant
- **Narrative Flow:** Seamlessly advances background conflicts

## 5. The Ultimate Choice & The Council Vote

### The Mandate Showdown
**When:** End of Act II  
**Decision:** Players must definitively choose which faction to help in the **three-way battle** for the Crown-Regent's Mandate  
**Stakes:** This choice cements their final allegiance and determines which philosophy they believe should triumph

### The Epilogue Vote
**When:** After the final battle  
**Power:** The players become the **ultimate arbiters** of Estellon's future  
**Decisions:**
1. **The fate of Duke Valerius** - Exile, Trial, Pardon, or Execution
2. **The form of the new government** - Reformed Dukedom, Military Stewardship, People's Council, or Free State

### 🎯 Goal
Makes the players' choices the **driving force** behind the campaign's conclusion, ensuring their actions throughout the entire campaign matter in the final outcome.

### Player Agency
- **Every major choice** has been tracked and remembered
- **Faction relationships** influence the final council
- **Personal bonds** with leaders affect available options
- **The ending** is entirely determined by player values and decisions

## Design Philosophy

This framework transforms abstract political concepts into **concrete, playable mechanics**:

- **📊 Progress Tracks** make ideological conflict measurable
- **🎭 Roleplay incentives** encourage thematic engagement  
- **⭐ Personal quests** create investment in faction leaders
- **🌍 Living world systems** make consequences feel real
- **🗳️ Player agency** ensures choices drive the narrative

***The result: Political intrigue becomes as engaging and tactical as any dungeon crawl.***

## Character Hooks System

### Overview
When creating your character, you choose a **Background Hook** - your reason for being in Estellon during the crisis. This choice is more than flavor text; it grants:
1. **Special Dialogue Access** - Specific NPCs and conversation topics available to you
2. **Behavior Rewards** - Mechanical bonuses when playing to your hook's themes

### How It Works

#### Special Dialogue Access
Your hook determines who recognizes you and what conversations you can initiate:
- **Example:** A "Festival Trader" Merchant can discuss luxury goods with nobles naturally
- **Example:** A "War Profiteer" Merchant can openly talk military supply with officers
- **Benefit:** Opens roleplay opportunities that would otherwise seem forced or suspicious

#### Behavior Rewards
When you act in ways that align with your hook, you receive bonuses:
- **Passive Bonuses:** Always-active small benefits (+1 to specific checks)
- **Active Rewards:** Once-per-session special abilities
- **Conditional Benefits:** Bonuses in specific situations or City Moods

### Design Philosophy
Your hook should:
- **Feel Distinct:** Each option plays differently
- **Reward Roleplay:** Acting true to your hook grants mechanical benefits
- **Open Opportunities:** Give you unique access other hooks don't have
- **Matter Mechanically:** Not just flavor - real gameplay impact

### Example: Merchant Hooks

**Festival Trader** benefits from peaceful times and social gatherings
**Guild Representative** leverages institutional authority and networks
**Independent Entrepreneur** thrives on risk-taking and outsider status
**War Profiteer** profits from chaos and has no moral restrictions

Each hook changes how you experience the same role!
