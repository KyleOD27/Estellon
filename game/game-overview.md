### Gameplay Modes
**📖 [Complete Gameplay Modes Framework](gameplay-modes.md)**

Learn how to structure every session using three distinct modes:

- **🎬 SCENE Mode** - Narrative moments where PCs observe story unfold
- **⚔️ ENCOUNTER Mode** - Active gameplay with rolls, combat, or social challenges
- **🗺️ FREE-ROAM Mode** - Open exploration where players have agency

**Why This Matters:** Provides clear pacing, player expectations, and smooth transitions between narrative and gameplay.

### Progress Tracks (The Faction War)

Three scores (0-10) that measure the city's balance of power:

#### Revolutionary Zeal (Ashka's Rebellion)
- **Measures:** Public support and revolutionary fervor
- **High Score Effects:** Riots, civil disobedience, underground networks grow
- **Leader:** Ashka the Flameheart (Wild Magic Sorcerer)

#### City Control (Brennis's Guard)
- **Measures:** Order, fear, and authoritarian grip
- **High Score Effects:** Curfews, checkpoints, oppressive patrols
- **Leader:** Captain Brennis (Fighter, Lawful Neutral)

#### Coalition Strength (Elara's Reformers)
- **Measures:** Political and institutional support
- **High Score Effects:** Legal challenges, noble backing, church support
- **Leader:** Ser Elara Brightshield (Paladin, Lawful Good)

**How It Works:**
- Mission success/failure modifies tracks (+1 to +3 typically)
- **Critical Threshold:** When any track hits **7**, the Crown-Regent's Mandate is revealed (triggers Act II climax)
- Current scores dictate City Mood, random encounters, and NPC attitudes

### Titles & Abilities System

**📖 [Complete Titles & Abilities](titles-and-abilities.md)**
**📖 [Mission-Title Integration](mission-title-integration.md)**

Players earn **Titles** through mission accomplishments, which unlock special abilities and alternative mission approaches.

**Title Progression:** Bronze → Silver → Gold

**Categories:**
- **Combat Titles:** Breacher, Vanguard, Tactician, Duelist
- **Social Titles:** Orator, Diplomat, Investigator, Agitator
- **Faction Titles:** Revolutionary Icon, Lawbringer, Reform Champion

**Example - Breacher Title:**
- **Bronze:** +2 Stealth, advantage on Thieves' Tools (earn: complete infiltration mission)
- **Silver:** "Silent Takedown" ability (earn: 3 infiltrations with zero alarms)
- **Gold:** "Breach Master" - team gains +5 initiative (earn: Ironhold Prison Break legendary success)

**Why This Matters:** Titles create long-term progression beyond levels and encourage creative mission approaches.

### Faction Leader Quest Chains

Each faction leader has a **3-step personal quest** running parallel to the main story.

#### Ashka's Quest Chain (Revolutionary Path)
1. **Secure a Smith** - Convince Borin to join (Mission K1)
2. **Rally the Workers** - Unite the guilds (Mission K3)
3. **Arm the People** - Establish weapon caches (Mission K5)
- **Empowerment:** Ashka gains "Arsenal Master" - summon 2d6 armed citizens (1/day) in Act III

#### Elara's Quest Chain (Reform Path)
1. **The Church's Ear** - Gain High Priestess's support (Mission E1)
2. **Noble Backing** - Convince five noble houses (Mission E3)
3. **Legal Precedent** - Find the Charter of Common Rights (Mission E4)
- **Empowerment:** Elara gains "Righteous Authority" - command NPCs to stand down (1/session) in Act III

#### Brennis's Quest Chain (Order Path)
1. **Veteran Loyalty** - Recruit retired captains (Mission B2)
2. **Intelligence Network** - Establish informants everywhere (Mission B3)
3. **Military Precision** - Organize elite guard units (Mission B6)
- **Empowerment:** Brennis gains "Tactical Commander" - devastating combined unit tactics (1/session) in Act III

**Mission Format:** Each mission has **Main Objective** (affects Progress Tracks) + **Bonus Objective** (advances Leader Quest)

## 🗺️ Campaign Structure

### Act I: The Sunstone Festival
**📖 [Complete Act I](../story/act%20i/act-i.md)**

**Purpose:** Introduction, inciting incident, faction choice

**Structure:**
- **Part 1:** The Festival Morning - Character introduction, world-building

- **Part 3:** The Festival Riot - First major choice (which faction to aid?)

---

### Act II: The Faction War
**📖 [Complete Act II Mission Catalogue](act-ii-missions.md)**

**Duration:** 8-15 sessions
**Purpose:** Faction missions, Progress Track manipulation, Leader Quest Chains

**Structure:** 26 missions across three factions:
- **Ashka (K1-K8):** Revolutionary missions - sabotage, recruitment, arming the people
- **Elara (E1-E8):** Reform missions - investigation, diplomacy, legal maneuvering
- **Brennis (B1-B8):** Order missions - suppression, intelligence, military organization
- **Neutral (N1-N2):** Independent missions available to all factions

**Gameplay:**
- PCs choose which faction missions to accept (or try to balance multiple factions)
- Each mission affects Progress Tracks
- Leader Quest Chain bonus objectives run parallel
- City Mood shifts based on faction scores
- When any track hits **7**, Crown-Regent's Mandate is revealed → Act III begins

**Mission Format:**
- 🗺️ FREE-ROAM: Preparation and intelligence gathering
- 🎬 SCENE: Narrative setup and stakes
- ⚔️ ENCOUNTER: Active gameplay (combat, social, infiltration)
- 🗺️ FREE-ROAM: Aftermath and consequences

---

### Act III: The Siege of Estellon
**📖 [Complete Act III](../story/act%20iii/act-iii.md)**

**Duration:** 3-5 sessions
**Purpose:** Climactic battle, villain confrontation, ultimate choice

**Structure:**

**Part 1: The Two-Front War**
- **Front 1: Battle of the Walls** - Duke Theron's army siege (12-round combat)
- **Front 2: Battle of the Streets** - Gorok's bandit infiltration (10-round urban warfare)

**Part 2: The Surgical Strike**
- **Boss Fight 1: Gorok the Hillbreaker** - Brutal melee barbarian king
- **Boss Fight 2: Lord Pellor** - Tactical enchantment wizard (TRUE VILLAIN reveal)

**Key Revelations:**
- Lord Pellor orchestrated the entire crisis to seize power
- PCs discover his treachery and must stop both external and internal threats
- The Crown-Regent's Mandate allows legal regime change

**Outcome:** City saved, villain defeated, PCs become arbiters of Estellon's future.

---

### Epilogue: The Council Vote
**📖 [Complete Epilogue](../story/epilogue.md)**

**Duration:** 1 session
**Purpose:** Players decide Estellon's future

**Player Decisions:**
1. **Fate of Duke Valerius** - Exile, Trial, Pardon, or Execution?
2. **Form of New Government** - Reformed Dukedom, Military Stewardship, People's Council, or Free State?

**Influenced By:**
- Faction relationships throughout campaign
- Which Leader Quest Chains were completed
- Personal bonds with faction leaders
- Titles earned and reputation built

**Multiple Endings:** Based entirely on player choices and values demonstrated throughout the campaign.

### Session Start Routine

**Opening Every Session:**

1. **Faction Turn** - Narrate what other factions did off-screen (1-2 minutes)
2. **City Mood Update** - Describe current atmosphere based on faction scores (1 minute)
3. **Progress Track Reminder** - Show current scores, remind of stakes (30 seconds)
4. **Last Time Recap** - Quick summary of previous session (2 minutes)
5. **Scene Opening** - Drop into first scene with mode tag (🎬 SCENE or 🗺️ FREE-ROAM)

**During Session:**
- Track mission outcomes and Progress Track changes
- Note which dialogue bingo phrases used
- Record NPC interactions for continuity
- Track Title earning progress

**Session End:**
- Update Progress Tracks
- Calculate new City Mood for next session
- Preview next session's likely content
- Award any earned Titles

**Social Checks:**
- **Easy (DC 10):** Convincing someone who already agrees
- **Medium (DC 13):** Standard persuasion/deception

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "My Players Won't Pick a Faction"

**Solution:** That's valid! Some parties want to stay neutral or play all sides. Options:
- Focus on Neutral missions (N1-N2)
- Let them cherry-pick missions from each faction
- Increase consequences - neutrality becomes harder as city destabilizes
- Use City Mood "Powder Keg" or "Chaos Reigns" to force choices

### "One Faction Track Is Too High/Low"

**Solution:** Use Faction Turns to balance:
- If one faction dominates, have other two temporarily ally
- Add missions that specifically counter the dominant faction
- Let natural consequences play out - high Control causes Revolutionary Zeal rise

### "Players Murdered an Important NPC"

**Solution:** Roll with it! The campaign is flexible:
- Promote a lieutenant (if guard/revolutionary killed)
- Have faction splinter (new leader with different approach)
- Make it a major plot point (assassination triggers escalation)
- Continue without them (empowerment unavailable, but not campaign-breaking)

### "They're Solving Everything Without Combat"

**Solution:** That's amazing! Reward creative problem-solving:
- Give bonus faction points for non-violent solutions
- Unlock special dialogue options in future missions
- Let them feel powerful - sometimes diplomacy IS the answer
- Save combat for Act III when stakes are highest

### "Act II Is Taking Forever"

**Solution:** Adjust pacing:
- Run fewer missions (6-8 instead of 15+)
- Fast-forward through some missions (narrative summary + track changes)
- Advance faction scores via Faction Turns to reach threshold 7 faster
- Skip to Act III if players are getting restless

## City Mood System (Detailed) <a name="city-mood-system-detailed"></a>

### Overview
The **City Mood** represents Estellon's overall atmosphere based on the relative strength of faction scores. Calculate it at the start of each session to inform narration, encounters, and NPC behavior.

### How to Calculate City Mood

#### Step 1: Identify the Dominant Faction(s)
- **Clear Dominance:** One faction is 3+ points higher than all others
- **Contested Leadership:** Two factions within 2 points of each other (both high)
- **Balanced/Stalemate:** All three factions within 2 points of each other
- **Power Vacuum:** All factions below 4

#### Step 2: Check Overall Tension Level
Add all three faction scores together:
- **0-9:** Low Tension (Early conflict)
- **10-17:** Rising Tension (Escalating crisis)
- **18-24:** High Tension (Boiling point)
- **25+:** Critical Mass (City on edge of explosion)

#### Step 3: Determine City Mood

### City Mood States

#### 1. **FRAGILE PEACE**
**Conditions:** All factions 0-3, Total < 10
- **Atmosphere:** Calm before the storm; people sense something brewing
- **Street Life:** Normal activities, but whispered concerns in taverns
- **Guard Presence:** Standard patrols, relaxed posture
- **Encounters:** Merchants worried about future, anxious nobles, quiet tension
- **Mechanical Effects:** +1 to social checks; information flows freely

---

#### 2. **REVOLUTIONARY FERVOR**
**Conditions:** Revolutionary Zeal 5+, Zeal leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Defiant energy, underground meetings, graffiti everywhere
- **Street Life:** Protests, rallies, workers organizing openly
- **Guard Presence:** Nervous patrols, avoid Cobbleward at night
- **Encounters:** Revolutionaries recruiting, guard crackdowns, secret supply lines
- **Mechanical Effects:** +2 to recruiting common folk; -2 to noble audiences; random protests

**Sub-Mood - Revolutionary Uprising (Zeal 7+):**
- Open riots, barricades in poor districts
- Guards only patrol in force
- +1 Revolutionary Zeal per session automatically

---

#### 3. **MARTIAL LAW**
**Conditions:** City Control 5+, Control leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Oppressive fear, checkpoints everywhere, informants watching
- **Street Life:** Curfews enforced, people hurry home, streets empty at night
- **Guard Presence:** Heavy patrols, suspicious of everyone, quick to arrest
- **Encounters:** Random stop-and-searches, informant accusations, public punishments
- **Mechanical Effects:** -2 to Stealth in open; +2 to Intimidation by authority; curfew restrictions

**Sub-Mood - Iron Fist (Control 7+):**
- Brutal crackdowns, mass arrests
- Fear dominates all social interaction
- +1 City Control per session automatically

---

#### 4. **HOPEFUL REFORM**
**Conditions:** Coalition Strength 5+, Coalition leads by 3+
- **Atmosphere:** Cautious optimism, public debates, intellectual energy
- **Street Life:** Political discussions in squares, pamphlets everywhere, hope in air
- **Guard Presence:** Moderate, trying to appear reasonable
- **Encounters:** Town hall meetings, petition drives, noble reformers speaking publicly
- **Mechanical Effects:** +2 to Persuasion in debates; +1 to gathering noble support; peaceful rallies

**Sub-Mood - Peaceful Transition (Coalition 7+):**
- Major reforms being discussed openly
- System changing from within
- +1 Coalition Strength per session automatically

---

#### 5. **POWDER KEG**
**Conditions:** Two factions at 6+, within 2 points of each other
- **Atmosphere:** Extreme tension, everyone choosing sides, violence imminent
- **Street Life:** Divided city, rival rallies, street fights between factions
- **Guard Presence:** Overwhelmed, picking sides themselves
- **Encounters:** Faction confrontations, forced loyalty checks, targeted violence
- **Mechanical Effects:** All social DCs increase by 2; violence erupts randomly; factions demand commitment

**Variations:**
- **Zeal vs Control:** Street battles between rebels and guards
- **Zeal vs Coalition:** Radicals vs moderates, ideology war
- **Control vs Coalition:** Authority vs reform, institutional crisis

---

#### 6. **CHAOS REIGNS**
**Conditions:** All three factions 5+, within 3 points of each other
- **Atmosphere:** Complete breakdown of order, three-way conflict, city paralyzed

### Using City Mood in Play

#### Narrative Application
- **Opening Description:** Start each session with City Mood atmosphere
- **NPC Reactions:** Filter all social interactions through current mood
- **Random Encounters:** Use mood to determine what players encounter on streets
- **Escalation:** Show how player actions shift the mood session to session

#### Example Narration
**Fragile Peace → Revolutionary Fervor:**
> *"When you arrived in Estellon two weeks ago, the Sunstone Festival felt like a celebration. Now, as you walk through the Cobbleward, revolutionary graffiti covers every wall, workers gather in angry crowds, and the air crackles with defiant energy. The peace was an illusion—the revolution has begun."*

**Hopeful Reform → Powder Keg:**
> *"Last session, nobles debated reform in comfortable parlors. Today, a revolutionary march collided with a guard checkpoint, and blood stains the cobblestones. The moderates' moment has passed—now, only force remains."*

#### Mechanical Integration
- Adjust social DCs based on mood
- Generate random encounters fitting the atmosphere
- Track how player choices shift between moods
- Use mood transitions as session recap/opening hooks
