# Player Setup Guide

> *"Creating your character for The Weight of the Crown - A step-by-step journey from concept to introduction"*

---

## Overview

This guide walks you through creating your character for the campaign. Take your time with each step - your choices shape not just your character's mechanics, but how you'll experience Estellon's political crisis.

**Estimated Time:** 60-90 minutes
**Materials Needed:** Character sheet (or tableau board + cards), pencil, paper for notes/drawing

## Step 4: Define Your Intent (10 minutes)

### What Is Intent?
Your **Intent** is your character's primary goal in Estellon - what you're secretly trying to accomplish during the crisis. This is separate from which faction you support (you haven't decided that yet).

### Categories of Intent
Choose one category, then specify:

#### **Personal Gain**
- Accumulate wealth during the chaos
- Build reputation and fame
- Establish business or power base
- Example: *"I want to become the richest merchant in Estellon by controlling supply chains."*

#### **Ideological Mission**
- Prove a philosophical point
- Advance a cause or belief
- Convert others to your worldview
- Example: *"I want to prove that reform works better than revolution."*

#### **Personal Quest**
- Find someone or something
- Achieve redemption or revenge
- Discover truth about your past
- Example: *"I'm searching for my sister who disappeared in the city years ago."*

#### **Protect or Preserve**
- Keep someone/something safe
- Prevent specific outcomes
- Maintain stability for those you love
- Example: *"I must keep the refugee community safe, no matter which faction wins."*

#### **Transformation**
- Become someone different
- Overcome a flaw or fear
- Prove yourself to someone
- Example: *"I need to prove I'm more than just my noble title."*

#### **Witness and Record**
- Document events for history
- Gather information for others
- Understand what's truly happening
- Example: *"I'm here to witness this moment for the academy's historical records."*

## Step 9: Group Introduction Session (20 minutes total)

### Setup
- All players gather with completed characters
- Each player gets 3-4 minutes to present
- Other players can ask clarifying questions

### Your Presentation (3-4 minutes)

#### Part 1: Show Your Character (1 minute)
- Show your drawing/description
- Display character sheet or tableau board
- Point out key visual elements

#### Part 2: Read Race and Role (1 minute)
**Read aloud to the group:**
- "I'm playing a [RACE] [ROLE]"
- Share 2-3 key passive abilities or role abilities
- Explain one unique thing about your race's standing in Estellon

**Example:**
> "I'm playing an Orc Artisan. As an orc, I face prejudice from nobles but I'm respected by workers. My passive abilities include Hardy - I'm resistant to poison and disease - and Orcish Fury - when I'm wounded, I fight harder. As an artisan, I can use Master Craftsman once per session to create or repair something important."

#### Part 3: Present Your Hook (30 seconds)
- Name your hook
- Share one interesting dialogue opportunity it grants
- Mention one behavior reward

**Example:**
> "My hook is Guild Business. I represent my craft guild's interests, which means I can invoke guild authority with other workers and call on guild members for help once per session."

#### Part 4: Give Your Introduction (1 minute)
- Deliver your prepared introduction from Step 8
- Include your character's arrival, first impression, and stated motivation
- **State your intent (truthfully or falsely, as you decided)**

**Example:**
> "I arrived with the festival caravan, my wagon loaded with masterwork tools. I'm here to showcase my skills and establish my guild's presence in Estellon. When you meet me, you'll notice my calloused hands and the proud way I wear my guild seal. My intent is to ensure workers are treated fairly in whatever government emerges from this crisis." *(This could be true or a cover story!)*

#### Part 5: Questions from Other Players (30 seconds)
Other players can ask 1-2 brief questions:
- "Where are you staying?"
- "Have you taken sides yet?"
- "What's your most prized possession?"

**You can answer honestly or deceptively - your choice!**

## Tips for Success

### Take Your Time
- Don't rush through character creation
- Read everything thoroughly
- Ask questions if anything is unclear

### Think About Roleplay
- Choose options that excite you to roleplay
- Consider how your character talks, moves, thinks
- Imagine scenes with your character

### Embrace Uncertainty
- You don't need to know everything about your character yet
- Discovery through play is part of the fun
- Your character will evolve

### Be Honest About Intent (Internally)
- Even if you lie to the group, **you** must know your true intent
- Write it down somewhere private if needed
- Revisit it periodically to stay true to your character

### Collaborate
- Listen to other players' introductions
- Build on their ideas
- Look for natural connections

### Trust the Process
- This setup creates rich, complex characters
- Every choice has gameplay implications
- Your character matters to the story
