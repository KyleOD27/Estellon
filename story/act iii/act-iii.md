# Act III: The Siege of Estellon

**Goal:** To force the fractured factions into a desperate alliance to save the city from annihilation, uncover the deepest layer of corruption, and decide the ultimate fate of Estellon.

---

## The State of the City

Estellon is under full-scale assault. The internal conflict has left its gates weakened and its defenders divided. Panic reigns in the streets.

---

## Part 1: Army at the Gates (The Two-Front War)

The city is assaulted from both outside and within.

---

## FRONT 1: THE WALLS (THE SHIELD)

### 🎬 SCENE: The Enemy Approaches
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Purpose:** Establish scale of threat, show army, build tension

*"Dawn breaks red over Estellon. From the eastern ramparts, you see them: Duke Theron's army, five hundred strong, marching in perfect formation. Trebuchets roll forward, siege towers loom like wooden giants. The ground trembles with the march of boots. Captain Brennis stands beside you, jaw clenched. 'This is it,' he says quietly. 'Today we hold, or Estellon falls.' Ser Elara places a hand on your shoulder. 'We fight for every soul in this city.' The first trebuchet stone arcs through the sky."*

**Visual Details:**
- 500 soldiers in disciplined ranks (terrifying sight)
- 3 trebuchets being loaded with massive stones
- 2 siege towers advancing slowly (40 feet tall)
- Knight-Captain Aldric on horseback, armor gleaming
- Defenders on wall look frightened but determined

**Stakes:**
- If the wall falls, the city is overrun
- Thousands of civilians shelter behind these walls
- This is the culmination of all faction conflicts

**Transition Signal:**
DM: "The first trebuchet fires. Roll initiative."

---

### Front 1: The Walls (The Shield)
Duke Theron's professional, disciplined army lays siege with trebuchets and siege towers. Their goal is conquest.

- **Commanded by:** Brennis and Elara. This is a battle of formations, discipline, and holding the line.
- **PC Role:** Players aligned with them will be on the walls, repelling scaling ladders, manning ballistae, and dueling enemy officers.

---

## ⚔️ ENCOUNTER: BATTLE OF THE WALLS
**Encounter Type:** Large-Scale Combat Defense

**Duration:** 12 rounds of combat + multiple objectives

**Setup:**
Duke Theron's 500-strong army assaults Estellon's eastern wall. The city's defenders are outnumbered 3-to-1. PCs must hold critical sections of the wall, destroy siege weapons, and prevent the enemy from breaching the gates.

- Abilities: Second Wind, Action Surge, Indomitable (2/day), Tactical Commander

- Commands: South Tower section

**Ser Elara Brightshield** (If alive and allied - Human Paladin 7)
- AC 19 (plate + shield + Shield of Faith)
- HP 68
- Melee: Longsword +8 (1d8+4 slashing + 1d8 radiant with Divine Smite)
- Abilities: Lay on Hands (35 HP pool), Divine Smite, Aura of Protection (+3 to all saves within 10 ft)
- Spells: Bless, Shield of Faith, Aid, Lesser Restoration
- **Righteous Authority:** Can command enemy soldiers to stand down (Wisdom save DC 15, 1/day)
- Commands: North Tower section
- Prioritizes protecting civilians and wounded

**City Guard Veterans** (20x Guards - split across wall sections)
- AC 16 (chain shirt, shield)
- HP 11 each
- Melee: Spear +3 (1d6+1 piercing)
- Ranged: Light crossbow +3 (1d8+1 piercing) 80/320
- Morale: Steady while leaders alive; shaken if leader falls (disadvantage on attacks)

**Militia Defenders** (30x Commoners)
- AC 12 (leather armor)
- HP 4 each
- Ranged: Bow +2 (1d6 piercing) 80/320
- Morale: Fragile; flee if wall breached or 50% casualties

**War Machines:**
- **2x Ballistae** (AC 15, HP 30 each) - +6 to hit, 3d10 piercing, 120/480 ft range, requires 2 crew
- **1x Catapult** (AC 15, HP 40) - Lobbed shot, DC 15 Dex save in 10-ft radius or 3d10 bludgeoning

### Attacking Forces (Duke Theron's Army)

**Duke Theron's Commander - Knight-Captain Aldric** (Human Fighter 6)
- AC 18 (plate armor)
- HP 65
- Melee: Greatsword +8 (2d6+4 slashing), Extra Attack
- Leads from siege tower
- **Rally the Assault:** Bonus action, all soldiers within 30 ft gain advantage on next attack (recharge 5-6)
- Will retreat if reduced below 20 HP to preserve his life

**Siege Engineers** (4x Skilled Soldiers)
- AC 14 (leather armor)
- HP 16 each
- Operate trebuchets and siege towers
- Non-combatants; will surrender if directly threatened

**Assault Infantry** (40x Soldiers - CR 1/2 each, deployed in waves)
- AC 16 (chain mail, shield)
- HP 16 each
- Melee: Longsword +4 (1d8+2 slashing)
- Tactics: Climb siege ladders, swarm defenders
- Morale: Break if Knight-Captain Aldric falls

**Climbing Assassins** (6x Rogues - CR 2 each)
- AC 14 (leather armor)
- HP 22 each
- Melee: Shortsword +5 (1d6+3 piercing + 2d6 sneak attack)
- Tactics: Climb walls independently, assassinate leaders, sabotage ballistae
- Equipment: Grappling hooks, smoke bombs

**Siege Equipment:**
- **3x Trebuchets** (AC 15, HP 50 each, 300 ft away) - Lob stones at wall, 4d10 bludgeoning to 10-ft area, DC 15 Dex save for half
- **2x Siege Towers** (AC 15, HP 80 each, advancing 30 ft/round) - Carries 20 soldiers each, allows them to assault wall when adjacent

### Combat Objectives

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: Hold the Wall**
- Prevent enemy from controlling any wall section for 3+ consecutive rounds
- Keep gate secure (AC 19, HP 150; if destroyed, enemy floods courtyard)
- Survive 12 rounds until reinforcements arrive from other city sections

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
1. **Destroy Trebuchets** - Each destroyed prevents 1d4 wall breaches
2. **Collapse Siege Towers** - Each destroyed before reaching wall saves 20 defenders from melee
3. **Kill Knight-Captain Aldric** - Breaks enemy morale, assault weakens
4. **Protect Brennis/Elara** - Bonus to defender morale if both leaders survive

**BONUS OBJECTIVES:**
1. **Zero Civilian Casualties** - Evacuate civilians from wall section before bombardment
2. **Capture Knight-Captain Aldric Alive** - Intelligence and possible parley with Duke Theron
3. **Destroy All 3 Trebuchets** - Legendary defense, prevents future sieges
4. **Repel Assault with <10 Defender Deaths** - Masterful tactics, Brennis gains renown

### Combat Phases

#### Phase 1: The Bombardment (Rounds 1-3)
**Enemy Actions:**
- 3 trebuchets fire at wall (1d4 hits per round)
- Each hit: 4d10 bludgeoning damage to 10-ft area, DC 15 Dex save for half
- Wall sections take structural damage (track: 0/150 damage = intact, 150+ = breach)

**Defender Actions:**
- PCs can man ballistae (action to aim + attack roll)
- Attempt to destroy trebuchets at range (300 ft, AC 15, HP 50 each)
- Organize defense, move civilians to safety
- Cast defensive spells (Shield of Faith on wall sections, Bless on defenders)

**Event:** Round 3 - Siege towers reach 120 ft (halfway to wall)

#### Phase 2: The Assault (Rounds 4-8)
**Enemy Actions:**
- Siege towers reach wall (Round 6)
- 40 soldiers deploy in waves: 10 per round from towers + ladders
- 6 climbing assassins scale wall independently (Stealth +5, DC 15 Perception to spot)
- Trebuchets continue bombardment (targeting gate now)

**Defender Actions:**
- **Melee Combat:** Repel soldiers climbing ladders/towers
  - Soldiers appear at wall top in groups of 5-10
  - Defenders have high ground advantage (+2 to hit)
- **Siege Tower Targets:** PCs can target towers (AC 15, HP 80, 30 ft away when adjacent)
  - Fire arrows (damage/round can ignite: DC 13, 2d6 fire damage/round)
  - Catapult (direct hit collapses tower)
- **Assassin Threat:** Rogues attempt to kill Brennis/Elara
  - DC 15 Perception to spot before they strike
  - Surprise attack if undetected (auto-crit)

**Event:** Round 6 - Gate takes 75 damage (50% integrity), begins to splinter

#### Phase 3: The Desperate Stand (Rounds 9-12)
**Enemy Actions:**
- Knight-Captain Aldric personally leads final assault (climbs siege tower, Round 9)
- All remaining soldiers swarm wall (20+ soldiers engage simultaneously)
- Assassins attempt to sabotage ballistae if leaders still alive
- Trebuchets target wall foundations (attempting full breach)

**Defender Actions:**
- **Hold Chokepoints:** Brennis and Elara each hold a tower (defenders rally around them)
- **Elite Duel:** PCs can challenge Knight-Captain Aldric (honor duel = morale boost)
- **Emergency Repairs:** DC 16 Athletics/Carpenter's Tools to shore up gate (requires 2 rounds, restores 50 HP to gate)
- **Last Stand:** If wall breached, fall back to courtyard and fight defensively

**Event - Round 10:** Gorok's infiltration discovered (messenger arrives from city interior)
- Brennis/Elara must decide: Hold wall or send reinforcements to streets?
- PC CHOICE: Stay on wall OR go to Front 2 (streets)

**Event - Round 12:** Reinforcements arrive from western wall (30 fresh guards)
- Enemy morale check: If Knight-Captain alive and siege towers intact, assault continues (4 more rounds)
- If Knight-Captain dead OR both towers destroyed, enemy retreats

### Success Metrics

**VICTORY CONDITIONS:**
- **Decisive Victory:** Wall held, <10 defender deaths, 2+ siege towers destroyed, Knight-Captain dead/captured
  - Enemy retreats fully, no further siege attempts
  - Brennis/Elara recognized as war heroes

- **Standard Victory:** Wall held, <30 defender deaths, gate intact
  - Enemy retreats but regroups for second assault next day
  - Time to address Front 2 (streets)

- **Costly Victory:** Wall held but heavy casualties (30+ deaths) OR gate destroyed
  - Enemy retreats temporarily
  - Defenders weakened, vulnerable to second assault

**DEFEAT CONDITIONS:**
- **Wall Breached:** Enemy controls wall for 3+ rounds
  - Combat moves to courtyard (defender disadvantage)
  - City population at risk

- **Gate Destroyed + Brennis/Elara Dead:** Total collapse
  - Enemy floods city
  - Emergency retreat to palace district

### Tactical Considerations

**High Ground Advantage:**
- Defenders on wall have +2 to ranged attacks
- Enemies climbing have disadvantage on attacks (using hands to climb)

**Siege Ladder Mechanics:**
- Each ladder brings 5 soldiers per round
- PCs can push ladders away (DC 13 Str check, action)
- Destroy ladder: AC 12, HP 20, prevents 5 soldiers/round

**Ballista/Catapult Priority Targets:**
1. Siege towers (prevent 20 soldiers from engaging)
2. Trebuchets (prevent wall damage)
3. Knight-Captain Aldric (break morale)

**Spell Synergies:**
- Wall of Fire across siege tower ramp (prevents deployment)
- Grease on wall top (enemies fall, 2d6 damage from 40-ft fall)
- Thunderwave to push multiple soldiers off wall
- Healing Word/Mass Healing to keep defenders alive

**Environmental Hazards:**
- **Burning Oil:** Can be poured on climbers (2d6 fire, DC 13 Dex save)
- **Falling Debris:** Trebuchet impacts may knock PCs prone (DC 12 Dex save)
- **Wall Collapse:** If section takes 150+ damage, DC 15 Dex save or fall 40 ft (4d6 damage)

### NPC Actions & Roleplay

**Brennis (if present):**
- Shouts tactical orders: "Reinforcements to South Tower!"
- Personal combat: Duels enemy officers who reach wall
- If gate threatened: "The gate must hold! No retreat!"
- If Elara wounded: Shows rare concern, orders her to safety

**Elara (if present):**
- Uses Lay on Hands to heal wounded defenders
- Casts Bless on key defenders before assault
- Attempts to parley with Knight-Captain (if captured): "Your Duke sends you to die for his ambition"
- If civilians endangered: Prioritizes their safety over tactical advantage

**Knight-Captain Aldric:**
- Honorable warrior, respects courage
- If challenged to duel: Accepts (sees it as chance for glory)
- If defeated honorably: Surrenders, provides intel on Duke Theron
- If treated poorly: Defiant, "Duke Theron will avenge this dishonor"

**Messenger (Round 10):**
- Arrives bloodied, panicked: "The streets burn! Bandits are inside the city! Ashka calls for aid!"
- Brennis: "We cannot abandon the wall!"
- Elara: "We must trust the people to defend their homes."

### Rewards & Consequences

**If Victory:**
- Reputation: +3 City Defense (Brennis's respect, even if previously opposed)
- Loot: Knight-Captain's Masterwork Greatsword (+1), siege equipment salvage (ballistae bolts, oil)
- Intelligence: If Aldric captured, learn Duke Theron's deal with Lord Pellor

**If Defeat:**
- Enemy occupies outer districts
- Forced to defend palace district in final stand (harder encounter)
- Civilian casualties high (affects epilogue choices)

## FRONT 2: THE STREETS (THE SPEAR)

### 🎬 SCENE: The City Burns
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Purpose:** Show chaos, establish urban warfare stakes

*"You descend from the wall into nightmare. Cobbleward burns. Civilians flee screaming through smoke-filled streets. Bandits chase them with torches and blades. In Market Square, 150 people huddle in terror as raiders close in. Ashka stands defiant in the center of the chaos, fire dancing in her hands. 'About time!' she shouts over the roar of flames. 'Pellor betrayed us - opened the cistern tunnels. Gorok's horde is everywhere. We need to save what we can - which districts do we defend?'"*

**Visual Details:**
- Buildings ablaze, smoke choking the air
- Bandits looting shops, civilians fleeing
- Three distinct threat areas: Cobbleward, Trade District, Market Square
- Ashka's revolutionaries fighting desperately, outnumbered
- Children crying, families separated in chaos

**Stakes:**
- 150 civilians in Market Square will be massacred if not protected
- Grain stores burning = city starves
- Temple District sanctuary = wounded defenders have nowhere to go
- This is guerrilla warfare - different from wall defense

**Transition Signal:**
DM: "A bandit lieutenant spots you. 'Fresh meat!' he roars, charging with twenty raiders at his back. Roll initiative."

---

### Front 2: The Streets (The Spear)
Gorok the Hillbreaker's bandit horde, aided by Lord Pellor's treachery, has infiltrated through a forgotten gate or tunnel. Their goal is to sow chaos, burn districts, and assassinate leaders.

- **Commanded by:** Ashka and the PCs. This is a battle of ambushes, guerrilla tactics, and urban warfare.
- **PC Role:** Players aligned with Ashka will be fighting street-by-street, putting out fires, stopping looters, and hunting saboteurs.

---

Gorok's 200 bandits have infiltrated through the old cistern tunnels (aided by Lord Pellor's intelligence). They're spreading through Cobbleward and Trade Districts with orders to burn, loot, and assassinate faction leaders. ### Combat Objectives

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: Save the Districts**
- Prevent 3+ districts from falling to bandits
- Evacuate at least 100 civilians to safety (Temple District sanctuary)
- Defeat all 3 bandit lieutenants

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES (Choose districts to save):**
1. **Cobbleward** - Ashka's stronghold; if lost, revolutionary morale collapses
2. **Trade District** - Grain stores and supplies; if burned, city starves
3. **Market Square** - 150 civilians trapped; if bandits reach them, massacre occurs
4. **Temple District** - Healers and sanctuary; if sabotaged, wounded die

**BONUS OBJECTIVES:**
1. **Zero Building Fires** - Stop all 6 arsonists before they complete their mission
2. **Rescue All Civilians** - Save 150+ civilians, no non-combatant deaths
3. **Capture a Lieutenant Alive** - Interrogate to learn Pellor's involvement (intelligence for boss fight)
4. **Discover Pellor's Treachery Early** - Find evidence (coded letter, bandit with signet ring) before boss fight

### Combat Phases

#### Phase 1: The Infiltration (Rounds 1-3)
**Bandit Actions:**
- 60 bandits spread across 3 districts (20 per district)
- Arsonists target key buildings (Round 1: Grain store, Round 2: Militia armory, Round 3: Bridge)
- Civilians panic, flee toward Market Square
- Assassins move into position to strike leaders

**Defender Actions:**
- PCs must choose: Which district to defend first?
- Ashka: "We must save Cobbleward - it's our heart!"
- Guild Leaders: "The grain stores! Without food, we starve!"
- Temple Priests: "The sanctuary must hold - the wounded need us!"
- **PC CHOICE:** Split party across districts OR focus all efforts on one

**Event - Round 2:** First building catches fire (arsonist success)
- Fire spreads 10 ft per round if not extinguished
- DC 13 Athletics/Acrobatics to rescue trapped civilians from burning building
- Each round fire burns: 2d6 damage to civilians inside

**Event - Round 3:** Assassins strike at chosen leader
- If PCs with Ashka: Assassin targets her (surprise attack if DC 16 Perception failed)
- If PCs split: Assassin targets isolated PC (sneak attack)

#### Phase 2: Street Fighting (Rounds 4-7)
**Bandit Actions:**
- Lieutenants arrive with reinforcements (20 more bandits each)
- Bandits form defensive positions in captured buildings
- Saboteurs attempt to collapse bridge (DC 15 Thieves' Tools, requires 3 rounds)
- Market Square threatened: Bandits advance toward 150 trapped civilians

**Defender Actions:**
- **District Combat:** PCs fight lieutenant + 20 bandits in chosen district
  - Urban terrain: Alleys (5-ft wide, single file), barricades (half-cover), rooftops (10-ft jump to cross)
- **Fire Fighting:** DC 14 Athletics to form bucket brigade, extinguish fire in 2 rounds
- **Civilian Evacuation:** DC 12 Persuasion to calm panicked civilians, guide them to Temple District
- **Assassination Defense:** Protect leaders from assassin squads

**Tactical Options:**
- **Ashka's Fireball:** Can clear 20-ft radius of bandits but risks fire spread
- **Rooftop Ambush:** PCs can gain high ground (advantage on attacks, bandits must climb DC 12)
- **Barricades:** Create chokepoints (action, DC 12 Str check, grants half-cover to defenders)
- **Alarm Bell:** Ring district bell to summon 1d10 militia (takes 2 rounds to arrive)

**Event - Round 6:** Bandit lieutenant reveals Pellor's treachery
- If captured/dying: "Pellor promised us the palace vault... showed us the tunnels..."
- PCs gain crucial intelligence: Pellor is the traitor
- Can send messenger to Front 1 (wall) to warn Brennis/Elara

#### Phase 3: The Final Push (Rounds 8-10)
**Bandit Actions:**
- Surviving lieutenants make desperate assault on Market Square (massacre attempt)
- All remaining bandits converge (30-40 bandits + 1-2 lieutenants)
- Arsonists throw final alchemist's fires (targeting Temple District sanctuary)
- Saboteurs complete objectives if not stopped

**Defender Actions:**
- **Last Stand at Market Square:** Protect 150 civilians
  - Narrow streets funnel bandits (only 5-ft wide approach)
  - Civilians cowering behind PCs/defenders
  - If bandits reach civilians: Massacre begins (10 civilians die per round)
- **Extinguish Fires:** Critical saves to prevent city-wide conflagration
- **Hunt Saboteurs:** DC 16 Perception to spot, chase through streets (Athletics/Acrobatics DC 14)

**Event - Round 9:** Gorok the Hillbreaker himself approaches from palace district
- Messenger: "The Bandit King comes! He's heading for the palace!"
- PCs must choose: Finish street fight OR rush to intercept Gorok

**Event - Round 10:** Resolution
- If bandits defeated: City saved, civilians safe, can pursue Gorok to palace
- If bandits still fighting: Must leave militia to finish fight, rush to palace weakened

### Success Metrics

**VICTORY CONDITIONS:**
- **Heroic Victory:** All 3 lieutenants dead, <10 civilian casualties, no fires spread, Market Square secured
  - Revolutionary fervor: Citizens hail PCs as saviors
  - Ashka: "You fought like true children of Estellon!"
  - Full strength for palace assault

- **Standard Victory:** 2 lieutenants dead, 10-50 civilian casualties, 1-2 fires contained
  - City saved but scarred
  - Some districts damaged
  - Moderate strength for palace assault

- **Costly Victory:** 1 lieutenant dead, 50+ civilian casualties, multiple fires burning
  - City in chaos
  - Ashka must stay to organize rescue efforts
  - PCs assault palace without her support

**DEFEAT CONDITIONS:**
- **District Lost:** Bandits control 2+ districts
  - Civilians massacred (150+ deaths)
  - City morale collapses
  - Must retreat to palace district for final stand

- **Ashka Assassinated:** Revolutionary leader killed
  - Revolution collapses
  - Cobbleward falls into bandit hands
  - PCs isolated

### Tactical Considerations

**Urban Combat Advantages:**
- **Narrow Streets:** Limit bandit numbers (only 2-3 can fight PCs simultaneously)
- **Rooftop Superiority:** High ground grants advantage, bandits disadvantaged
- **Barricades:** Improvised cover (crates, wagons) provides half-cover (+2 AC)
- **Ambush Positions:** Ashka knows every alley; set traps (nets, caltrops, oil slicks)

**Fire Mechanics:**
- **Ignition:** Alchemist's fire ignites 10-ft area
- **Spread:** Fire expands 10 ft per round toward flammable material
- **Extinguish:** DC 14 Athletics check (bucket brigade) or Create Water spell
- **Damage:** 2d6 fire per round to creatures in fire
- **Structural:** Building collapses after 10 rounds of burning (DC 15 Dex save or 4d6 bludgeoning)

**Civilian Protection:**
- **Evacuation:** DC 12 Persuasion per 10 civilians, move 30 ft/round toward sanctuary
- **Panic:** If bandits within 30 ft, civilians flee randomly (50% toward danger)
- **Casualties:** If bandits reach civilians, 1d10 civilians die per round until defenders intervene

**Spell Synergies:**
- **Ashka's Wall of Fire:** Block street (bandits take 5d8 fire if they cross)
- **Grease:** Slick streets (bandits fall prone, disadvantage on Dex saves)
- **Darkness:** Hide civilian evacuation routes
- **Thunderwave:** Push bandits off rooftops (2d6 fall damage + 2d8 thunder)

### NPC Actions & Roleplay

**Ashka (if present):**
- Opening: "These cowards burn our homes! Show them the fury of Estellon!"

- **Flight/Kiting:** Force Gorok to use Legendary Move, burn actions chasing
- **Tanking:** High AC character with Shield spell can survive Reckless attacks
- **Psychic/Force Damage:** Not resisted by Rage (Mind Spike, Eldritch Blast, etc.)

**Environmental Use:**
- **Columns:** Break them to create difficult terrain (Gorok's speed negated)
- **Balconies:** Knock archers off (10 ft fall, 1d6 damage, prone)

- **Standard Victory:** Pellor dead, Duke alive, Mandate secured, some PCs were dominated
  - Evidence of conspiracy found
  - City accepts Pellor's death as justice
  - Standard epilogue path

- **Costly Victory:** Pellor dead, Duke wounded, Mandate destroyed/damaged, heavy domination
  - Conspiracy evidence incomplete
  - Some doubt Pellor's full guilt (Brennis faction skeptical)
  - Epilogue complicated by lack of legal mandate

**DEFEAT/COMPLICATIONS:**
- **Pellor Escapes:** Via Dimension Door + escape tunnel
  - Becomes recurring villain
  - Can sabotage epilogue (assassinate leaders, steal Mandate)
  - Extended campaign option

- **Duke Killed:** By Pellor's hand
  - No legal abdication possible
  - Revolution must seize power by force (Ashka's path)
  - Brennis opposes illegitimate government

- **Mandate Destroyed:** By Pellor
  - Elara's legal path blocked
  - Must use force or popular uprising
  - Coalition Strength diminished

### Tactical Considerations

**Pellor's Weaknesses:**
- **Low HP:** Only 80 HP - focus fire can drop him quickly
- **Concentration:** Many key spells require concentration (hit him to break)
- **Melee Vulnerability:** AC 15 is mediocre; he fears melee combatants
- **Counterspell Limit:** Can only Counterspell once per round (coordinate spells)

**Pellor's Strengths:**
- **Mind Control:** Can turn party against itself
- **Invisibility:** Very hard to target with attacks
- **Defensive Reactions:** Shield, Instinctive Charm, Counterspell
- **Escape Options:** Dimension Door, Misty Step (very slippery)

**Counter-Strategies:**
- **Protection from Evil/Good:** Immunity to charm/domination (hard counters Pellor)
- **Blindsight/Truesight:** Bypasses Greater Invisibility
- **Silence:** Shuts down his spellcasting entirely (DC 17 Con saves to maintain concentration)
- **Counterspell Chain:** If multiple casters, can out-counterspell Pellor
- **Zone Control:** Grapple/restrain to prevent Dimension Door escape

**Spell Priority Targets:**
- **Must Dispel:** Dominate Person (frees allies)
- **Must Counterspell:** Dimension Door (prevents escape), Hold Person (saves PCs)
- **Can Ignore:** Minor Illusion, Disguise Self (fluff spells)

### NPC Involvement

**Duke Valerius:**
- Opening: Cowering, drunk, pathetic
- "Pellor, you promised to protect me!"
- If dominated: Attacks PCs with bare hands (ineffective, pitiful)
- If saved: Grateful but broken, "I'll sign anything... just let me leave this city..."

**Dominated Guards:**
- Beg PCs not to hurt them: "I can't stop! Help!"
- If freed: Immediately turn on Pellor, "You monster!"
- Provide testimony against Pellor (legal evidence)

**If Ashka Present:**
- Counterspells Pellor's key spells
- "I knew you were a snake, Pellor!"
- If dominated: Pellor uses her to cast Fireball on party (nightmare scenario)

**If Elara Present:**
- Casts Protection from Evil/Good on PCs (prevents charm)
- Recognizes Mandate in vault: "The Charter! It's real!"
- Attempts to parley with Pellor: "Surrender and face justice honorably"

**If Brennis Present:**
- Engages Animated Armor (martial expert)
- If dominated: Pellor commands him to attack weakest PC (brutal efficiency)
- Respects Pellor's tactics despite hating his treachery

## Part 3: The Abdication (The Legal Transfer)

With the internal threat neutralized and the external attack breaking due to the loss of its leadership, the alliance has won. But now they must face the peace.

- **The Mandate's Purpose:** If Elara's faction secured the Crown-Regent's Mandate in Act II, this is its moment.
- **The Ceremony:** Elara (or the PCs) formally presents the charges against Valerius using the Mandate's clauses for "Failure of Justice, Provision, and Defense."
- **The Outcome:** He is forced to sign a letter of abdication, legally and peacefully ending his rule. If the Mandate was destroyed, the abdication is forced through threat of popular revolt (Ashka's way) or a pragmatic deal (Brennis's way).

## Epilogue: The Council of Estellon

The factions gather in the great hall. The PCs, as the heroes of the hour, are given the honour of casting the deciding votes.

### 1. The Fate of Duke Valerius

- **Exile:** A peaceful, permanent removal.
- **Trial:** A long, public airing of the city's trauma.
- **Pardon:** A pragmatic, but unpopular, clean slate.
- **Execution:** A violent, symbolic end to the old era.

### 2. The New Form of Government

- **Reformed Dukedom:** (Lawful) A new, virtuous ruler is installed.
- **Military Stewardship:** (Neutral) Brennis rules as Lord-Protector.
- **People's Council:** (Chaotic) A representative council from all walks of life.
- **Free State:** (Anarchy) Central authority is dissolved.

The DM narrates the future of Estellon based on the players' choices, providing a satisfying, player-driven conclusion to the story of law, chaos, and the weight of the crown.
