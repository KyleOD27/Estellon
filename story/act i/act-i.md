# Act I: The Spark of Rebellion

**Goal:** To transform the players from observers into active participants by forcing them to choose a side in the escalating conflict, forging an alliance with one of the three faction leaders.

---

## Part 1: The Sunstone Festival

**Setup:** The party is in the market square as the Sunstone Festival begins. It's a defiant celebration, a burst of colour and noise in a grey city.

- **The Calm:** Have each player describe what their character is enjoying about the festival. This builds investment before it's torn down.
- **The Announcement:**
*"As the sun sets, a town crier moves through the districts: 'Hear ye! By order of the Duke's chamberlain, the unsanctioned Sunstone Festival is prohibited! All gatherings will be dispersed! Public disturbances will be dealt with severely!' You see squads of the city watch, led by the grim Captain Brennis, already moving into position. The festive mood of the morning has curdled into tension."*
 
- **The Storm:** Captain Brennis and a squad of guards, including Sergeant Rykard, arrive. Brennis declares the gathering "unsanctioned" and orders it shut down.
- **The Spark:** The guards begin roughly dispersing the crowd. Rykard shoves an elderly woman. A drunk reveller throws a punch at him. Chaos erupts.
- **The Confrontation:** Ashka appears, using minor magic to create barriers of light and gusts of wind to protect fleeing civilians from guard clubs. Brennis locks eyes with them. "Bog-Witch. I should have known. Your chaos follows you like a stench."
- **The Player's Choice:** This is the first major decision.
-Do the players:
  - Help Ashka create diversions and protect people? (Option A)
  - Help Brennis and the guards restore order? (Option B)
  - Try to protect themselves and neutral bystanders? (Option C)

- **Option A**:

Ashka says: "You there! You look capable, give me a hand with this would you?"

*Protective Actions*:
- Shield Civilians: Use bodies, shields, or magic to block guard clubs and protect fleeing festival-goers. DC 10 Toughness check. Pass: +2 Zeal. Fail: +1 Zeal, take 1d4 bash damage

- Create Escape Routes: Clear paths through overturned stalls, guide people to safe exits, or create new openings. DC 10 Cardio check. Pass: +2 Zeal. Fail: +1 Zeal, trip and lose 1d4 gold

- Tend to the Wounded: Help injured civilians who've been trampled or struck by guards. Requires: First Aid. +2 Zeal. Can't fail.

- Form Human Chains: Link arms with other defenders to create barriers between guards and civilians Requires: 3 PCs. DC 5 Toughness (x3) Pass: +4 Zeal. Fail: 1d2 bash damage

Gain title: Protector (Gain aura: all allies in 10 feet gain +1 Armor)

*Diversionary Tactics*:
- Cause Distractions: Knock over merchant stalls, release livestock, start fires in trash barrels (away from people). DC 10 Mischief check. Pass: +2 Zeal. Fail: +1 Zeal, lose an item

- Misdirection: Point guards in wrong directions, create false alarms elsewhere in the square DC 10 Persona check. Pass: +1 Zeal. Fail: No effect

- Use the Environment: Trigger festival mechanisms - release balloon displays, activate fireworks, tip over stages. Requires: Sharp Instincts, +2 Zeal. Can't fail.

Gain title: Trickster (+2 Mischief) 

*Direct Assistance to Ashka*:
- Channel Magic: Help sustain Ashka's barriers or create complementary effects. Requires: Spellcasting. +2 Zeal. Can't fail.

- Physical Support: Guard Ashka's flanks while they concentrate on spellcasting. DC 10 Toughness check. Pass: +2 Zeal. Fail: take 1d4 slash damage

- Relay Communications: Help coordinate with other festival defenders across the chaotic square. DC 10 Persona Check. Pass: +1 Zeal. Fail: No effect.

- Gather Materials: Fetch components Ashka needs for more complex protective spells. DC 10 Cardio check. Pass +2 Zeal. Fail: Lose 1d4 gold

Gain title: Sidekick (Gain ability: Ashka's favour)


- **Option B**:

Brennis says: "Newcomer, this doesn't regard you - step aside or make yourself useful."

*Crowd Control Actions*:
- Cordon the Area: Form defensive lines to contain the riot and prevent it from spreading. DC 10 Toughness check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: +1 Control, take 1d4 bash damage from thrown objects

- Subdue Agitators: Identify and restrain the most violent rioters using non-lethal force. DC 10 Combat check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: +1 Control, lose 1 piece of equipment in struggle

- Clear the Square: Systematically push crowds toward designated exits using shields and formation tactics. DC 10 Authority check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: +1 Control, crowd pushes back

- Protect Property: Guard merchant stalls and shops from looters. Requires: 2 PCs. DC 8 Toughness (x2) Pass: +3 Control. Fail: 1d4 gold worth of goods destroyed

Gain title: Enforcer (Gain aura: all enemies in 10 feet gain -1 to attack rolls)

*Investigation Actions*:
- Identify Ringleaders: Point out specific troublemakers to the guard for targeted arrests. DC 10 Perception check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: Identify wrong person, -1 Control

- Document Evidence: Record names and faces of riot participants for later prosecution. Requires: Writing materials. +2 Control. Can't fail.

- Trace the Source: Figure out who organized this "spontaneous" gathering. DC 12 Investigation check. Pass: +3 Control. Fail: No effect

- Interview Witnesses: Question bystanders about who started the violence. DC 10 Persona check. Pass: +1 Control. Fail: Witnesses too frightened

Gain title: Investigator (+2 Investigation)

*Direct Assistance to Brennis*:
- Guard Captain's Flank: Protect Brennis while he coordinates the response. DC 10 Combat check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: take 1d4 slash damage from rioter

- Relay Orders: Help communicate Brennis's commands across the chaotic square. DC 10 Authority check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: Orders misunderstood, no effect

- Medical Support: Tend to wounded guards to get them back in formation. Requires: First Aid. +2 Control. Can't fail.

- Rally the Guard: Boost morale of wavering watchmen. DC 10 Persona check. Pass: +2 Control. Fail: Guards ignore you

Gain title: Deputy (Gain ability: Brennis's favor)


- **Option C**:

A shopkeeper cries: "Please, someone help! We're not part of this!"

*Neutral Protection Actions*:
- Evacuate Bystanders: Guide innocent festival-goers away from both guards and rioters. DC 10 Cardio check. Pass: +1 Independent Influence. Fail: Some people get caught in violence

- Create Safe Zones: Establish neutral areas (temple steps, healer's tent) where no fighting is allowed. DC 12 Persona check. Pass: +2 Independent Influence. Fail: Zone violated by one side

- Shield the Innocent: Physically protect children, elderly, and wounded from both sides. DC 10 Toughness check. Pass: +2 Independent Influence. Fail: take 1d4 damage protecting others

- Negotiate Passages: Convince both guards and rioters to let civilians leave safely. Requires: 2 PCs. DC 10 Persona (x2). Pass: +3 Independent Influence. Fail: One side refuses

Gain title: Peacekeeper (Gain ability: Both sides hesitate to attack you first)

*Mercantile Actions*:
- Protect Merchant Goods: Help traders save their wares from destruction. DC 10 Strength check. Pass: +1 Independent Influence, gain merchant favor. Fail: Goods destroyed

- Organize Merchant Defense: Rally shopkeepers to protect their own property. DC 10 Authority check. Pass: +2 Independent Influence. Fail: Merchants flee instead

- Document Damages: Record losses for insurance claims and compensation. Requires: Writing materials. +1 Independent Influence. Can't fail.

- Broker Temporary Truce: Convince both sides to avoid the merchant quarter. DC 14 Persona check. Pass: +3 Independent Influence. Fail: No effect

Gain title: Merchant's Friend (+2 to merchant interactions)

*Opportunistic Actions*:
- Profit from Chaos: Loot abandoned stalls while everyone's distracted. DC 12 Mischief check. Pass: Gain 2d6 gold. Fail: Caught by one side, -2 to their faction

- Information Gathering: Use the chaos to overhear important conversations. DC 10 Stealth check. Pass: Learn valuable secret. Fail: No useful information

- Play Both Sides: Appear to help each faction when they're watching. DC 14 Deception check. Pass: +1 to both Control and Zeal. Fail: Both sides distrust you, -1 to each

- Document Everything: Record the event for later sale to interested parties. Requires: Sharp Instincts. Gain information to sell later. Can't fail.

Gain title: Opportunist (Gain ability: Fence goods at better prices)

- **Outcome:** Their actions here grant them their initial reputation with the People (Zeal) or the City Guard (Control).

## Part 2: The Conscription Decree

**Setup:** The morning after the riot, the Duke's regime—advised by Lord Pellor—announces a harsh conscription decree to "restore order and remove troublesome elements." The city awakens to fear as squads of guards begin sweeping through districts, dragging young men and women from their homes.

### Elara's Position

**Her Role:**
Ser Elara has been assigned by her paladin order to oversee the conscription in the Craftsmen's Quarter to ensure it's conducted "lawfully and with honor." She's accompanied by a squad led by Sergeant Rykard. Her superiors believe this will lend legitimacy to the operation.

**Her Inner Conflict:**
Elara knows the decree is unjust—targeting the poor while exempting nobles who can afford "exemption fees." But she's sworn oaths to both her order and the lawful authority of Estellon. She tells herself she can minimize abuses from within the system.

**Her Presence:**
When players first see her, Elara is:
- Standing near the conscription wagons, reviewing lists
- Speaking quietly with families, explaining their "options"
- Visibly uncomfortable but maintaining composure
- Occasionally correcting guard behavior (pulling someone's arm less roughly, allowing a tearful goodbye)

> **DM Note:** Elara is trying to do the right thing within an evil system. She's not yet ready to break her oaths, but she's reaching her limit.

---

### The Scene Unfolds

**The Tanner's Shop:**

The conscription detail arrives at a modest tannery. The owner, **Aldric the Tanner**, is a grizzled veteran missing two fingers from the War of the Northern Reach. His son, **Tomás** (age 16), helps run the shop.

**The Approach:**

Sergeant Rykard consults his list and pounds on the door.

> **Rykard:** "By order of the Duke, this household must provide one able-bodied conscript for the city militia!"

Aldric limps to the door, his bad leg (old war wound) dragging. Tomás appears behind him.

> **Aldric:** "Sergeant, please—I've already served. My boy here, he's all I have. The shop can't run without him, and I can't work the vats anymore with this leg..."

> **Rykard:** *(sneering)* "The decree is clear. One conscript per household. The boy comes with us."

**The Bribe:**

Aldric's face hardens. He disappears inside and returns with a leather pouch—his life savings.

> **Aldric:** "Two hundred gold pieces. Everything I have. Take it and leave us be. Mark my house as... already provided. Please."

Rykard weighs the pouch in his hand, grinning. Then he pockets it.

> **Rykard:** "I'll take this as a generous donation to the city's coffers. Good of you. However..." *(signals guards)* "You're also under arrest for attempting to bribe an officer of the Duke. Both of you, in the wagon."

**Elara's Witness:**

Elara has been reviewing paperwork nearby but looks up at the commotion. She sees:
- Rykard pocketing the money
- Guards grabbing both father and son
- Tomás struggling, Aldric pleading
- The smug satisfaction on Rykard's face

Her hand moves to her sword hilt. She freezes.

---

### Elara's Internal Crisis (Roleplay This Moment)

**The Conflict:**

DM, show this through Elara's body language and brief internal monologue (if narrating):

- Her jaw clenches
- Her hand trembles on her sword
- She takes a step forward... then stops
- She closes her eyes briefly, as if praying

**What She's Thinking:**

*"This is wrong. This is evil. But if I intervene, I break my oath to the chain of command. I'll be branded a rebel. My order will disown me. Everything I've worked for, gone. But... what good are oaths that serve cruelty? What is law without justice?"*

**The Player Proximity:**

The players are present for this scene. They might be:
- Hired as additional guards (if they sided with Brennis in Part 1)
- Following Elara to speak with her (if they sided with her tendencies)
- Just passing through the district (if neutral)
- Deliberately observing the conscription (any reason)

**Make Eye Contact:**

Before Elara acts, she locks eyes with one of the players. There's a silent question in her gaze: *"What would you do? Will you stand with me if I do this?"*

---

### Elara's Decision: The Intervention

**If Players Show Support (or Elara Judges Them Honorable):**

Elara takes a deep breath and strides forward, her voice ringing with authority.

> **Elara:** "SERGEANT RYKARD. Release these men. Immediately."

> **Rykard:** *(turning, surprised)* "Ser Elara? These two are being arrested for—"

> **Elara:** "I witnessed the entire exchange. You solicited and accepted a bribe, then arrested them to cover your crime. You will return the money, release this family, and report to the barracks for disciplinary action."

> **Rykard:** *(recovering, smirking)* "With respect, *Ser*, I am following the decree exactly as written. The chamberlain himself authorized harsh measures against resistance. Perhaps you should remember your place."

**Elara's Response:**

> **Elara:** *(drawing her sword partway—not threatening, but unmistakable)* "My place is as a paladin of the Light. I serve justice, not tyranny. These men go free. The decree is law, but what you've done here is theft and abuse of power. Stand down, Sergeant."

**The Standoff:**

Guards look uncertain. They respect Elara, but Rykard is their direct commander. The tension is palpable.

**This is the Player's Moment:**

---

### Player Choice Point

**IMPORTANT: Special Actions Available**

Before choosing a main path, players can take these critical actions:

**Persuade Elara to Stand Down:**
- **DC 15 Persuasion check:** "Ser Elara, this isn't the way. Don't throw away everything for this."
  - **Pass:** Elara hesitates, then lowers her sword. "You're... you're right. I can fight this through proper channels." She stands down. Rykard smirks and takes the family. **Result:** +1 City Control, Elara's trust in players is complicated. She's grateful you prevented her from becoming a rebel, but haunted by her inaction.
  - **Fail:** Elara shakes her head. "No. I've stood aside too many times. Not today." She continues the confrontation. Proceed to Option A, B, or C.

**Attack Rykard Directly:**
- If players attack Rykard (physically or with magic) during the standoff:
  - **Elara's Response:** Even though she opposes Rykard, attacking him is attacking the lawful authority she still respects.
  - **Immediate Action:** Elara moves to restrain the attacking player. "STOP! I cannot allow this violence, no matter how justified your anger!"
  - **Arrest:** Elara will attempt to arrest the player who attacked (grapple or disarm, non-lethal)
  - **Consequences:** Players are taken to the barracks. Elara vouches for them as "misguided" and they're released with a warning. **Result:** +2 City Control, -2 Coalition Strength. Elara sees them as dangerously impulsive. Relationship damaged.

**Attack Elara Directly:**
- If players attack Elara:
  - **Immediate Combat:** Elara defends herself, guards join her
  - **Outcome:** Players likely overpowered, arrested, or forced to flee
  - **Consequences:** Branded as enemies of the paladin order and city guard. **Result:** +3 City Control (for helping stop lawbreakers), permanent enemy status with Elara's faction

---

### Standard Player Paths

**Option A: Support Elara**

Players can:

**Verbal Support:**
> "She's right, Sergeant. I saw what you did."

**Physical Positioning:**
- Move to flank Elara
- Place hands on weapons
- Create a united front

**Actions:**
- **Testify:** DC 10 Persuasion check. "I witnessed Rykard take the bribe before making the arrest."
  - Pass: +2 Coalition Strength, guards waver
  - Fail: +1 Coalition Strength, Rykard sneers "And who are you to question the Duke's law?"

- **Intimidate Rykard:** DC 12 Intimidation check. "Back down before this escalates."
  - Pass: +2 Coalition Strength, Rykard reluctantly releases them
  - Fail: +1 Coalition Strength, Rykard calls for more guards

- **Appeal to Guards:** DC 10 Persuasion check. "You all know this isn't right. Stand with honor."
  - Pass: +3 Coalition Strength, guards step away from Rykard
  - Fail: +1 Coalition Strength, guards stay loyal to Rykard

- **Offer Alternative:** DC 12 Insight check. Find legal justification for release.
  - Pass: +2 Coalition Strength, provide technicality Elara can use
  - Fail: Can't find loophole

**If Players Support Elara:**

Elara gains confidence. She unsheathes her sword fully (still not attacking).

> **Elara:** "I am declaring this arrest unlawful. Aldric, Tomás—you are free to go. Guards, if you value your honor, you will stand down. Rykard, you will answer to Captain Brennis for this."

**Rykard's Reaction:**

If players have intimidated him or swayed the guards, Rykard backs down with a venomous glare.

> **Rykard:** "This isn't over, *Paladin*. The chamberlain will hear of your insubordination."

He stalks off. The guards disperse, muttering. Aldric and Tomás are freed.

**Elara to Players:**

> **Elara:** *(lowering sword, breathing heavily)* "Thank you. I... I couldn't have done that alone. What happened here today—this is what I've been fighting against from within. But I see now that some evils cannot be reformed. They must be confronted."

She looks at the players directly.

> **Elara:** "Will you walk this path with me? I'm going to gather others who still believe in justice. We're going to change this city the right way—through law, through honor, but also through courage. I need allies I can trust."

**Result:** +2 Coalition Strength. Elara remembers this moment. The players gain access to Elara's reformist network.

---

**Option B: Support Rykard / Enforce the Decree**

Players can:

**Verbal Support:**
> "The decree is the law, Ser Elara. We can't just ignore it."

**Physical Actions:**
- Stand with Rykard
- Block Elara's path to the prisoners
- Remind her of "duty"

**Actions:**
- **Cite Authority:** DC 10 Persuasion check. "The chamberlain's orders are clear."
  - Pass: +2 City Control, Elara hesitates
  - Fail: +1 City Control, Elara isn't swayed

- **Restrain Aldric/Tomás:** DC 10 Strength check. Physically ensure they can't escape.
  - Pass: +2 City Control, they're secured
  - Fail: +1 City Control, struggle causes scene

- **Remind Elara of Oaths:** DC 12 Persuasion check. "You swore to obey lawful authority."
  - Pass: +3 City Control, Elara stands down, conflicted
  - Fail: +1 City Control, Elara's resolve strengthens

**If Players Support Rykard:**

Elara looks at the players with profound disappointment.

> **Elara:** *(quietly, sword arm falling)* "I see. Then... you're right. The law is the law."

She sheathes her sword but doesn't look at Rykard.

> **Elara:** "Proceed, Sergeant. I will... report this to my superiors."

Aldric and Tomás are loaded into the wagon, betrayed and broken. Aldric meets Elara's eyes briefly—there's no anger, only sadness that she didn't act.

**Rykard to Players:**

> **Rykard:** "Glad to see some people understand how the world works. You did the right thing. The Duke needs order, and order requires hard choices."

He tosses them a small reward (25gp).

**Elara's Departure:**

Elara walks away without another word. Players can see her shoulders shaking slightly. This moment will haunt her.

**Later Scene (Optional):**

If players seek out Elara afterward, they find her in the cathedral, kneeling before the altar.

> **Elara:** *(without turning)* "I failed them. I knew it was wrong, and I failed them. You reminded me of my duty to the law... but what good is law without justice? I don't know what to do anymore."

She's breaking. Players can:
- Console her (soft approach)
- Justify the action (reinforce authority)
- Leave her to her crisis

**Result:** +2 City Control. Elara's trust in the system is shaken. She may not seek players out for her faction.

---

**Option C: Third Way / Creative Solutions**

Players might:

**Distraction:**
- Create a commotion elsewhere to draw guards away
- Start a fire or fake emergency

**Bribery:**
- Offer Rykard more money to let them go
- Promise future favors

**Legal Maneuvering:**
- Find a technicality (Aldric already served, son is too young, etc.)
- Claim you need them as witnesses for another investigation

**Direct Action:**
- Sneak the family out during the confusion
- Knock out Rykard (risky!)
- Forge documents exempting them

**Actions:**
- **Create Distraction:** DC 12 Mischief check. Stage incident nearby.
  - Pass: +2 Independent Influence, family escapes in chaos
  - Fail: +1 Independent Influence, caught in the act

- **Negotiate Compromise:** DC 14 Persuasion check. "What if the son serves in a non-combat role?"
  - Pass: +2 Independent Influence, Rykard accepts alternative
  - Fail: No effect

- **Smuggle Them Out:** DC 14 Stealth check (group). Get family to safehouse.
  - Pass: +3 Independent Influence, family hidden successfully
  - Fail: Caught, must fight or flee

**Elara's Reaction to Third Way:**

If players succeed with a creative solution:

> **Elara:** *(approaching after guards leave)* "That was... clever. You found a way I couldn't see. Perhaps there are more paths than I thought."

She seems thoughtful rather than resolved.

> **Elara:** "You're not bound by the same oaths I am. Maybe that's your strength. If you're willing, I could use people who think outside the law's rigid lines—people who seek justice without being paralyzed by it."

**Result:** +2 Independent Influence. Elara sees players as pragmatic allies rather than true believers. Relationship is transactional but respectful.

### Aftermath & Consequences

**If Elara Intervened (with player support):**

- **Immediate:** Rykard reports Elara to Lord Pellor
- **Within Hours:** Elara is summoned by her order and questioned
- **By Evening:** Elara is officially "reassigned to administrative duties" (effectively suspended)
- **Her Response:** Elara uses this freedom to begin organizing the reform movement openly
- **Player Connection:** Elara seeks out the players that night

**If Elara Backed Down:**

- **Immediate:** Rykard completes conscription, family taken away
- **Within Hours:** Elara goes to the cathedral to pray and reflect
- **By Evening:** Elara has a crisis of faith and begins questioning everything
- **Her Response:** She may disappear for several days, wrestling with her failure
- **Player Connection:** Players may need to find her to continue her storyline

**If Players Persuaded Elara to Stand Down (DC 15):**

- **Immediate:** Elara lets the injustice happen but doesn't actively participate
- **Within Hours:** Elara seeks out the players to thank them for preventing her "rash action"
- **By Evening:** Elara is conflicted—grateful she didn't break her oaths, but guilt-ridden
- **Her Response:** She tries to work within the system to help Aldric and Tomás later
- **Player Connection:** Complex relationship—Elara trusts players' judgment but questions her own

**If Players Attacked Rykard:**

- **Immediate:** Elara restrains/arrests attacking player, prevents further violence
- **Within Hours:** Elara uses her influence to get players released with warning
- **By Evening:** Elara speaks to players sternly: "Your heart was in the right place, but violence without authority is chaos. That's Ashka's path, not mine."
- **Her Response:** Keeps distance from players, sees them as well-meaning but dangerous
- **Player Connection:** Damaged trust. Elara won't seek them out for partnership, but won't oppose them

**If Third Way Worked:**

- **Immediate:** Situation resolved without direct confrontation
- **Within Hours:** Elara realizes the law isn't enough
- **By Evening:** Elara begins building a network of reformers
- **Her Response:** Grateful but still uncertain of her path
- **Player Connection:** Elara sees players as valuable independent operators

   - If they supported her: Personal meeting, offers partnership
   - If they opposed her: Cautious inquiry, seeks understanding

### Elara's Offer to Players

**If They Supported Her:**

Elara meets them at her chapterhouse (or neutral location if suspended).

> **Elara:** "What we did today—what *you* did—showed me that change is possible. But we need allies. I'm gathering people who believe in justice, not blind obedience. Nobles who see the system is failing. Clergy who remember the light serves the people, not power. Common folk with courage."

> "I need people like you. People who act when action is needed. Will you help me build something better? Not through violence and chaos like Ashka's path, but through law, honor, and standing up when it matters?"

**Quest Offer:** "Rykard must be stopped. He frequents a tavern called The Rusty Nail—a vile place where he boasts of his 'earnings' and plans his next shakedowns. Find evidence of his corruption. Testimonies. Records. Anything I can bring before the Duke or the City Council. If we can remove men like him, we prove the system *can* work."

**Reward:** 100gp, +3 Coalition Strength, Elara's personal gratitude, access to noble contacts

**If They Opposed Her:**

Elara approaches cautiously.

> **Elara:** "I want to understand. Why did you side with Rykard? Do you truly believe what he did was just? Or did you simply follow the letter of the law?"

She listens to their answer without judgment.

> "I can't condone what happened. But I respect that you see order as valuable. Perhaps... we're not so different. You want stability. So do I. The question is: what kind of stability? One built on fear and corruption? Or one built on fairness and trust?"

> "If you believe the latter is possible, I could use people who understand the value of order. Help me prove that reform can bring real stability—not chaos, not revolution, but evolution."

**Quest Offer:** Same as above, but framed as "We both want order. Let's remove the rot that threatens it."

**Reward:** 75gp, +2 Coalition Strength, cautious alliance

---

**If They Persuaded Her to Stand Down (DC 15):**

Elara meets them later that day, conflicted.

> **Elara:** "You stopped me from making a terrible mistake today. Breaking my oaths, defying the chain of command... that would have made me no better than Ashka's rebels."

She pauses, looking troubled.

> "But I can't shake the image of Aldric and Tomás in that wagon. The law failed them. I failed them. Perhaps... perhaps working within the system isn't enough. But neither is abandoning it entirely."

> "I need to think. But I also need people like you—people who can see both sides. Will you help me find a way to make the system work? To prove that reform doesn't require revolution?"

**Quest Offer:** "Help me gather evidence against Rykard through legal means. We'll build a case so airtight even the Duke can't ignore it. Justice through law, not rebellion."

**Reward:** 50gp, +1 Coalition Strength, +1 Independent Influence (Elara sees you as pragmatic moderates)

---

**If They Attacked Rykard:**

Elara does not seek them out. If players approach her:

> **Elara:** *(coolly)* "You acted on impulse. Violence without authority, without law... that's the path to chaos. I understand your anger, but I cannot condone what you did."

> "Rykard is a corrupt man, but he is still an officer of the city. Attacking him was attacking the very order I'm trying to preserve and reform. Do you see the difference?"

She sighs.

> "I've ensured you won't face charges this time. But I cannot work with people who resort to violence so readily. If you want to fight injustice, find another way. Or find another leader."

**Quest Offer:** None. Elara will not recruit players who attacked Rykard. Relationship is strained.

**Reward:** None from Elara

---

### The Scene's Impact

This scene is Elara's defining moment. She moves from:
- **Paladin Within the System** → **Paladin Challenging the System**

What she does here sets the stage for her entire faction arc:

- **If she intervened:** She becomes a figure of moral courage, attracting idealists
- **If she backed down:** She becomes a figure of redemption, seeking to make amends
- **If players found third way:** She becomes more pragmatic, willing to bend rules for justice

---

**DM Tips:**

- **Pace this scene slowly.** Let tension build. Give players time to react.
- **Make Elara's conflict visible.** Show her struggling with the decision.
- **Respect player choice.** No option is "wrong"—each has consequences
- **Use Rykard as a pure antagonist.** He's unrepentantly cruel, making the moral stakes clear
- **Show the human cost.** Aldric and Tomás are real people, not abstractions
- **Follow through.** Whatever happens here echoes through the rest of the campaign

## Part 3: The Aftermath & The Offer

**Setup:** The party is sought out by the faction they most aided.

### If they aided Ashka
They are brought to a hidden safehouse in the sewers or a dilapidated tenement. Ashka is nursing a wound but is defiant. "You saw what they did. They won't stop. We have to make them stop. Are you with us?"

### If they aided Elara
They are called to the paladin chapterhouse. Elara is troubled but resolute. "What happened today was not law; it was tyranny. The system is sick, but it can be healed from within. I need allies who believe in true justice. Will you help me?"

### If they aided the City/Brennis
They are summoned to the barracks. Brennis is grim. "You understand the need for order in the face of chaos. The city is fraying. I need capable hands to help me hold it together. Can I count on you?"

### The Neutral Path
If they remained neutral, a representative from a merchant's guild or another independent party might approach them with a paid job, setting them on a mercenary path.

### The Hook
The faction leader gives them their first proper quest, pulling them into the growing war.

- **Ashka:** "We need to hit their coffers. Sabotage the tax collector's carriage on the main road tomorrow."
- **Elara:** "Rykard must be stopped. Find evidence of his corruption. He frequents a vile tavern called The Rusty Nail."
- **Brennis:** "The rebels are getting bold. Their agitators are spreading pamphlets in the market. Find the printing press and shut it down."

---

## Act I Conclusion

The players are no longer visitors. They have chosen a side (or chosen to walk their own path) and are now active participants in the fate of Estellon. The philosophical conflict is now their conflict. The stage is set for the open faction war of Act II.
