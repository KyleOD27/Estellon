# Human

> *"Humans are the clay of Estellon—shaped by circumstance, hardened by choice, broken and remade by revolution."*

## Race Overview

Humans form the backbone of Estellon's population and every faction within it. Neither blessed with elven grace nor orcish strength, humans succeed through adaptability, ambition, and sheer determination. In Estellon's crisis, humans are found on every side, their flexibility making them both the agents of change and the defenders of tradition.

## Racial Traits

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+1 to any two skills** of your choice
- **Extra Ability Slot** - Can hold 4 ability cards instead of 3
- **Adaptable** - Once per session, gain +2 to any single check
- **Quick Learner** - Gain new abilities faster than other races

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 5'4" to 6'2" typically
- **Lifespan:** 60-80 years
- **Variety:** Wide range of appearances, skin tones, and builds
- **Adaptability:** Thrive in any climate or condition

## Social Standing in Estellon

### General Perception
- **Nobles:** "Proper humans from good families deserve respect"
- **Merchants:** "Reliable trading partners, predictable needs"
- **Common Folk:** "We are the city's true heart"
- **Guards:** "Easiest to manage, understand authority"

### District Reception
- **Noble Quarter:** Depends entirely on class and dress
- **Trade District:** Welcome based on coin and reputation
- **Cobbleward:** Judged by actions, not blood
- **Temple District:** Seen as the norm, baseline faithful

### Faction Views
- **Elara's Coalition:** "Humans can bridge all divides"
- **Kael's Revolution:** "Human suffering demands human uprising"
- **Brennis's Guard:** "Humans understand the need for order"

## Playing a Human

### Advantages
- **Social Flexibility:** Fit in anywhere with the right approach
- **No Prejudice:** Not automatically excluded from any group
- **Faction Choice:** No racial pressure toward any faction
- **Skill Variety:** Excel at any role or profession

### Challenges
- **No Special Treatment:** Must earn everything through merit
- **Internal Conflict:** Humans fight on all sides of the war
- **Class Matters More:** Your birth matters more than your race
- **Average Everything:** No exceptional racial abilities

### Roleplay Hooks
- Are you a noble trying to maintain privilege?
- A merchant's child choosing between profit and principle?
- A laborer who's had enough of oppression?
- An outsider trying to find their place?

## Human Variations in Estellon

### Estellon Natives
- **Born and Raised:** Deep connections, family obligations
- **Old Families:** Centuries of history in the city
- **Street Knowledge:** Know every alley and secret

### Recent Arrivals
- **Merchants:** Here for opportunity
- **Refugees:** Fleeing other conflicts
- **Adventurers:** Drawn by the crisis

### Social Classes
- **Nobility:** Ancient bloodlines, inherited privilege
- **Merchants:** New money, practical power
- **Artisans:** Skilled workers, guild members
- **Laborers:** The suffering majority

## Faction Affinity

### Natural Advantages
Humans have no inherent faction bias, making them valuable to all sides:

- **For Reformers:** Can navigate both noble and common circles
- **For Revolutionaries:** Understand the common human struggle
- **For Guards:** Represent traditional human authority

### Starting Reputation
- No racial modifiers to faction scores
- Judged purely on role and actions
- Can maintain cover in any faction

## Human Culture in Estellon

### Values
- **Ambition:** Always striving for more
- **Family:** Blood ties matter deeply
- **Tradition:** Respect for "how things are done"
- **Progress:** Belief in improving one's station

### Traditions
- **Sunstone Festival:** Humans lead most celebrations
- **Guild Systems:** Human-created and dominated
- **Marriage Customs:** Elaborate ceremonies crossing class lines
- **Funeral Rites:** Burial in family plots, continuing legacy

### Language and Names
- **Common Tongue:** The human language is the default
- **Names:** Varied by class and origin
  - Noble: Elaborate (Alexander, Cornelius, Isabella)
  - Common: Simple (Jack, Tom, Mary, Anne)
  - Foreign: Reflecting distant homeland

## Mechanical Integration

### With Roles
- **Merchant + Human:** Ultimate social chameleon
- **Scholar + Human:** Access to all archives
- **Sellsword + Human:** Hired by anyone
- **Pilgrim + Human:** Universal religious acceptance
- **Noble + Human:** Purest bloodline claims
- **Artisan + Human:** Guild leadership potential
- **Refugee + Human:** Invisible in crowds

### Unique Abilities
- **Voice of the People:** Rally humans regardless of faction
- **Cultural Adaptation:** Blend into any district
- **Network Building:** Form connections faster
- **Survivor's Will:** Endure when others would fall

## Story Potential

### Personal Arcs
- Rising from nothing to faction leadership
- Choosing between family loyalty and conscience
- Uniting humans across faction lines
- Discovering hidden noble/common heritage

### Campaign Moments
- Recognized by old friend on opposing side
- Family pressure to support specific faction
- Caught between human factions
- Becoming symbol of human unity or division

## Human Phrases for Dialogue

- *"We built this city with our hands and blood."*
- *"Humans have always found a way to survive."*
- *"I'm not defined by my blood, but by my choices."*
- *"Every human in Estellon is my kin."*
- *"We adapt, we endure, we overcome."*
