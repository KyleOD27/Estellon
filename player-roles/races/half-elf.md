# Half-Elf

> *"Too human for the elves, too elven for the humans—we Half-Elves make our own path between worlds."*

## Race Overview

Half-Elves embody the bridge between two worlds, inheriting both human adaptability and elven grace. In Estellon's divided society, they are perpetual outsiders who understand both perspectives but fully belong to neither. This unique position makes them natural diplomats, spies, and mediators in the city's political crisis.

## Racial Traits

### Passive Abilities
These abilities are always active and define your half-elven heritage:

1. **Dual Heritage** - Can invoke either human or elven ancestry for situational benefits
2. **Diplomat's Insight** - +1 to Persuasion and Insight checks
3. **Cultural Chameleon** - +1 to any skill of choice (versatile heritage)
4. **Extra Ability Slot** - Can hold 4 ability cards instead of 3
5. **Extended Lifespan** - Live 150-200 years, bridging generations

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+1 to Persuasion and Insight** (Reading both cultures)
- **+1 to any skill of choice** (Versatile heritage)
- **Dual Heritage** - Can invoke either human or elven ancestry when beneficial
- **Extra Ability Slot** - Bridge between worlds grants flexibility
- **Extended Lifespan** - 150-200 years

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 5'6" to 6'2" typically
- **Appearance:** Blend of human and elven features
- **Ears:** Slightly pointed, often hidden
- **Eyes:** Inherit elven colors but human expressiveness

## Social Standing in Estellon

### General Perception
- **Nobles:** "Useful but never quite one of us"
- **Merchants:** "Good negotiators, understand both sides"
- **Common Folk:** "Can't trust someone with divided loyalties"
- **Guards:** "Valuable for their connections"

### District Reception
- **Noble Quarter:** Tolerated but not fully accepted
- **Trade District:** Valued for diplomatic skills
- **Cobbleward:** Suspicion of elven blood
- **Temple District:** Seen as spiritually confused

### Faction Views
- **Elara's Coalition:** "Perfect bridge-builders for reform"
- **Kael's Revolution:** "Which side are they really on?"
- **Brennis's Guard:** "Useful but watch them closely"

## Playing a Half-Elf

### Advantages
- **Social Flexibility:** Navigate between cultures
- **Diplomatic Skills:** Natural mediators
- **Dual Access:** Partial acceptance in both worlds
- **Unique Perspective:** See flaws in both societies

### Challenges
- **Identity Crisis:** Never fully belonging anywhere
- **Trust Issues:** Both sides question loyalty
- **Family Complications:** Torn between two heritages
- **Loneliness:** Few others understand the experience

### Roleplay Hooks
- Raised by one parent, seeking the other's culture?
- Trying to prove yourself to one heritage?
- Embracing outsider status as strength?
- Working to unite both sides of your blood?

## Half-Elf Identity in Crisis

### Internal Conflict
- Human side craves immediate action
- Elven side counsels patience
- Torn between revolution and reform
- Understanding but belonging to neither

### Unique Advantages
- See through both human and elven propaganda
- Understand the fears of both sides
- Can speak to anyone's perspective
- Natural spies and double agents

## Faction Affinity

### Natural Advantages
- **Independent Influence +2** - Outsiders stick together
- **No strong faction bias** - Can work with anyone
- **Diplomatic bonus** - All factions value mediation

### Starting Challenges
- **Trust deficit** - Must prove loyalty twice as hard
- **Suspicion from extremists** - Moderates are enemies to all
- **Family pressure** - Both sides pull different ways
