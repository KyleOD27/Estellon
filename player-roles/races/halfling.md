# Halfling

> *"When the big folk start swinging, we halflings duck, weave, and survive. We've outlasted empires by knowing when to hide and when to help."*

## Race Overview

Halflings in Estellon have perfected the art of being overlooked. Living in tight-knit communities within larger districts, they survive through cooperation, cleverness, and knowing when to stay out of the way. The current crisis threatens their carefully maintained stability, forcing halflings to choose between their traditional neutrality and taking a stand.

## Racial Traits

### Passive Abilities
These abilities are always active and define your halfling heritage:

1. **Halfling Luck** - Once per session, reroll any failed check
2. **Nimble** - Can move through spaces occupied by larger creatures
3. **Naturally Stealthy** - +2 to Stealth and Sleight of Hand checks
4. **Community Network** - Can find help and shelter in any halfling neighborhood
5. **Friendly Folk** - +1 to Social checks with common folk

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+2 to Stealth and Sleight of Hand** (Small and nimble)
- **+1 to Social checks with common folk** (Friendly reputation)
- **Halfling Luck** - Once per session, reroll any failed check
- **Nimble** - Can move through spaces occupied by larger creatures
- **Community Network** - Can find help in any halfling neighborhood

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 3'0" to 3'6" typically
- **Build:** Proportioned but small
- **Appearance:** Childlike features, hairy feet
- **Lifespan:** 100-150 years

## Social Standing in Estellon

### General Perception
- **Nobles:** "Useful servants, harmless and efficient"
- **Merchants:** "Honest traders, good with details"
- **Common Folk:** "Good neighbors, always helpful"
- **Guards:** "Not worth watching, never cause trouble"

### District Reception
- **Noble Quarter:** Welcome as servants and entertainers
- **Trade District:** Run many small shops and inns
- **Cobbleward:** Integral part of community
- **Temple District:** Devoted but unassuming faithful

### Faction Views
- **Elara's Coalition:** "Community-minded natural supporters"
- **Ashka's Revolution:** "The smallest suffer most under tyranny"
- **Brennis's Guard:** "Harmless, not worth monitoring"

## Playing a Halfling

### Advantages
- **Overlooked:** Powerful people ignore you
- **Community Support:** Halflings help each other
- **Natural Stealth:** Size makes hiding easier
- **Universal Welcome:** No one sees you as threat

### Challenges
- **Not Taken Seriously:** Dismissed as childlike
- **Physical Limitations:** Can't reach high shelves
- **Protective Instinct:** Community comes first
- **Risk Averse:** Cultural tendency toward caution

### Roleplay Hooks
- Breaking from traditional halfling caution?
- Protecting your community from the crisis?
- Using overlooked status for spy work?
- Proving halflings can be heroes too?

## Halfling Culture in Estellon

### Values
- **Community First:** Individual needs come second
- **Comfort:** Good food, warm homes, simple pleasures
- **Cleverness:** Brains over brawn
- **Tradition:** "This is how we've always done it"

### The Burrows
- **Location:** Subdistricts within larger areas
- **Architecture:** Smaller doors, cozy spaces
- **Governance:** Elder councils, consensus decisions
- **Defense:** Hiding rather than fighting

### Traditions
- **Seven Meals:** Daily ritual of community eating
- **Harvest Home:** Seasonal celebration
- **Gift Giving:** Constant small exchanges
- **Story Circles:** Oral tradition keeping

## Faction Affinity

### Natural Advantages
- **Independent Influence +1** - Self-sufficient communities
- **Community organizing** - Natural network builders
- **Information gathering** - Overlooked means overhearing

### Starting Position
- **Neutral reputation** - All factions see as harmless
- **Flexible allegiance** - Can support any faction
- **Community pressure** - Must consider halfling impact

## Halfling Survival Strategies

### Historical Approach
- Stay neutral in conflicts
- Provide services to all sides
- Maintain essential businesses
- Keep heads down during violence

### Current Crisis Challenge
- Neutrality becoming impossible
- Communities threatened directly
- Young halflings want action
- Old ways failing
