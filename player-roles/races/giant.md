# Giant

> *"We giants stand above your petty conflicts—literally. But when we choose a side, mountains move and walls fall."*

## Race Overview

Giants in Estellon are rare and remarkable, their very presence changing the dynamics of any situation. Standing twice the height of humans, giants cannot hide or blend in—they are always noticed, always remembered, always consequential. The city's crisis forces these normally solitary beings to decide whether their great strength will preserve order, fuel revolution, or forge something entirely new.

## Racial Traits

### Passive Abilities
These abilities are always active and define your giant heritage:

1. **Giant's Reach** - Can interact with objects and attack targets 10 feet away
2. **Siege Breaker** - Deal double damage to structures, objects, and siege equipment
3. **Thick Skin** - Natural armor provides +1 to defense (stacks with worn armor)
4. **Massive Presence** - +2 to Intimidation checks, -2 to Stealth (impossible to hide)
5. **Towering Strength** - +3 to all Strength-based checks
6. **Massive Appetite** - Requires 4x normal food (trade-off for power)

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+3 to Strength-based checks** (Unmatched physical power)
- **+2 to Intimidation** (Size alone terrifies)
- **Giant's Reach** - Can interact with objects 10 feet away
- **Siege Breaker** - Double damage to structures and objects
- **Thick Skin** - Natural armor (+1 defense)
- **Massive Appetite** - Requires 4x normal food

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 9'0" to 12'0" typically
- **Build:** Proportionally massive
- **Appearance:** Varied based on giant heritage
- **Lifespan:** 200-300 years

## Playing a Giant

### Advantages
- **Unmatched Strength:** Literally move mountains
- **Intimidation Factor:** Presence changes everything
- **Living Siege Engine:** Walk through walls
- **Unique Perspective:** See over crowds and walls

### Challenges
- **Cannot Hide:** Always visible and remembered
- **Space Issues:** Don't fit in normal buildings
- **Resource Drain:** Expensive to feed and equip
- **Isolation:** Few others understand giant experience

### Roleplay Hooks
- Gentle giant trying to avoid violence?
- Last of your clan seeking purpose?
- Mercenary selling strength to highest bidder?
- Philosopher giant contemplating human folly?

### Storm Giants
