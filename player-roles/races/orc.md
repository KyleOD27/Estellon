# Orc

> *"They fear our strength, but it is our honor they should respect. In Estellon's chaos, we will prove what Orcs truly are."*

## Race Overview

Orcs in Estellon fight against centuries of prejudice while proving their worth through strength, honor, and determination. Often relegated to manual labor or military service, orcs see the current crisis as an opportunity to claim their rightful place in society. Their warrior culture and communal bonds make them natural revolutionaries, though some seek to prove they can uphold order better than those who oppressed them.

## Racial Traits

### Passive Abilities
These abilities are always active and define your orcish heritage:

1. **Orcish Fury** - When below half health, gain +2 to all damage rolls
2. **Hardy** - Advantage on saves against poison and disease
3. **Powerful Build** - +2 to Strength-based checks, count as one size larger for carrying
4. **Intimidating Presence** - +2 to Intimidation checks, -1 to noble Persuasion checks
5. **Relentless Endurance** - +1 to Constitution saves

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+2 to Strength-based checks** (Combat, intimidation, labor)
- **+1 to Constitution** (Endurance, resistance)
- **Orcish Fury** - When below half health, +2 damage
- **Intimidating Presence** - +2 to intimidation, -1 to noble persuasion
- **Hardy** - Resistant to poison and disease

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 6'0" to 7'0" typically
- **Build:** Muscular and powerful
- **Appearance:** Green to gray skin, pronounced tusks
- **Lifespan:** 60-80 years (similar to humans)

## Social Standing in Estellon

### General Perception
- **Nobles:** "Useful servants, dangerous if not controlled"
- **Merchants:** "Strong backs for loading, slow minds for thinking"
- **Common Folk:** "Hard workers but quick to anger"
- **Guards:** "Natural soldiers if properly disciplined"

### District Reception
- **Noble Quarter:** Allowed only as servants or guards
- **Trade District:** Hired for heavy labor
- **Cobbleward:** Respected for shared struggles
- **Temple District:** "Seeking redemption from savage nature"

### Faction Views
- **Elara's Coalition:** "Deserve equal treatment under reformed law"
- **Ashka's Revolution:** "Natural allies against oppression"
- **Brennis's Guard:** "Excellent soldiers if kept in line"

## Playing an Orc

### Advantages
- **Physical Power:** Unmatched strength and endurance
- **Warrior Culture:** Natural combat abilities
- **Community Bonds:** Strong loyalty to allies
- **Revolutionary Spirit:** Nothing left to lose

### Challenges
- **Constant Prejudice:** Assumed violent and stupid
- **Limited Access:** Barred from many spaces
- **Reputation:** Must overcome negative stereotypes
- **Internal Rage:** Struggle between fury and control

### Roleplay Hooks
- Educated orc proving intelligence equals strength?
- Former pit fighter seeking legitimate respect?
- Trying to uplift entire orc community?
- Torn between traditional ways and integration?

## Orcish Culture in Estellon

### Values
- **Honor:** Word is bond, promises are sacred
- **Strength:** Physical, mental, and spiritual
- **Community:** Tribe/clan over individual
- **Courage:** Face any challenge head-on

### The Orcish Quarter
- **Location:** Edge of Cobbleward
- **Community:** Tight-knit, self-policing
- **Economy:** Labor, crafting, fighting
- **Leadership:** Elder council system

### Traditions
- **Trial by Combat:** Settling disputes through strength
- **Blood Oaths:** Unbreakable vows
- **Clan Markings:** Tattoos showing heritage
- **War Chants:** Building courage before battle

## Faction Affinity

### Natural Advantages
- **Revolutionary Zeal +2** - History of oppression
- **Working class solidarity** - Bonds with laborers
- **Military respect** - From those who've fought alongside

### Starting Challenges
- **Coalition Strength -1** - Nobles resist orc equality
- **Noble access blocked** - Systemic exclusion
- **Guard suspicion** - Assumed troublemakers

## Orc Stereotypes vs. Reality

### What Others Believe
- All orcs are violent
- Orcs lack intelligence
- Orcs can't be trusted
- Orcs only understand force

### The Truth
- Violence is last resort for most
- Oral tradition preserves complex knowledge
- Honor makes orcs most trustworthy
- Orcs understand loyalty and justice
