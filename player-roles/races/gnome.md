# Gnome

> *"While others fight over power, we gnomes innovate. Every crisis is an opportunity for a clever solution—or a profitable invention."*

## Race Overview

Gnomes in Estellon are the city's inventors, tinkerers, and unconventional thinkers. Neither as established as halflings nor as physically imposing as other races, gnomes carve their niche through ingenuity and cunning. The current crisis presents them with opportunities to test new ideas, profit from chaos, and perhaps reshape society through innovation rather than revolution.

## Racial Traits

### Passive Abilities
These abilities are always active and define your gnomish heritage:

1. **Gnomish Cunning** - Advantage on all Intelligence-based puzzle and riddle checks
2. **Tinkerer** - Can improvise tools and simple devices from available materials
3. **Artificer's Mind** - +2 to Crafting and Engineering checks
4. **Small but Mighty** - Can use Intelligence in place of Strength for certain tasks
5. **Sharp Eye** - +2 to Detecting Deception and noticing details

### Mechanical Bonuses
- **+2 to Crafting and Engineering** (Innovation and invention)
- **+2 to Detecting Deception** (Natural paranoia and cleverness)
- **Gnomish Cunning** - Advantage on puzzles and problem-solving
- **Small but Mighty** - Can use intelligence in place of strength for certain tasks
- **Tinkerer** - Can improvise tools and devices

### Physical Characteristics
- **Height:** 3'0" to 3'6" typically
- **Build:** Slight and wiry
- **Appearance:** Large eyes, pointed ears, wild hair
- **Lifespan:** 200-250 years

## Social Standing in Estellon

### General Perception
- **Nobles:** "Amusing and occasionally useful oddities"
- **Merchants:** "Brilliant but unreliable business partners"
- **Common Folk:** "Strange but helpful when things break"
- **Guards:** "Too clever for their own good"

### District Reception
- **Noble Quarter:** Hired for novelties and innovations
- **Trade District:** Valued for unique goods and repairs
- **Cobbleward:** Appreciated for practical solutions
- **Temple District:** Viewed as spiritually distracted

### Faction Views
- **Elara's Coalition:** "Innovation could reform society"
- **Kael's Revolution:** "Their cleverness serves whoever pays"
- **Brennis's Guard:** "Useful but need watching"

## Playing a Gnome

### Advantages
- **Problem Solvers:** See solutions others miss
- **Technological Edge:** Create unique advantages
- **Intellectual Respect:** Intelligence recognized
- **Economic Opportunity:** Profit from innovation

### Challenges
- **Physical Weakness:** Size and strength limitations
- **Social Oddity:** Seen as weird or obsessive
- **Trust Issues:** Suspected of ulterior motives
- **Obsessive Nature:** Can lose sight of bigger picture

### Roleplay Hooks
- Inventor whose creation could change the war?
- Information broker selling secrets to all sides?
- Trying to solve crisis through innovation?
- Obsessed with particular aspect of the conflict?

## Gnomish Culture in Estellon

### Values
- **Innovation:** New is always better
- **Cleverness:** Brains beat brawn
- **Profit:** Everything has value
- **Curiosity:** Must understand how things work

### The Workshop Quarter
- **Location:** Between Trade District and Cobbleward
- **Features:** Workshops, laboratories, testing grounds
- **Hazards:** Occasional explosions and experiments
- **Community:** Competitive but collaborative

### Traditions
- **Invention Fairs:** Monthly demonstrations
- **Patent Disputes:** Elaborate legal arguments
- **Apprenticeship:** Master-student relationships
- **Trade Secrets:** Closely guarded techniques

## Faction Affinity

### Natural Advantages
- **Independent Influence +2** - Self-reliant innovators
- **Merchant connections** - Trade in unique goods
- **Information network** - Curiosity yields intelligence

### Starting Position
- **Opportunistic reputation** - Seen as mercenary
- **Valuable to all** - Everyone needs innovations
