# The Refugee

> *"I've lost everything once; I won't lose again. In Estellon's struggle, I see my own story written large."*

## Role Overview

As a Refugee, you arrived in Estellon fleeing war, persecution, or disaster elsewhere. You understand suffering intimately and recognize the signs of systemic oppression. The city's crisis resonates deeply with your own experiences, forcing you to decide whether to keep your head down and survive, or risk everything again for change.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
- **War Survivor:** Fleeing conflicts that destroyed your homeland
- **Political Exile:** Escaped persecution for beliefs or identity
- **Economic Refugee:** Driven out by poverty and starvation
- **Natural Disaster:** Lost everything to plague, fire, or catastrophe

### Starting Resources
- Common clothes and basic survival gear
- Hidden caches in poor districts
- Underground network contacts
- Hard-won survival skills
- Stories that inspire or horrify

## Starting Bonuses (Mechanical)
- +2 to Survival checks
- +2 to Stealth checks
- +2 Revolutionary Zeal (Kael's faction) score
- Set of common clothes and basic survival gear

## Social Access
- **Trusted by common folk and the downtrodden** - Immediate kinship with the suffering
- **Can move freely in poor districts (Cobbleward)** - Know the hidden paths and safe spots
- **Recognized by Kael's rebellion network** - Your story makes you one of them
- **Access to underground networks and safe houses** - The invisible city of the displaced

## Special Ability
**Street Knowledge** - Once per session, can automatically succeed on finding safe shelter, hidden routes, or friendly contacts in poor/dangerous districts. You know where the desperate gather and how to move unseen by authority.

## Faction Perspectives

### Supporting the Revolution (Kael) - Natural Alignment
- **Kindred Spirit:** You've lived what they fight against
- **Nothing to Lose:** Already lost everything once
- **Revolutionary Fervor:** Personal experience fuels passion
- **People's Champion:** Voice for the voiceless

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Peaceful Change:** Seen enough violence and bloodshed
- **Legal Protection:** Seek rights and recognition
- **Sanctuary Hope:** Church offers genuine shelter
- **Gradual Progress:** Revolution might make things worse

### Supporting the Guard (Brennis)
- **Survival Instinct:** Side with strength to stay alive
- **Order from Chaos:** Stability after constant upheaval
- **Pragmatic Choice:** Better devil you know
- **Protection Price:** Trade freedom for security

## Unique Opportunities

### Survival Leverage
- Navigate dangerous areas others can't
- Unite the displaced and desperate
- Invisible movement through city
- Testimony that moves hearts

### Refugee-Specific Quests
- **The Underground Railroad:** Establish escape routes for others
- **Voice of the Voiceless:** Rally the displaced to action
- **Survival Network:** Organize mutual aid systems
- **Witness Testimony:** Your story changes powerful minds

### Special Assets
- **Safe House Knowledge:** Hidden refuges throughout city
- **Survivor Network:** Fellow refugees share information
- **Street Invisibility:** Guards don't notice "just another beggar"
- **Desperation Strength:** Nothing left to lose makes you dangerous

## Roleplaying Guidance

### Core Motivations
- Never be vulnerable again
- Protect fellow sufferers
- Find home and belonging
- Ensure others don't suffer your fate

### Key Questions
- Is it better to hide or fight?
- Can you trust again after betrayal?
- What's worth risking the little you've rebuilt?
- How do you balance survival with conscience?

### Refugee Phrases for Dialogue Bingo
- *"I've seen what happens when order collapses."*
- *"The powerful never see us until we rise."*
- *"Survival teaches lessons books cannot."*
- *"We who have nothing, have nothing to lose."*
- *"Home is wherever we stand together."*

## Playing a Refugee

In Estellon's crisis, you embody the human cost of political failure. Your lived experience of loss and survival gives you unique credibility and perspective. You see through pretty words to harsh realities, understanding that for the powerless, political philosophy matters less than bread and safety.

Whether you're a traumatized survivor seeking only safety, a hardened fighter who refuses to run again, or an organizer building solidarity among the displaced, your refugee's journey through Estellon explores how the dispossessed reclaim agency in times of upheaval.

### The Weight of Trauma

Your past shapes every interaction:
- **Triggers:** Certain sounds, smells, or sights bring flashbacks
- **Trust Issues:** Betrayal made you cautious
- **Hypervigilance:** Always noting exits and threats
- **Survivor's Guilt:** Why did you survive when others didn't?

### Refugee Communities

You're not alone in displacement:
- **Fellow Exiles:** Others from your homeland
- **Economic Refugees:** Driven out by poverty
- **Political Dissidents:** Fled persecution
- **War Orphans:** Children who've lost everything
- **Mixed Families:** Bonds formed in shared suffering

### From Victim to Agent

Remember that being a refugee doesn't mean being helpless:
- Your survival skills are exceptional
- Your networks are invisible to power
- Your testimony carries moral weight
- Your resilience inspires others
- Your perspective sees through propaganda

In Estellon, you represent not just personal survival but the possibility that the forgotten and displaced can shape the future rather than merely endure it.
