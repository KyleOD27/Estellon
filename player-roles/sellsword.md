# The Sellsword

> *"In Estellon's streets, loyalty is negotiable, steel is honest, and every warrior must choose what's worth fighting for."*

## Role Overview

As a Sellsword, you are a professional warrior who lives by skill and reputation, arriving in Estellon with the caravan seeking employment during the festival. The city's brewing conflict presents opportunities for profit, but also forces you to confront questions about honor, loyalty, and what causes deserve your blade.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
- **Festival Security:** Hired to protect merchant caravans
- **Reputation Building:** Seeking fame in a major city
- **Between Wars:** Following the coin after other conflicts ended
- **Running From Past:** Escaping complications in other lands

### Starting Resources
- Battle-tested equipment
- Reputation from past campaigns
- Contacts in mercenary companies
- Knowledge of military tactics
- Scars and stories from previous wars

## Starting Bonuses (Mechanical)
- +1 weapon or armor of choice (martial, non-magical)
- +2 to Intimidation checks
- +1 City Control (Brennis's faction) OR +1 Revolutionary Zeal (Kael's faction) - player's choice
- Proficiency with one additional weapon type

## Social Access
- **Can enter military barracks and training grounds** - Military bearing grants automatic entry
- **Recognized by City Guard and mercenary companies** - Fellow warriors know your reputation
- **Can take contracts from all factions without prejudice** - Mercenary neutrality is respected
- **Access to weapon smiths and armories** - Professional warriors get professional equipment

## Special Ability
**Mercenary's Reputation** - Once per session, can invoke past military service to gain advantage on a social check with soldiers, guards, or veterans. Your scars and stories carry weight among those who live by the blade.

## Faction Perspectives

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Honorable Cause:** Fight for justice and protection of innocents
- **Legitimate Employment:** Official sanction and regular pay
- **Noble Patronage:** Long-term contracts with reformed government
- **Righteous Purpose:** Your sword serves higher ideals

### Supporting the Revolution (Kael)
- **People's Champion:** Defend the oppressed against tyranny
- **Freedom Fighter:** Break chains rather than forge them
- **Revolutionary Glory:** Songs will remember your stand
- **New Beginning:** Chance to be more than just hired muscle

### Supporting the Guard (Brennis)
- **Professional Soldiering:** Work with disciplined forces
- **Steady Pay:** Regular government wages
- **Military Structure:** Clear command and objectives
- **Maintaining Order:** Stability ensures continued employment

## Unique Opportunities

### Combat Leverage
- Train faction forces
- Lead crucial operations
- Challenge enemy champions
- Protect or eliminate key targets

### Sellsword-Specific Quests
- **The Duel:** Settle faction disputes through single combat
- **Training Day:** Prepare civilians for conflict

### Special Assets
- **Mercenary Contacts:** Can recruit additional fighters

- **Intimidation Factor:** Reputation affects negotiations
- **Veteran Instincts:** Sense ambushes and evaluate threats

## Playing a Sellsword

In Estellon's political crisis, you represent the practical reality of force. Every faction needs warriors, but you must decide whether you're merely a weapon to be pointed or a person with agency choosing your battles. Your combat skills make you valuable, but your choices about when and why to fight define your character.

Whether you're a cynical mercenary selling to the highest bidder, an honor-bound warrior seeking a worthy cause, or a weary veteran looking for one last meaningful fight, your sellsword's journey through Estellon explores what it means to live by the sword in times of moral complexity.

### The Warrior's Choice

Remember that in Estellon, every contract is also a political statement. The faction you fight for shapes the city's future, and your blade may tip the balance. You're not just choosing an employer - you're choosing what kind of warrior you want to be and what legacy you'll leave in blood and steel.

### Combat Philosophy

Consider how your character approaches violence:
- **Professional:** Efficient, clean, minimal casualties
- **Brutal:** Fear as a weapon, maximum impact
- **Honorable:** Fair fights, protecting non-combatants
- **Pragmatic:** Whatever wins, whatever survives

Your fighting style reflects your character's values and affects how factions view you.
