# The Scholar

> *"In Estellon's libraries lie forgotten laws, ancient precedents, and the power to reshape reality through knowledge."*

## Role Overview

As a Scholar, you are a seeker of truth and wielder of knowledge, drawn to Estellon by its ancient archives and the philosophical questions its crisis poses. You understand that information is power, and in a city torn between Law, Chaos, and Order, your expertise in history, law, and philosophy makes you invaluable to all sides.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
- **Academic Pilgrimage:** Studying Estellon's famous legal archives
- **Philosophical Tourism:** Drawn by the city's ideological conflict
- **Historical Research:** Investigating ancient texts and precedents
- **Institutional Representative:** Sent by universities or temples to observe

### Starting Resources
- Access to library archives
- Letters of introduction to learned societies
- Rare books and scrolls
- Network of academic contacts
- Knowledge of obscure laws and precedents

## Starting Bonuses (Mechanical)
- +2 to Investigation checks
- +2 to one knowledge skill (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion)
- +2 Coalition Strength (Elara's faction) score
- Scholar's pack with research materials

## Social Access
- **Access to libraries, archives, and universities** - Academic credentials open scholarly doors
- **Can request audiences with learned nobles and clergy** - Intellectual reputation carries weight
- **Recognized by Ser Elara's reformist circle** - Education aligns with reform ideals
- **Can consult with scribes and record-keepers** - Fellow scholars share knowledge

## Special Ability
**Research Network** - Once per session, can spend downtime researching a topic and gain detailed information the DM deems relevant (legal precedents, historical context, magical theory, etc.). Your academic connections and methodical approach unlock hidden knowledge.

## Faction Perspectives

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Legal Precedent:** Ancient laws support reform
- **Intellectual Framework:** Philosophical justification for change
- **Educational Reform:** Knowledge should serve all people
- **Institutional Wisdom:** Blend tradition with progress

### Supporting the Revolution (Kael)
- **People's Education:** Democratize knowledge and learning
- **Revolutionary Theory:** Intellectual foundation for new order
- **Historical Cycles:** Past revolutions inform present action
- **Knowledge Liberation:** Break academic gatekeeping

### Supporting the Guard (Brennis)
- **Order Through Law:** Scholarly interpretation maintains stability
- **Historical Continuity:** Preserve institutional knowledge
- **Precedent Power:** Use tradition to justify authority
- **Academic Privilege:** Current system protects scholars

## Unique Opportunities

### Intellectual Leverage
- Discover legal loopholes and precedents
- Interpret laws to favor chosen faction
- Uncover historical secrets and scandals
- Provide philosophical legitimacy

### Scholar-Specific Quests
- **The Lost Precedent:** Find ancient laws that change everything
- **Historical Revelation:** Uncover truths about Estellon's founding
- **Philosophical Debate:** Convert opponents through reason
- **Archive War:** Control or destroy key documents

### Special Assets
- **Archive Access:** Hidden knowledge in libraries
- **Academic Network:** Information from fellow scholars
- **Legal Expertise:** Understanding of complex laws
- **Historical Insight:** Context for current events

## Roleplaying Guidance

### Core Motivations
- Truth and knowledge above all
- Preserving or spreading learning
- Philosophical consistency
- Historical perspective on events

### Key Questions
- Is truth more important than stability?
- Should knowledge serve power or people?
- Can philosophy change reality?
- What does history teach about revolution?

### Scholar Phrases for Dialogue Bingo
- *"History will judge us by our choices today."*
- *"The law is clear on this matter, if properly interpreted."*
- *"Knowledge without action is merely philosophy."*
- *"The precedent for this was set centuries ago."*
- *"Truth is the first casualty of revolution."*

## Playing a Scholar

In Estellon's crisis, you hold the power to legitimize or delegitimize any faction through your interpretation of law, precedent, and philosophy. Your research can uncover game-changing secrets, your arguments can sway nobles and commoners alike, and your knowledge of history provides crucial context for current events.

Whether you're an idealistic academic seeking truth, a pragmatic researcher trading knowledge for influence, or a revolutionary intellectual reshaping society through ideas, your scholar's journey proves that in times of upheaval, the pen truly is mightier than the sword.

### Scholar's Burden

Remember that with knowledge comes responsibility. Every fact you reveal or conceal, every law you interpret, every historical parallel you draw shapes how others understand the conflict. In Estellon, you don't just study history - you write it.
