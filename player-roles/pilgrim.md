# The Pilgrim

> *"In Estellon's crisis of faith, the divine speaks through action, and every prayer demands a choice between order and justice."*

## Role Overview

As a Pilgrim, you are a spiritual seeker who arrived in Estellon for the sacred Sunstone Festival, only to find a city where faith itself has become political. You navigate the conflict through the lens of divine purpose, moral duty, and spiritual truth, seeking to understand what the gods truly want for this troubled city.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
- **Sacred Journey:** Following holy visions or completing religious vows
- **Festival Devotion:** Drawn by the Sunstone Festival's spiritual significance
- **Divine Mission:** Sent by your faith to witness or influence events
- **Spiritual Crisis:** Seeking answers to personal questions of faith

### Starting Resources
- Holy symbols and religious texts
- Blessing and healing capabilities
- Church connections across districts
- Divine insight or visions
- Reputation for piety and wisdom

## Starting Bonuses (Mechanical)
- +1 to Wisdom saving throws
- +2 to Religion checks
- +2 Coalition Strength (Elara's faction) score
- Holy symbol and traveler's pack

## Social Access
- **Welcome in all temples and shrines** - Sacred hospitality is yours by right
- **Can seek sanctuary with Ser Elara and Church hierarchy** - Faith provides political protection
- **Respected by common folk in religious districts** - Piety earns trust and deference
- **Can request blessings and divine counsel** - Clergy share spiritual wisdom freely

## Special Ability
**Divine Guidance** - Once per session, can seek divine insight on a moral dilemma (DM provides hints about consequences of choices). Through prayer and meditation, the divine offers clarity in chaos.

## Faction Perspectives

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Righteous Path:** Divine justice requires systemic change
- **Moral Authority:** Faith legitimizes reform efforts
- **Protecting the Weak:** Religious duty to defend the oppressed
- **Sacred Law:** Higher authority than mortal rulers

### Supporting the Revolution (Kael)
- **Liberation Theology:** The gods favor the oppressed rising up
- **Cleansing Fire:** Sometimes destruction precedes renewal
- **People's Faith:** Religion belongs to all, not just elites
- **Divine Rebellion:** Even gods overthrew titans

### Supporting the Guard (Brennis)
- **Divine Order:** The gods established hierarchies for reasons
- **Stability's Blessing:** Chaos invites evil and suffering
- **Traditional Faith:** Preserve established religious structures
- **Testing Times:** Suffering builds spiritual strength

## Playing a Pilgrim

In Estellon's political upheaval, you represent the moral and spiritual dimension of the conflict. Your faith provides a unique perspective on events, seeing divine purpose where others see only chaos. Every faction seeks your blessing, but you must determine which path truly serves the gods' will.

Whether you're a devoted servant following divine visions, a questioning soul seeking truth through trial, or a pragmatic cleric balancing faith with reality, your pilgrim's journey through Estellon explores how spirituality intersects with politics and power.

### The Divine Paradox

Remember that in Estellon, different faiths and interpretations exist. Your character must navigate not just political factions but also religious divisions:
- **Orthodox:** Traditional interpretations favor stability
- **Reformed:** Progressive theology supports change
- **Mystical:** Personal revelation transcends institutions
- **Syncretic:** Multiple traditions offer different truths

### Miracles and Mundane

Consider how your pilgrim balances divine intervention with practical action:
- Do you expect the gods to act directly, or work through mortal hands?
- Are miracles rare and sacred, or does divinity infuse everyday acts?
- How do you maintain faith when prayers seem unanswered?

Your approach to the divine shapes how you engage with the very real, very mortal conflict tearing Estellon apart.
