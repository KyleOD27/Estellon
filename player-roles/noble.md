# The Noble

> *"In Estellon's halls of power, bloodlines carry weight, but choices determine whether you'll keep your head—and your crown."*

## Role Overview

As a Noble, you are born to privilege and power, visiting Estellon for the festival or political obligations. The city's crisis threatens everything your class represents, forcing you to choose between preserving your status, reforming the system that benefits you, or even supporting those who would see you fall.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
Choose one hook below. Your choice grants special dialogue opportunities and rewards specific behaviors.

#### **Political Obligation**
*Representing your family's interests at the festival*

**Special Dialogue Access:**
- Can invoke family name to request meetings with other nobles
- Other houses recognize your diplomatic status
- Can discuss "family interests" and alliances openly

**Behavior Rewards:**
- **House Representative:** +2 to Persuasion when negotiating on behalf of your family
- **Political Marriage:** Once per session, can arrange an advantageous introduction or social connection
- **Family Resources:** Can request funds from home once per session (100gp, but creates obligation to report back)

---

#### **Exile or Escape**
*Fleeing scandals or enemies in other lands*

**Special Dialogue Access:**
- Can relate to other disgraced or fallen nobles
- Understand court intrigue and backstabbing
- Can ask about "starting fresh" or "new reputations"

**Behavior Rewards:**
- **Fallen Grace:** +2 to Insight when detecting deception or betrayal (learned the hard way)
- **Nothing Left to Lose:** Once per session when taking a risk that could restore your honor, gain +2 to all checks
- **Low Profile:** When City Mood is Martial Law or Powder Keg, can hide among common folk more easily (+2 to Stealth despite noble bearing)

---

#### **Idealistic Youth**
*Seeking to prove yourself beyond inherited titles*

**Special Dialogue Access:**
- Can challenge other nobles' outdated views without severe social penalty
- Common folk more willing to talk to a "good noble"
- Can question traditions and status quo openly

**Behavior Rewards:**
- **Prove Your Worth:** +2 to checks when acting against noble privilege or helping common folk
- **Noble Sacrifice:** Once per session when giving up personal advantage for greater good, inspire allies (+1 to all allies' checks next scene)
- **Bridge Builder:** +1 to all faction reputation gains; your idealism transcends normal class barriers

---

#### **Secret Mission**
*Pursuing hidden agendas for your house*

**Special Dialogue Access:**
- Can conduct "discreet inquiries" without raising suspicion
- Other spies and agents recognize fellow operative
- Can request private audiences for "sensitive matters"

**Behavior Rewards:**
- **Covert Operator:** +2 to Deception and Stealth when working on your secret mission
- **Hidden Agenda:** Once per session, can reveal you secretly arranged something earlier (retroactive planning)
- **House Secrets:** Can leverage your family's hidden knowledge for advantage on History or Investigation check

---

- **Recognized by military officers and aristocracy** - Your name carries ancestral weight
- **Can invoke noble privilege and family connections** - Ancient rights and modern networks

## Role Abilities

Your noble heritage grants you these abilities:

### Active Abilities
1. **Political Influence** (Once per session) - Leverage family name/connections to gain favor, arrange meetings, or influence decisions among the nobility. Your bloodline is currency in the halls of power.
2. **Command Presence** (Once per day) - Issue an order with such authority that commoners and servants automatically comply (unless directly against their interests).
3. **Noble's Privilege** (Once per scene) - Invoke your title to gain access to restricted areas, bypass common procedures, or demand respect.

### Passive Abilities
1. **Born to Lead** - +2 to Persuasion and Intimidation checks with anyone of lower social class
2. **Aristocratic Education** - Proficient in History, Heraldry, and Etiquette; +2 to related checks
3. **Wealthy Resources** - Start each session with extra coin; can easily secure loans or credit based on family name

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Enlightened Nobility:** Use privilege to lift others

- **Class Traitor:** Reject the system that created you
- **Guilty Conscience:** Atone for ancestral sins

## Playing a Noble

In Estellon's crisis, you embody the establishment under threat. Your birthright grants access and influence others lack, but also makes you a target for revolutionary fury. Every choice you make reflects on your entire class—will you be the noble who preserved tradition, reformed it, or had the courage to tear it down?

Whether you're an enlightened aristocrat seeking reform, a desperate noble clinging to power, or a rebel against your own class, your noble's journey through Estellon explores what leadership means when the crown itself is questioned.

### The Burden of Blood

Your nobility shapes every interaction:
- **Commoners:** Fear, respect, or resentment
- **Merchants:** Deference mixed with calculation
- **Guards:** Automatic authority (usually)
- **Revolutionaries:** Symbol of everything wrong
- **Other Nobles:** Complex games of precedence

### Noble Varieties

Consider what kind of noble you are:
- **Old Blood:** Ancient lineage, traditional values
- **New Money:** Recently ennobled, proving worth
- **Foreign Noble:** Different customs, outside perspective
- **Fallen House:** Lost wealth but kept the title
- **Idealist:** Believes nobility means service

Your noble background determines how you view the crisis and how others view you.

### The Price of Privilege

Remember that in revolution, nobles often pay the highest price. Your character must navigate:
- Loyalty to family vs. personal conscience
- Maintaining status vs. doing what's right
- Using privilege for self vs. others
- When to stand on ceremony vs. when to run
