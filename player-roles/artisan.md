# The Artisan

> *"In Estellon's workshops, every hammer blow strikes for either tradition or transformation, and every creation carries the maker's politics."*

## Role Overview

As an Artisan, you are a skilled craftsperson who creates with your hands and mind, arriving in Estellon to sell your wares at the festival or seek new opportunities. The city's conflict directly threatens your livelihood and guild, forcing you to decide whether your skills will preserve the old ways, forge a new future, or simply survive the chaos.

## Background Hooks

### Why You're Here
- **Festival Commerce:** Displaying masterworks at the Sunstone Festival
- **Guild Business:** Representing your craft guild's interests
- **Apprenticeship Complete:** Seeking to establish your own workshop
- **Innovation Opportunity:** Testing new techniques in a major market

### Starting Resources
- Masterwork tools of your trade
- Guild membership and connections
- Workshop or stall space
- Trade secrets and techniques
- Reputation for quality work

## Starting Bonuses (Mechanical)
- +50 gold pieces
- Proficiency with artisan's tools (player's choice)
- +2 to related skill checks (based on craft)
- +1 Revolutionary Zeal (Kael's faction) AND +1 Independent Influence
- Set of artisan's tools

## Social Access
- **Recognized by guilds and craftspeople** - Fellow artisans respect your skill
- **Can access workshops and guild halls** - Trade membership opens craft spaces
- **Trusted by working-class citizens** - Honest work earns honest respect
- **Can trade services for information or favors** - Everyone needs something made or fixed

## Special Ability
**Master Craftsman** - Once per session, can create, repair, or modify an item using your craft during downtime (DM determines mechanics and time required). Your exceptional skill turns raw materials into solutions.

## Faction Perspectives

### Supporting the Reformers (Elara)
- **Guild Evolution:** Reform antiquated guild restrictions
- **Merit Recognition:** Skill should matter more than birthright
- **Protected Trade:** Fair laws ensure honest craftspeople thrive
- **Innovation Freedom:** New systems allow new techniques

### Supporting the Revolution (Kael)
- **Workers' Pride:** Craftspeople should own their workshops
- **Break Monopolies:** Destroy guild restrictions on trade
- **People's Craft:** Art and craft for all, not just elites
- **Revolutionary Tools:** Your skills arm the uprising

### Supporting the Guard (Brennis)
- **Guild Protection:** Current system preserves craft traditions
- **Stable Markets:** Order ensures consistent work
- **Traditional Ways:** Ancient techniques deserve preservation
- **Known Systems:** Better the devil you know

## Playing an Artisan

In Estellon's upheaval, you represent the productive heart of society. Your skills keep the city running, whether forging weapons, building barricades, or creating beauty amid chaos. Every faction needs what you can make, but your choice of what to create—and for whom—shapes the conflict's outcome.

Whether you're a traditional master preserving ancient ways, an innovative creator pushing boundaries, or a pragmatic craftsperson just trying to keep working, your artisan's journey through Estellon explores how creation and creativity survive political upheaval.

### Craft Specializations

Consider your specific trade:
- **Blacksmith:** Weapons, tools, horseshoes—power through steel
- **Carpenter:** Buildings, barricades, scaffolds—shape the battlefield
- **Weaver:** Banners, uniforms, disguises—clothe the conflict
- **Alchemist:** Potions, explosives, medicines—chemistry of revolution
- **Jeweler:** Symbols, bribes, beauty—luxury in chaos
- **Mason:** Fortifications, monuments, prisons—permanent consequences

### Guild Dynamics

Your relationship with your guild shapes your options:
- **Master:** Leadership role, guild responsibilities
- **Journeyman:** Proven skill, seeking advancement
- **Apprentice:** Learning still, fewer obligations
- **Independent:** Outside guild structure, more freedom
- **Exile:** Cast out, bearing grudges

### The Maker's Choice

Remember that in Estellon, everything you create has consequences:
- A sword might kill a tyrant or a hero
- A lock might imprison the innocent or protect the vulnerable
- A banner might inspire revolution or rally oppression
- Your masterpiece might define the new order

Your artisan doesn't just create objects—you create possibilities, and must live with how others use them.
