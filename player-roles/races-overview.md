# Character Races of Estellon

> *"In Estellon's melting pot, your blood determines how others see you, but your choices determine who you become."*

## Overview

The city of Estellon draws people of all races, each bringing their own perspectives to the political crisis. Your racial heritage shapes not just your abilities, but how different factions and social classes react to you. In a city torn between tradition and revolution, your race can open doors or close them.

## Available Races

### Common Races
- **[Human](races/human.md)** - Versatile and ambitious, the backbone of every faction
- **[Half-Elf](races/half-elf.md)** - Bridge between worlds, trusted by neither, needed by both
- **[Halfling](races/halfling.md)** - Overlooked but observant, thriving in chaos through community

### Uncommon Races
- **[Elf](races/elf.md)** - Ancient wisdom meeting modern crisis, aloof but insightful
- **[Orc](races/orc.md)** - Strength and honor challenging prejudice and fear
- **[Gnome](races/gnome.md)** - Innovation and cunning in small packages

### Rare Races
- **[Giant](races/giant.md)** - Towering presence forcing immediate reactions

## Racial Mechanics (Simplified System)

### Starting Bonuses
Each race provides:
- **Attribute Bonuses** - Mechanical advantages
- **Racial Abilities** - Unique traits or powers
- **Social Standing** - How different groups react
- **Faction Affinities** - Natural alignments with factions

### Social Dynamics in Estellon

#### The Noble Quarter
- **Welcomed:** Elves, Humans, Half-Elves
- **Tolerated:** Halflings, Gnomes
- **Suspicious:** Orcs, Giants

#### The Cobbleward (Poor District)
- **Welcomed:** Halflings, Orcs, Humans
- **Neutral:** Half-Elves, Gnomes, Giants
- **Resented:** Elves (seen as privileged)

#### The Trade District
- **Welcomed:** All races (coin has no prejudice)
- **Advantages:** Gnomes (craftwork), Halflings (networking)

#### The Temple District
- **Welcomed:** Humans, Halflings, Elves
- **Seeking Redemption:** Orcs, Half-Elves
- **Curiosities:** Giants, Gnomes

## Racial Tensions During Crisis

### Current Climate
The political upheaval has inflamed racial tensions:
- **Elves** associated with the old noble order
- **Orcs** seen as natural revolutionaries
- **Humans** divided among all factions
- **Half-Elves** mistrusted as fence-sitters
- **Halflings** trying to protect their communities
- **Gnomes** profiting from the chaos
- **Giants** feared as potential weapons

### Faction Recruitment

#### Elara's Coalition (Reform)
- **Actively Recruiting:** Elves, Humans, Half-Elves
- **Welcoming:** All races (in principle)
- **Challenge:** Overcoming noble prejudices

#### Ashka's Revolution
- **Actively Recruiting:** Orcs, Halflings, Humans
- **Suspicious Of:** Elves (noble connection)
- **Opportunity:** Breaking racial barriers

#### Brennis's Guard (Order)
- **Preferred:** Humans, Elves
- **Utilitarian View:** Orcs and Giants (useful muscle)
- **Overlooked:** Halflings, Gnomes

## Playing Your Race

### Mechanical Integration
- Races work with any Role (Merchant, Scholar, etc.)
- Combine racial and role bonuses
- Stack social access where they overlap

### Narrative Opportunities
- Use racial prejudice as roleplay hooks
- Challenge or confirm stereotypes
- Build unexpected alliances
- Navigate social barriers

### Character Questions
- How does your race view the crisis?
- Do you conform to or defy racial expectations?
- Which faction appreciates your heritage?
- How do you handle prejudice?

## Racial Ability Examples

### Combat Abilities
- **Orc Fury** - Extra damage when wounded
- **Elven Grace** - Bonus to dodging
- **Giant's Reach** - Strike at distance

### Social Abilities
- **Human Adaptability** - Bonus to any skill check 1/session
- **Halfling Luck** - Reroll a failed check 1/session
- **Gnome Cunning** - Advantage on detecting lies

### Utility Abilities
- **Half-Elf Versatility** - Extra ability slot
- **Giant Strength** - Carry/move heavy objects
- **Elven Meditation** - Don't need full rest

## Creating Mixed Heritage

Players can propose mixed heritage with DM approval:
- Choose primary race for main bonuses
- Add minor trait from secondary heritage
- Create unique social standing
- Define your place in Estellon's society
