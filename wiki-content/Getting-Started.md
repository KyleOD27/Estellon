## 🎭 Understanding Your NPCs

### The Faction Leaders
Each represents a different approach to justice and governance:

1. **[[Ser Elara Vance]] (The Reformers)**
   - **Philosophy:** "The system can be redeemed from within"
   - **Methods:** Legal reform, institutional change, building coalitions
   - **Voice:** Passionate but professional idealist

2. **[[Kael]] (The Revolution)**  
   - **Philosophy:** "The system must be shattered, not reformed"
   - **Methods:** Direct action, sabotage, protecting the vulnerable
   - **Voice:** Street-smart revolutionary with attitude

3. **[[Captain Brennis]] (The Status Quo)**
   - **Philosophy:** "Order must be maintained at all costs"  
   - **Methods:** Enforcement, suppression, preserving existing structures
   - **Voice:** Cold, authoritative military command

### The Antagonists
- **[[Lord Pellor]]** - The true villain hiding behind helpful bureaucracy
- **[[Duke Valerius II]]** - Pathetic ruler whose weakness enabled the crisis
- **[[Gorok the Hillbreaker]]** - External threat and honest monster

## 🎵 Using Character Voices

### Setup Requirements
- **Audio Files:** Located in `audio/characters/` (MP3 format)
- **Playback:** Any device capable of playing MP3s
- **Test Page:** Use `docs/AUDIO_TEST.html` to verify all voices work

### Best Practices
1. **Character Introduction** - Play voice sample when NPCs first appear
2. **Key Moments** - Use voices for important dialogue and dramatic scenes  
3. **Don't Overuse** - Save for impactful moments, not every line
4. **Volume Balance** - Adjust audio to match your speaking voice
5. **Practice First** - Listen to samples before the session

### Voice Acting Tips
- **Match the Energy** - Use the AI voice's tone as a guide for your own portrayal
- **Character Consistency** - Each NPC should have a distinctive speaking pattern
- **Emotional Range** - The samples show each character's baseline; adjust for different situations

## 📊 Tracking Progress

### The Three Metrics
Track these throughout the campaign to measure player impact:

1. **Revolutionary Zeal (0-10)** - How radical the city becomes
2. **City Control (0-10)** - Which faction has the most influence  
3. **Coalition Strength (0-10)** - How well factions cooperate

### The Faction Turn
Between sessions, each faction takes actions based on:
- Player choices and allegiances
- Current progress track values  
- Individual faction goals and methods
- Random events and external pressures

## 🎲 Session Structure

### Typical Session Flow
1. **Faction Turn Results** - Narrate what happened since last session
2. **Player Actions** - Players pursue their goals and react to events
3. **NPC Interactions** - Faction leaders respond to player choices
4. **Consequence Integration** - Show how player actions affect the city
5. **Progress Tracking** - Update metrics based on session events

### Pacing Guidelines
- **Act I:** 2-3 sessions (establishment and choice)
- **Act II:** 4-6 sessions (faction war and building tensions)  
- **Act III:** 3-4 sessions (crisis and resolution)
- **Total:** 10-15 sessions depending on group pace

## 🎪 Running Political Intrigue

### Creating Meaningful Choices
- **No Perfect Options** - Every choice has costs and benefits
- **Show Consequences** - Player decisions should visibly affect the world
- **Personal Stakes** - Connect political events to character backgrounds
- **Information Warfare** - Control what players know and when they learn it

### Managing Factions
- **Consistent Motivations** - Each faction acts according to their philosophy
- **Reactive World** - Factions respond to player actions and each other
- **Internal Conflicts** - Show cracks and disagreements within factions
- **Changing Dynamics** - Let player choices shift faction relationships

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

### Things to Avoid
- **Railroading** - Don't force players toward predetermined outcomes
- **Faction Favoritism** - Present each faction's perspective fairly
- **Oversimplification** - Maintain moral complexity throughout
- **Audio Overuse** - Character voices should enhance, not dominate
- **Static World** - Keep factions active and responsive

### Troubleshooting
- **Players Avoid Choosing** - Create urgency through external threats
- **One Faction Dominates** - Introduce complications and rival actions
- **Moral Paralysis** - Remind players that inaction is also a choice
- **Audio Problems** - Have backup descriptions ready if technology fails
